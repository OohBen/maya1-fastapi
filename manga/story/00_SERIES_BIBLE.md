## Logline

A boy the village beats every year on his birthday is found in an alley by the man who founded that
village — his grandfather — who feeds him, teaches him, rebuilds his body, and then makes him commit
patricide, because the eyes only awaken through loss.

## The engine

Every escalation in this story is **bought with something**. That is the spine, and it's what makes
it worth adapting:

| Power gained | Price paid |
|---|---|
| Food, books, a family | Secrecy; a double life at seven |
| A rebuilt body (Uchiha + Senju cells) | His childhood — "your childhood is over" |
| **Sharingan** | Twenty-five men dead at nine; he was abandoned in the camp *on purpose* |
| **Mangekyō Sharingan** | His grandfather's life, by his own hand, arranged in advance |
| Rinnegan (later volumes) | Yet to be paid |

The line to hang the whole series on, from ch7: **"Is this the price of power?"**

## Themes

- **Hatred is inherited.** Konoha's parents teach it to their children deliberately. The opening
  narration is explicit: it passes "like the flu."
- **Masks.** Naruto's grin is a mask. Konoha's peace is a mask. Madara's kindness is — partly — a
  mask. Almost every character is performing.
- **Chosen family versus owed family.** Jiraiya (godfather) refuses him. Hiruzen loves him but lies
  to him. Madara, who wants to use him, is the only one who tells him the truth. That's the tragedy:
  the manipulator is also the only honest adult in his life.
- **Being made into a weapon by someone who genuinely loves you.** Madara's affection is real *and*
  instrumental. Do not flatten him into a villain — the fic doesn't.

Naruto is a **liar by necessity** from age seven onward. He performs idiocy in public on Madara's
instruction. Draw the difference: the public grin never reaches the eyes.

### Uchiha Madara
Old man in black robes. Long spiked black hair. Walking cane — he needs it. Three-tomoe Sharingan,
red. Hashirama's face embedded in his chest from the cell infusion. Dying throughout the whole
volume, and knows it. Warm to Naruto in a way that is *not* faked, while grooming him for a purpose.

### Zetsu
Plant-like. Emerges from ground and walls. Yellow eyes. **Right side white, left side black.** Black
cloak. Two personalities that can separate: black Zetsu (deep voice, serious, Madara's will, can use
Mokuton) and white Zetsu (lighter voice, playful, weak, hates being reminded of it).

### Hiruzen Sarutobi
The Third. Loves Naruto genuinely. Lies to him constantly, always "people hate what they don't
understand." Watches him through a crystal ball. His failure is the engine of Naruto's turn — every
lie pushes the boy further toward the one adult who tells the truth.

### Supporting (Volume 1)
Kakashi (silver hair, mask, orange book), Iruka (scar across the nose; starts hostile, softens),
Ayame + Teuchi (Ichiraku — the only unconditional warmth in the book), Shikamaru, Chōji, Hinata
(stares, never speaks), Sasuke, Sakura, Ino. Kushina and Minato appear only in the reveal imagery.

## World rules — where this diverges from canon

These are the load-bearing divergences. Get them wrong and the adaptation stops making sense.

1. **Madara is Naruto's grandfather.** Kushina was his daughter. Naruto is an Uchiha by blood.
2. **Kushina's Uchiha blood was sealed** by her mother, an Uzumaki seal master who left Madara.
   That's why nobody knew.
3. **Madara survived** his fight with Hashirama and has been alive, immobile then partially restored
   through self-experimentation, in a hideout outside Konoha.
4. **Naruto is rebuilt**, not merely trained. Madara infuses his own cells *and* Hashirama's. Naruto
   ends up with native Uchiha *and* Senju blood — his own Mokuton, not a graft. This is what makes
   the Rinnegan possible for him later.
5. **A blood clone attends the academy for years** while the real Naruto trains at the hideout. It
   dispels at graduation and dumps years of memories into him at once.
6. **Obito stole Madara's identity.** He operates as "Madara" to recruit and "Tobi" otherwise.
   Nagato holds the transplanted Rinnegan and fronts Akatsuki. Madara's dying instruction: kill
   Obito, take the eyes, rule Konoha.
7. **Naruto hates Minato**, not with ambivalence — actively. He stopped sitting on the Yondaime's
   head on the monument.
8. **There is no Wave arc.** Zabuza is a passing mention. Bell test → six-month skip → Chūnin Exams.

## Tone and visual language

Dark, quiet, interior. This is not a shonen adventure until much later — Volume 1 is closer to a
horror-tinged childhood drama. Konoha should look *warm and pleasant* in wide shots and rotten in
close-up; the contrast is the point.

**House style:** premium 2D shonen manga. Clean black linework with varied weight. Flat digital cel
colouring, two to three tonal values per material, hard-edged shadows. Hair as clusters and wedges,
never individual strands. No depth-of-field, no photorealistic skin, no 3D/CGI look, no painterly
rendering, no lens flare.

**Lighting logic per location** (restate on every page — drift is the enemy):
- Festival street / alley, night: warm orange lantern light from above, deep blue-black shadow.
- Naruto's apartment: dim, single bulb, cold.
- Madara's hideout: near-black, hard rim light, no warm tones anywhere.
- Academy: flat daylight, unromantic.
- Hokage Monument: cold blue moonlight or flat overcast.

## Adaptation principles

1. **Interiority must become image.** The source is enormous blocks of Naruto's inner monologue and
   Madara's exposition. Those are the hardest pages. Convert to: reaction shots, symbolic inserts,
   mythic flashback plates, and the tablets themselves. If a page is two people sitting at a kitchen
   table talking, it needs invented visual structure or it dies.
2. **The model infers nothing.** Every panel gets explicit composition, camera angle and content.
3. **Restate the constants every page.** Character design, palette, line weight, cel rules, lighting
   logic for that location.
4. **Say what to ignore in every reference.** "Use Image 1 for his face and outfit only; ignore its
   white background and three-view layout."
5. **Never let the model letter.** Art with clean space, text composited deterministically after.
6. **Name the protagonist as unique.** "He is the ONLY character on this page who looks like this" —
   without it, his blond hair and red swirl leak onto background extras.
7. **Content staging.** The volume contains a child beaten by a mob and stabbed through the hand, a
   child killing twenty-five men, and a child killing his grandfather. Stage for implication —
   silhouette, reaction shot, the moment after, blood on the floor rather than the wound. This is
   both a moderation necessity and how good manga shoots these beats anyway.
