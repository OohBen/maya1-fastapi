# Volume 6 source-truth ledger

This ledger records immutable source facts for fic chapters 17–21. It is not dialogue and must not be
used as page narration. Source line references use the ignored local `chNN.txt` files produced by
`manga/story/source/fetch_source.py`. Quoted lines are recorded only where the exact wording is
load-bearing for the adaptation; they are working continuity references, not a script.

## Verified seam with Volume 5

Volume 5 ends on fic ch16:617–683 — Yugao at lunch with Anko and Kurenai, admitting she is going out
with Naruto tonight and privately deciding to ask him about the red-haired girl. Fic ch17 opens the
same day. Volume 6 must begin there. Nothing is skipped and nothing from ch16 is re-adapted.

## Chapter 17 — the line no one may cross

### Immutable sequence

1. Naruto watches Konoha from the Hokage Monument. His body is failing: chakra-heavy work harms him,
   he must avoid battle to preserve his health, and he expects to "awaken" and be whole again
   (ch17:7–25).
2. Kakashi joins him and finally asks why every attempt at friendship was refused. Naruto answers
   directly: Minato's face means nothing, the villagers are nuisances, Jiraiya means nothing — but
   Kakashi is not one of the people he could watch die without caring (ch17:27–99). He then forbids
   Kakashi from visiting again without important business: *"I have no need for trivial things as
   friendship."*
3. Zetsu reveals he was present the whole time. Naruto cannot sense him; he only feels the moment
   Zetsu materialises (ch17:107–125).
4. **Naruto cannot activate his Sharingan without medicine.** He asks Zetsu to search Orochimaru's
   scrolls for a stronger drug and has not yet completed a blood analysis or full-body scan
   (ch17:127–147). His pride is injured by the dependence.
5. Preparations for **Edo Tensei** are underway; clones will have gathered everything by the next day
   (ch17:139–143).
6. **The date.** Yugao collects Naruto — she made the reservation and she walks to his door, which she
   accepts as normal for him. He wears his usual black, without gloves and without his forehead
   protector. Sasuke's joke about the date gets no reaction at all (ch17:149–244).
7. Naruto refuses to compliment her and explains why: *"Know who you are and what you are. Take a
   complement as a complement. Don't let it influence how you think and feel about yourself."*
   (ch17:229–239).
8. Makai Four Stars Restaurant, third floor: dim, red-themed, three or four occupied tables. A
   civilian-owned restaurant that has admitted shinobi — Yugao pulled strings (ch17:245–269).
9. Yugao asks who the girl in his house is. Naruto: **"Karin. She is family."** He reads her relief
   and names it aloud (ch17:277–291). She changes the subject to the council meeting.
10. Three hours of conversation. She cannot make him laugh but gets several smiles. He calls the
    evening *"a pleasant experience"* and says he finds it intriguing that he would find it pleasant
    (ch17:293–309).
11. At her door he draws her against him: *"You are not annoying and your presence is tolerable unlike
    any other women I have come across."* She raises Mei unprompted; he says Yugao is different. He
    then pushes her away by the chest and leaves without a goodnight (ch17:311–331).
12. **The Yondaime's house.** Naruto opens the barrier with his own blood, with Karin present. The
    house has been sealed roughly seventeen years; the seals protecting it are the work of a genius,
    which Naruto acknowledges only privately (ch17:333–355).
13. Karin is afraid of spiders. Naruto does not help her (ch17:363–377).
14. In the library Naruto finds a custom **three-pronged Hiraishin kunai**, then after nearly four
    hours the **Hiraishin formula and notes** and **notes on the Rasengan** — which Minato left
    incomplete, having intended to add a nature affinity (ch17:381–423). Naruto wants the space–time
    technique because he cannot travel like Zetsu or Obito.
15. He makes about 200 shadow clones: 50 to break down the Hiraishin (a week's expectation), 150 to
    learn the Rasengan (stages one and two by end of day; complete in two or three days). They train
    at the Uchiha compound (ch17:425–441).
16. Karin has read most of *Fuinjutsu for Beginners* but cannot yet apply it. Naruto makes her attack
    him and tells her plainly that her fighting is pathetic; he promises to shape her (ch17:449–479).
    He gives her money again and takes her to the Uchiha compound for the first time.
17. **Iwagakure.** Kabuto disappears from Ōnoki's custody: a vortex takes him, and the guards see a
    single Sharingan eye before it closes. No chakra or scent remains. Ōnoki rules out Kakashi and
    Sasuke, considers Itachi, and settles on Naruto as most likely (ch17:469–545).
18. Ōnoki's granddaughter **Kurotsuchi** reports that Oto is being rebuilt as a proper village by
    **Guren**, formerly Orochimaru's second only to Kabuto. Ōnoki sends her to Oto under cover of
    negotiating Iwa's assistance (ch17:509–549).
19. Naruto trains and studies obsessively; his clones work dawn to dusk. He refuses to be disturbed
    (ch17:551–563).
20. An ANBU is sent three times by Tsunade. On the third attempt Naruto turns cold enough to frighten
    Sasuke from a distance, then leaves in a swirl of **scorching black flames** — a Shunshin Sasuke
    has never seen, which he takes to be a foul-mood technique. Naruto returns in under two minutes.
    Tsunade had summoned him only to grant him the title of **Sannin** (ch17:565–585).
21. Sasuke has chosen **45** shinobi for the Police Force and asks for a training programme; Naruto
    sends him to Karin for the scrolls (ch17:587–597).
22. Naruto takes the Hiraishin decipherment away from the clones and works it himself (ch17:599–603).
23. Yugao has been seen once since the date, to tell him she is leaving on a solo mission. He simply
    nodded (ch17:605–609).
24. **Zetsu: "Danzou has moved against Yugao and Karin."** The solo mission was a fabrication Danzō
    created to take Yugao quietly. He intends to use **Shisui's eye** on both women to make them
    loyal spies inside Naruto's circle (ch17:611–629).
25. Naruto is infuriated, which in him reads as calm. He deliberately leaves his armour behind so he
    will not become excited and release too much power — which would destroy Root bases, alert the
    village, and damage his health. **"You will retrieve them while I punish Danzou."**
    (ch17:625–635). Zetsu takes Naruto's form so Yugao will not meet him.
26. **Root base.** Naruto kills everyone he meets, without speaking. Bodies burn with Amaterasu or
    are impaled on wooden stakes. This is explicitly contrasted with the Kusagakure hideout, where he
    killed no one (ch17:637–665).
27. **His left eye bleeds heavily** from repeated Amaterasu, far beyond what the usage explains. He
    does not wipe it. His body pulses with chakra (ch17:659–663).
28. Mokuton attacks come **out of his own body**: his right hand becomes multiple stakes; spikes erupt
    from every part of his body; later, living roots that bind, lift, and drain chakra
    (ch17:669–683, 763–777).
29. A group of Root allow themselves to be taken and then self-destruct. Naruto is thrown back and
    shielded by the **orange Susano'o ribcage**; his front takes several light burns
    (ch17:679–689).
30. In the throne hall about 150 Root surround him. Danzō threatens the women. Naruto forms a ring of
    Amaterasu around the throne and raises it into a **dome of black flames** with a viewing gap, and
    warns Danzō not to unseal his right arm or the barrier will collapse on him (ch17:693–719).
31. Zetsu, in Naruto's form, announces the women are already secured; a Root confirms the cell is
    empty (ch17:705–713).
32. Naruto drinks half a bottle of medicine before fighting. He deliberately limits Susano'o and
    chakra use for his health and fights mostly with Mokuton (ch17:721–733).
33. Danzō orders his men to kill Naruto if necessary but to preserve the Sharingan. Naruto kills all
    of them. Danzō is lifted and strangled by roots that drain his chakra (ch17:715–741).
34. Naruto names the punishment: he asks what Danzō loves most and answers it himself — Konoha and
    power — then takes both. **"I will take your Sharingans."** Danzō: *"You cannot take away the
    Sharingan, I will not allow it!"* (ch17:743–757).
35. **Injuries inflicted on Danzō, in order:** the forehead bandage unwrapped and the **right eye
    (Shisui's) removed slowly with Naruto's fingers**, without a scream; the **right arm severed at
    the shoulder** with a wind-charged sword taken from one of Danzō's own Root, then thrown into the
    air and burned with Amaterasu — destroying the Hashirama cells and **ten Sharingan**; then, after
    Naruto notes the self-destruct seal only worked on death, the **left leg broken and every bone in
    it crushed** (ch17:757–773).
36. Naruto's closing statement: he has left Danzō with nothing, invites him to kill himself, and tells
    him Konoha will belong to Naruto, who will be Hokage, while Danzō dies pathetic
    (ch17:771–777).
37. Shisui's eye goes to the hideout for future use (ch17:779).
38. Naruto leaves **exactly one Root alive** — the one who will keep Danzō alive — and has already
    ensured that man will lose all memory of the night within minutes. That Root has seen Zetsu
    (ch17:781–789).
39. At the compound: the women were taken to Karin's place. Naruto's medicine is failing — it weakens
    when Susano'o is active and his left eye is in enough pain that he says he could have gouged it
    out. He orders the **Shinigami mask** brought so he can take **Minato's soul from the death god's
    stomach tomorrow** (ch17:791–811).

### Start and end effect

- **Start:** Naruto is the most powerful person in Konoha and cannot open his own eyes without a drug.
- **End:** He has destroyed an army and a man over two people he claims not to care about, and the
  next thing he asks for is his father.

## Chapter 18 — legalising a massacre

### Immutable sequence

1. Naruto oversleeps, is woken by the same ANBU, and **puts his armour back on** — it restores his
   focus. He has not worn his forehead protector for days and nobody has commented (ch18:7–25).
2. **Flashback:** the Sannin-title summons. The office turns cold at Naruto's arrival. Tsunade
   attempts to assert authority and raises her chakra; Naruto brushes it off, calls her **"Senju,"**
   and raises his own for a second. She capitulates and tells him about the title. He answers with a
   sound and leaves (ch18:33–77). She has avoided him since.
3. Tsunade reports Danzō's condition: brought in barely alive by a Ne ANBU, right eye removed, right
   arm removed, one leg crushed beyond saving. She and Shizune saved his life (ch18:85).
4. Jiraiya reports the Root base: the entire force slaughtered, bloody walls, corpses, killed by wood,
   traces of black flames (ch18:87). No chakra signature or scent of Naruto was found; Inuzuka dogs
   found nothing. Jiraiya's case is circumstantial: only Naruto and Itachi use black flames, only
   Naruto and Tenzō use Mokuton (ch18:99–107).
5. Asked directly whether he did it, Naruto answers **"Maybe..."** and then refuses to answer at all
   (ch18:95–113). He states openly that he is pleased with what happened to Danzō.
6. Naruto then supplies, in sequence, four pieces of intelligence the Sannin did not have
   (ch18:133–213):
   - Danzō sent assassins after the Sandaime to take the hat, and the Sandaime, having found out,
     did nothing. **Kakashi is named as the man who told the Sandaime**, while still working for
     Danzō.
   - The Sandaime caught Orochimaru experimenting and allowed him to leave the village. Naruto
     admits he cannot prove this; both men are dead.
   - Danzō supplied Orochimaru with abducted children and was never punished.
   - Danzō attempted to steal Kakashi's Sharingan, fearing his own would go blind. This confirms to
     both Sannin that Danzō had implanted Sharingan.
   - Danzō assisted Orochimaru in the Sound/Sand invasion.
7. Jiraiya points out that the stolen Sharingan gives Naruto a motive. Naruto **agrees that any clan
   head would react that way** and does not deny it, then redirects (ch18:209–213).
8. **Sasuke enters on cue** with Police Force files and reports an S-rank operation carried out the
   previous night by the Military Police Force. Asked on whose authority: **"On my authority,
   Hokage-sama."** He argues the Police Force may act against Danzō because Root was never under the
   Hokage's control (ch18:217–245).
9. The files contain evidence of Danzō's treasons, including an attempt to scrap the Konoha–Iwa peace
   treaty, joint operations with Orochimaru, and **the kidnapping of Uzumaki Karin and Uzuki Yugao**
   (ch18:249–255). Tsunade concludes Danzō must be executed.
10. Sasuke refuses to leave copies: evidence gathered by the Police Force belongs to the Police Force
    and the Hokage alone may view it (ch18:277–285). He is ordered to the council at 1 p.m.
11. Jiraiya states outright that Naruto planned it and supplied the evidence. Naruto does not comment
    (ch18:271).
12. Tsunade privately concludes Naruto had the evidence all along and moved only because Danzō took
    Yugao and Karin, and that he knows more secrets than she and Jiraiya do (ch18:289–293).
13. Asked how he can use Mokuton, Naruto answers **"Genkai Kekkei"** and then refuses to explain
    further, comparing it to the origin of his Sharingan (ch18:295–305).
14. He **refuses the title of Sannin** as an insult and as a title given after a defeat. Tsunade
    grants the travel permit anyway, in exchange for Naruto sharing village secrets "in due time,"
    which he agrees to (ch18:307–335).
15. Asked how he can summon the Kyuubi, Naruto answers only **"Because I can"** and denies controlling
    it with the Sharingan (ch18:341–359). Neither Sannin can reconcile this with the seal.
16. Naruto restates that he will deal with the Akatsuki leader personally and will not name him.
    Jiraiya announces he is leaving for Suna and then Kumo (ch18:363–369).
17. Tsunade and Jiraiya agree to let Naruto believe he can do as he pleases and to watch him
    (ch18:377–389).
18. At the compound, Sasuke confirms he covered for Naruto knowingly and willingly; it also
    established the Police Force's competence within days of approval (ch18:391–413).
19. **Karin, at the training ground:** she felt weak and helpless when taken and now understands why
    Naruto wants her strong. She asks to be trained as hard as possible. Naruto: *"I will make you
    strong."* She argues the strong always devour the weak; he tells her not to conform to the
    world's principles but to make her own (ch18:417–439).
20. Naruto tells Karin about **Guren** and the **Crystal Palace**: his Oto headquarters, of his own
    design, four floors with the underground floor as his private quarters, exterior of wood and
    crystal, interior mostly crystal including ceilings and floors, larger than the Hokage tower. She
    will live there and help Guren once she has the basics; her presence must stay hidden until the
    rebuilding is complete (ch18:441–475).
21. Danzō will be executed soon; Karin is told so (ch18:477–481). She asks whether Yugao is his
    girlfriend; Naruto leaves without answering (ch18:485–489).
22. **Akatsuki assembly** — ten figures as holographic images. The Leader (Rinnegan) introduces two
    new members, **Tobi and Kabuto**, paired together for intelligence rather than jinchūriki capture.
    Hidan objects to Kabuto's rank; Kisame notes another Uchiha to Itachi, who does not respond. Asked
    if he is an Uchiha, Tobi answers only that he might be or might not (ch18:491–525).
23. **Assignments:** Sasori and Deidara to Shukaku, whose jinchūriki is now Kazekage; Kakuzu and Hidan
    to the Two-Tails outside Kumo. The plan continues even though the Sanbi has not reformed. Tobi
    warns them about Kumo's controlled jinchūriki and **"Watch out for Uchiha Naruto, he is not just
    your normal Shinobi."** Shukaku is expected within a week (ch18:519–535).
24. After the others leave, Tobi tells the Leader that **he should collect the Kyuubi himself**. The
    Leader narrows his eyes and says nothing (ch18:535–539).
25. Naruto has already retrieved **Minato's soul from the Shinigami** — off-page. He did not let the
    man speak, and stored him in a coffin for later. He will go to **Dragon Land** to finish his
    business with the Kyuubi (ch18:539–547).
26. Zetsu reports the Crystal Palace nearly complete (ch18:549–553).
27. **Yugao arrives.** She notes he is wearing his armour again, embraces him, thanks him for the save
    — calling Danzō's punishment "a bit extreme" — pushes his right bang aside and kisses his cheek.
    He gives no physical reaction. She says only, *"I will see you tonight,"* and walks away
    (ch18:555–571). Naruto reverse-summons himself.

### Knowledge state at chapter end

- Tsunade and Jiraiya believe Naruto destroyed Root and cannot prove it; the operation is now legally
  the Police Force's.
- Neither knows how Naruto uses Mokuton, how he can summon the Kyuubi, or who the Akatsuki leader is.
- Yugao believes **Naruto** rescued her. She has never seen Zetsu and must not.
- Karin knows about Oto, Guren, and the Crystal Palace.
- Akatsuki now names Naruto as a threat.

## Chapter 19 — a promise kept and an account closed

### Immutable sequence

1. **Dragon Land.** Zetsu expects Yugao to be emotionally fragile after the kidnapping; Naruto argues
   ANBU training makes that impossible, and that Karin survived working for Orochimaru
   (ch19:5–29).
2. Kurama is found sleeping. Naruto: *"Today is that day I make true to my promise."* Kurama grins and
   admits he had begun to think Naruto was only words (ch19:31–43).
3. **Kuchiyose: Edo Tensei.** A single coffin. Naruto plants a kunai carrying orders at the back of
   Minato's head and animates him. Minato asks how the Kyuubi is free and where Naruto is; Naruto
   **removes his personality** to silence him (ch19:45–57).
4. Minato touches Kurama; a seal spreads from his stomach across both of them; Kurama grows back to
   full size. Kurama's thanks is a paw strike that sends Minato flying (ch19:59–63).
5. **"You now deserve to be called Kurama."** No human has used the name except the Sage. Kurama
   settles for a different kind of snort — he does not thank Naruto (ch19:65–69).
6. Zetsu declines to watch the conversation with Minato, judging it personal (ch19:71–75).
7. **The bench conversation.** Naruto creates two benches with Mokuton, restores Minato's personality,
   and opens with Kushina: *"Mother said you are the most kindest man she knew. Now, I don't care for
   that... So Minato, tell me your story."* (ch19:77–97).
8. **Minato's account, as source truth** (ch19:99–137): his appointment after the Third War; Kakashi
   secretly guarding the pregnant Kushina for ten months; the secluded birth; the masked man killing
   the guards, the nurses and **Biwako**; Naruto taken and rigged with explosive tags and saved by
   teleport; Kushina's Kyuubi extracted and the beast set on her; Minato saving her; the Bijūdama
   stopped by a space–time barrier; the fight in which he **severed the masked man's control** of the
   Kyuubi and was told the man would return; the decision to seal the Kyuubi in his own son so the boy
   could stop that man; Gamabunta and the teleport out of the village; Kushina's chakra chains and
   barrier; her wish to re-seal the beast in herself; the **Dead Demon Consuming Seal** taking the Yin
   half and his life; both parents shielding the infant, which sealed Kushina's fate; the **Eight
   Trigrams Seal**; and both parents' chakra sealed inside Naruto — Minato's to reinforce a weakening
   seal, Kushina's to help him control the beast.
9. Minato's plan assumed **Kushina would live** and raise Naruto with Jiraiya, the Sandaime and
   Kakashi to protect him. Naruto answers with what actually happened: his mother died, Jiraiya left,
   the Sandaime watched, Kakashi was forbidden (ch19:139–145).
10. Naruto states he hated Minato and would have killed him painfully, and that he chose to disregard
    him as a father to avoid being consumed by hatred; he learned to discard emotion, but cannot
    forget (ch19:151).
11. **"I will be Hokage one day. At this time, I should become the Rokudaime."** (ch19:161).
12. Naruto says that had he not met Kushina he would have left Minato to rot; instead, on release,
    Minato will go where Kushina is rather than back into the Shinigami's stomach (ch19:165).
13. Minato's reply is the source's line: he has no right to tell Naruto how to live, Naruto may never
    call him father, but he is loved and will always be a source of pride whichever path he takes; he
    does not attempt an embrace (ch19:167–169).
14. Naruto releases him without comment (ch19:171).
15. Kurama wants Minato back to tear apart repeatedly. Naruto refuses — the Edo Tensei feels no
    physical pain, and Minato has "expired." He has no intention of using the technique again; only
    the knowledge remains (ch19:181–195).
16. **"There one who will kill Obito is me."** Kurama is refused his revenge (ch19:205–207).
17. **Council chambers.** Koharu demands Naruto's arrest. Sasuke — as Police Force head — challenges
    her for evidence; she says none is needed (ch19:209–221).
18. **Naruto admits it in open council:** he is not denying that he slaughtered Root and crippled
    Danzō, because he did it. The clan heads are not surprised (ch19:223–225).
19. Koharu calls for ANBU twice; none come. Tsunade slams the table and warns her never again to
    attempt to command her ANBU, releasing killing intent (ch19:227–237).
20. Sasuke presents the evidence. Hiashi asks why ANBU were not used; Sasuke states the Police Force
    operates separately. Homura objects to slaughtering an entire force; Naruto answers that a
    surviving Root would have revolted and that they were as guilty as their master
    (ch19:239–263).
21. **Shikaku secures the concession** that the Police Force must inform the Hokage before such
    operations in future. Sasuke accepts (ch19:265–269).
22. **Tsunade decrees Danzō's private execution as soon as he wakes.** Nobody objects, including the
    elders. The matter is declared S-rank and sealed inside Police Force headquarters so the villagers
    never learn of it (ch19:271–277).
    **The execution itself is never depicted in the source.**
23. Tsunade announces the **Mizukage has crossed the Fire Country border** (ch19:279–283).
24. **Hiashi approaches Naruto privately** — and Naruto uses formalities, calling him "Hiashi-san,"
    which surprises him (ch19:285–291).
25. At the compound Sasuke learns Hiashi proposed marrying one of his daughters to one of them. Naruto
    refused the marriage but **took the younger daughter as a future Police Force recruit**, calling
    her a "child" with much potential (ch19:293–299).
26. Naruto teaches Sasuke **Uchiha Hono Shiurudo** — his own invention, created after watching Neji at
    the Chūnin Exams. Chakra released through the tenketsu as an extremely hot vapour, turned to fire
    with a tiger seal, then spun into a dome of swirling flames; defensive and offensive; at high
    control it can launch fireballs in all directions. He demonstrates twice, slowly, and forbids
    Sasuke to use his Sharingan to copy it. He warns that a mistake means third-degree burns or death
    and recommends practising with a clone (ch19:301–319).
27. **Yugao is cooking in his kitchen.** Naruto's opening line is that if he did not know better he
    would say she was married. He explains he had a friend study couples for him because he will
    marry one day and wants the knowledge (ch19:321–361).
28. He asks whether she is taking advantage of the fact that he finds her presence pleasing; she
    answers *"Perhaps"* in his own idiom and he is visibly amused (ch19:365–381).
29. He mentions brothels as a place he once conducted experiments and took lessons, and offers to show
    her. She agrees. **"Sharingan: Genjutsu"** — he shows her the memory as an illusion
    (ch19:383–399).
    **Adaptation lock: stage this by implication only** — the building, her face, the reaction, the
    aftermath. Nothing explicit. See the series bible's content-staging rule.
30. She flees the room, returns, and admits it will be hard to speak to him now. He counters that he
    has already seen her naked and cleaned her while she was unconscious (ch19:401–421).
31. **"There is no one else that is more suitable to be my mate and be the subject of my affections if
    they are there... You hold the keys; you have not answered my question after all."** She smiles and
    says nothing; the silence is deliberate (ch19:427–431).
32. **Oto, two days later.** The village is lively and rebuilding; the unity did not exist under
    Orochimaru. Guren watches from the top floor of the Crystal Palace. She has killed challengers
    publicly to make examples; Zetsu has been thinning rebels privately and devouring them out of
    sight. The hospital and the ANBU and jōnin headquarters are nearly finished; businesses are moving
    in. Her problem is a **shortage of experienced medics** (ch19:433–479).
33. Zetsu tells her the Tsuchikage's granddaughter is arriving on an intelligence mission and orders
    her to let Kurotsuchi in and reveal nothing — partly for amusement, partly to see what Naruto does
    when Iwa forces itself into his "play." Nothing traceable to Naruto exists; Zetsu has never used
    his name (ch19:481–507).
34. Kurotsuchi arrives openly admiring the village, offers Iwa's alignment, and asks for a tour. Guren
    smiles and gives her one (ch19:509–531).

## Chapter 20 — the price is named

### Immutable sequence

1. **Mei arrives in Konoha** with Ao and Chōjūrō, in Mizukage robes. Her last visit was to beg the
   Sandaime for help during the civil war and she was refused; she has not forgiven him. She has come
   because of Naruto, and she worries about what he will do when he grows bored of a world that cannot
   challenge him (ch20:7–43).
2. Tsunade receives her formally, offers a hotel and a tour, and warns her ANBU will follow her
   (ch20:45–77).
3. Mei asks where Naruto is. While waiting, the two Kage compare notes on him — Tsunade admits she
   sometimes wants to punch him; Mei reports **the only time she saw him happy was before the battle**
   (ch20:79–113). The ANBU returns: Naruto is busy. Mei's face flickers (ch20:113–117).
4. **Lunch with Yugao.** Naruto agreed for reasons he does not examine; he notes he could not deny
   her. He tries one stick of dango, calls the food bad flatly, and refuses to pretend otherwise
   (ch20:119–137).
5. Yugao asks him to walk through the village with her and then to hold hands. **"No."** He privately
   asks himself whether this is the heart and whether he has developed feelings, and postpones the
   question (ch20:141–155).
6. **Anko and Kurenai intercept them.** Naruto recognises Anko as the second-exam proctor and calls
   her methods of instilling fear pathetic; he reminds Kurenai of the preliminary round when she
   objected to his beating of Kiba. As they part he warns Anko not to approach him again **reeking of
   snakes**, because they remind him of Orochimaru (ch20:157–195).

## Chapter 21 — the questions

### Immutable sequence

1. **Suna, reported not shown** (ch21:17–31): Deidara threatened to destroy parts of the village
   unless Gaara was handed over; **the villagers refused to give up their Kazekage** and coped with the
   explosions; Deidara barely escaped while being overwhelmed. **Gaara's right leg was crushed and his
   right hand cut off**; he lost consciousness from blood loss and pain but survived. Suna's top
   shinobi fought Sasori, who poisoned several of them but **was killed**, because they were prepared
   by Naruto's intelligence. **Zetsu killed the escaping Deidara and took his head to Guren for the
   bounty** — money Oto needs. Gaara's sand defence has improved to the point that speed alone will no
   longer bypass it.
2. A messenger is already on the way to Konoha for medical help (ch21:27).
3. Naruto counts his position: Suna, Kiri, Konoha and Oto are all close to him, and he considers
   taking **Amegakure** after fighting Nagato (ch21:41).
4. **The questions begin.** Naruto asks Zetsu whether he is worse than Madara, who at least wanted
   peace in his own way — and then: **"Do you think my mother would be proud if she saw me walking
   about without care in the world?"** Zetsu does not answer either time. Naruto attributes the
   questions to Yugao, or to something buried surfacing, and states that nothing has changed
   (ch21:45–57).
5. Ōnoki has sent **two spies** to Oto to assist and protect Kurotsuchi. Naruto's order:
   **"Deal with them and then drop them in Ohnoki's office, dead."** — calculated to frighten Iwa into
   caution (ch21:59–65).
6. **Hokage's office.** The Kiri alliance is finalised; the marriage terms are left to Naruto and Mei.
   Naruto's answer is a flat "Okay," which surprises Tsunade. Mei asks him to be happy about it and to
   dance for her; he refuses (ch21:67–83).
7. **Tsunade's statement of trust** (ch21:93–107): Suna's gratitude for Naruto's help in the failed
   abduction; the Wave trade agreement; the Kiri alliance and healthy relations with the Water Daimyō;
   smaller villages seeing Konoha favourably. She thanks him as Hokage, states she is now positive he
   has nothing against Konoha and does not believe he will betray it, and offers to put their clashes
   behind them. Naruto calls it unexpected, calculates that accepting smooths his eventual succession,
   and agrees: *"Very well then."*
8. **Karin will be moved to Oto.** Naruto plans to tell Tsunade openly that Karin is returning to the
   village that was her home, to look as though he is hiding nothing (ch21:113–127).
9. Karin's progress: she can make working explosive tags with a small blast radius, and can now throw a
   punch like a genin but without power. Naruto does not want her built like a man but will not excuse
   weak strikes (ch21:129–139).
10. Naruto will **move Minato's library to the Crystal Palace** and use the palace as his base of
    operations once his ownership of Oto is known; the Hokage goal is unchanged (ch21:145–147).
11. **Mei in the compound garden.** She restates that she wants love, not only a political marriage,
    and that this is her one chance. Naruto recites her history back to her — a good childhood, then
    Yagura's reign, her parents killed, leading the rebels. She tells him he must talk about his past
    to be happy; he says he dislikes the subject (ch21:159–185).
12. **"We will have to put everything on hold until I finish some things."** He says he already has
    distractions and will not add another; when asked why he agreed at all, he ignores the question.
    He tells her to be patient and that he will come to her when his objectives are complete or near
    it. He tells her to visit Oto before returning to Kiri, and leaves without answering her final
    question about whether he dislikes the changes around him (ch21:187–215).
13. Mei decides to be formally **engaged before leaving** — no ring — so the title reminds him of her
    (ch21:217–219).
14. **Iwagakure.** Ōnoki has given up finding Kabuto. He worries about Kurotsuchi. **Two corpses drop
    into his office** — his own two ANBU, still warm. No chakra, no scent, nothing seen at the gates.
    He recalls Kurotsuchi and resolves to send no more shinobi to Oto; if it continues he will consider
    war (ch21:221–259).
15. **Konoha, night.** Naruto sits shirtless in an unlit room, his eyes the only light. He inventories
    his position — future Hokage, leader of Oto, possible leader of Ame, tied to Kiri and Suna — and
    asks which path he will take. He smiles bitterly at his own emotions surfacing
    (ch21:261–271).
16. **Yugao comes to his room** — a room he has never shown her. She raises the marriage: she felt
    something move in her heart, and she thought she had lost her chance. **"But now I can say that
    yes, I do like you."** (ch21:277–291).
17. Naruto's answer: **"I already told you, you are acceptable to me. I would not throw you away after
    saying that."** She kisses him; he returns it. She asks to stay the night; *"Do as you please."*
    She asks him to close his eyes while she undresses; he points out he has already seen her naked
    (ch21:291–303).
18. **They only sleep.** Nothing else happens. Yugao sleeps well and privately resents that she will
    have to share him with Mei and will not be his first wife (ch21:303–319).
19. **She wakes alone.** His forehead protector is still on the nightstand; the armour and Gunbai are
    gone. Sasuke tells her Naruto left the village two hours earlier for **Otogakure**, taking Karin,
    and that he already informed the Hokage. Expected back in two or three days; he also mentioned
    "cleaning." Yugao is hurt that he left without telling her; Sasuke advises her to give him time,
    since knowledge and experience are not the same thing (ch21:321–351).
20. **Oto.** A gate guard halts them and demands papers; Naruto hands over a paper rather than use the
    Sharingan. Karin sees a beautiful small Konoha: new gates, children playing, people from other
    small villages and wanderers. She is awed by the **Crystal Palace**, which she calls double the
    size of the Hokage tower, and revises her opinion of Naruto on seeing what his mind designed
    (ch21:353–393).

    either way. He lays a **permanent privacy seal** over the office even though Zetsu clones are
    already guarding it (ch21:395–411).
22. **Mei left Oto around noon; the Oto–Kiri treaty is signed** (ch21:409–413).
23. Karin will live in Oto and help Guren; Naruto leaves a large scroll of scrolls for the palace
    library for Guren and Karin to shelve, and orders Karin's training to continue
    (ch21:413–421).
24. **Jiraiya ambushes him outside Oto.** He has been digging: Naruto was seen in Kusagakure near the
    hideout Guren left after his visit. Naruto neither confirms nor denies and suggests he might have
    been taking Guren as a mate. Jiraiya attacks with **Ōdama Rasengan**; Naruto raises the **Gunbai**
    and uses **"Uchiha Return,"** reflecting the technique into Jiraiya, who is thrown but not really
    hurt (ch21:423–457).
25. Naruto warns him not to attack again, says he has no time for a petty dance, and then indulges him
    once: he was returning Karin to her home because Konoha would not do it for her. Tsunade knows
    (ch21:457–467).
26. Jiraiya notes that **Naruto has come to resemble Madara**, which disturbs him, and cannot explain
    the reflection. He considers whether Naruto engineered the Kiri alliance, decides that is paranoia,
    and resolves to give him the benefit of the doubt (ch21:453–469).
27. **Valley of the End.** Naruto stands on Madara's statue's head, looking at Hashirama's, and places
    himself between light and dark. His mission is to clean out a bandit camp deeper in Fire Country
    (ch21:471–475).
28. **Obito arrives by Kamui vortex.** He says Madara trained them differently and never made his one
    dream — uniting the world under a single genjutsu — into Naruto's dream, whereas Obito was made to
    agree to revive Madara with Rinne Rebirth (ch21:477–485).
29. **Obito's questions, which Naruto never answers** (ch21:487–493): why did Madara abandon a dream he
    had spent an age preparing; why did Obito leave him alive rather than kill him; why did he not
    force the same dream on Naruto; why did he only come to Naruto after Obito left; why, knowing of
    him from birth, did he let him suffer for six years. Obito's conclusion: *"It would be a naive
    thought to believe that a heartless man like Madara could ever be capable of loving you,"* and
    Naruto was only a child longing for affection who would have believed anything. He closes with
    **"Uchiha Madara, reconsidering his ideals? Laughable"** and Kamuis out.
30. **Naruto's face gives nothing.** He walks to the edge of the statue, drops to the water, walks to
    the waterfall, closes his eyes, and jumps down. **The source deliberately withholds his reaction.**
    (ch21:495–499).

## Character state changes

- **Naruto** begins the volume unable to activate his Sharingan without medicine and ends it having
  been told, by the man he intends to murder, that the only person who ever loved him never did. In
  between he keeps a promise to Kurama, closes his account with Minato, destroys Root, accepts a
  marriage, acquires a girlfriend, and starts asking questions he has never asked: whether he is worse
  than Madara, and whether his mother would be proud. **The source states explicitly that none of this
  changes his goals.** He is fractionally warmer, not different.
- **Yugao** moves from an unanswered question to a stated relationship, sleeps in his bed without sex,
  is kidnapped and rescued, and is the only person whose questions land on him.
- **Karin** is kidnapped, asks to be made strong, and moves to Oto with a real job.
- **Sasuke** takes public authority for an operation he did not carry out, defends it in council,
  builds the Police Force to 45 recruits, learns Naruto's invented jutsu, and still has **no
  Mangekyō** — he envies Naruto's (ch22:52).
- **Danzō** loses Root, an eye, an arm, ten Sharingan, a leg, his chakra and his future, and is
  condemned to a private execution the village will never hear about.
- **Kurama** is made whole, is named by a human for the second time in history, and agrees to be used
  without agreeing to obey.
- **Minato** tells his story, is forgiven nothing, is not called father, and is released to Kushina.
- **Tsunade** goes from summoning Naruto for a mock-honour to publicly thanking him and stating she
  believes he will not betray Konoha.
- **Jiraiya** goes from active suspicion to a decision to give Naruto the benefit of the doubt — after
  losing an exchange to his own technique.
- **Mei** secures the alliance and the engagement and is told to wait.
- **Ōnoki** loses his prisoner, gains a suspicion, loses two ANBU, recalls his granddaughter, and
  decides not to send anyone else to Oto.
- **Obito** joins Akatsuki as Tobi, has Kabuto extracted and installed as his partner, names Naruto a
  threat, pushes Nagato at the Kyuubi, and delivers the volume's final attack — with words.

## Exclusions — what Volume 6 must not depict

1. **Danzō's execution.** Decreed on page, carried out off-page. It is confirmed only in passing in
   ch22. Volume 6 ends with it pending or reported, never staged.
2. **The Suna battle.** Sasori's death and Deidara's escape and beheading are told to Naruto by Zetsu.
   Do not dramatise them as a scene — the point is that Naruto chose not to go.
3. **Gaara on page.** He does not appear.
4. **The Hiraishin in use.** Studied only.
5. **Katon: Rasengan in combat.** Created, not fired, until ch22.
6. **Kusanagi carried.** It stays at the hideout until ch22.
7. **Shisui's eye used.** Recovered and stored only.
8. **Any answer to Obito's questions.** The source withholds Naruto's reaction entirely; so must the
   volume. No thought balloon, no caption, no reaction line that resolves it.
9. **Zetsu revealed to Yugao.**
10. **Sasuke's Mangekyō.** Not awakened.
11. **The Nibi capture, Hidan and Kakuzu on page, and Itachi's confrontation with Sasuke.** All ch22
    and later. Akatsuki appears in Volume 6 only in the holographic assembly and as Tobi at the Valley
    of the End.
12. **The brothel memory shown explicitly.** Stage by implication only — the exterior, her face, the
    slap, the aftermath, the reaction. See the series bible's content-staging rule.
13. **Any cure or diagnosis beyond what ch20 states.** The virus is not solved in this volume.
