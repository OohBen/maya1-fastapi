## Source effect

Two people spend a chapter finding out where the line is, and both of them find it in the same place.
Mei has come a very long way to see a man who is told about her and does not come; the flicker on her
face when the ANBU brings back the word "busy" is the only crack she shows all volume, and it lands
in a room where the other Kage is admitting she sometimes wants to punch him. Then the chapter cuts —
inside the spread, deliberately — to the man himself, eating dango he despises with someone else.

Everything after that cut is a slow accumulation of the same discovery. He will not pretend the food
is good. He will not hold her hand. He will not go with the others. He will not stop being what he
is for a single line of dialogue. And in the middle of all that refusal he says two things he has no
tactical reason to say — that she is proving to be quite a woman, and that he prefers being here with
her than with them — and both of them notice him saying them.

So the last spread must not read as a breakthrough. Yugao asks the three questions the volume is
actually about; the source gives his heart one line of trouble and then has him postpone the whole
subject, exactly as he has postponed it since Chapter 2. His answer is four words and it concedes
nothing except that she might be right, and the bowl in front of him has gone cold while he was
proving a point to people he does not care about. **The chapter's engine is that he gets what he
wanted — nobody's company but hers — and it costs him an answer he does not have.**

## Final dialogue and executable `name`

Reading order is right-to-left within each page. Coordinates use page width `x=0–100` from left to
right and page height `y=0–100` from top to bottom. Panel 1 is always the top-right panel; within a
row, a lower-numbered panel is always further right. Balloon numbers restart on every page and are
final lettering order; within a panel the first-read balloon sits further right or higher. Every
balloon record gives placement and a visible tail target or explicitly states `OFF`/tail-less.

Integrated ImageGen lettering is intended. During production, each generated page must be compared at
full size with the exact page/panel/speaker order below; any missing, duplicated, misspelled, moved,
or misattributed line fails the page and requires editing or regeneration.

**Global geometry locks — these prevent tail inversion and must not be varied.**

1. **The Hokage's office (pp. 1 memory, 2–4).** Tsunade behind her desk at **frame left** with the
   window behind her; the door and the visitors' chairs at **frame right**. Mei is always frame-right
   of Tsunade. **Ao stands at Mei's frame-right shoulder and Chōjūrō at her frame-left shoulder in
   every panel in which they appear.** In the page 1 memory the Sandaime occupies the frame-left desk
   position and the younger Mei stands frame right — the same axis, so the reader reads the memory as
   the same room.
2. **Naruto is the rightmost principal figure in every panel of the Naruto half (pp. 5–18), without
   exception.** Yugao is immediately to his frame left. Anko, Kurenai, Teuchi, Ayame and the nine
   Konoha shinobi are all further frame-left again. Any balloon of his that must exit a panel exits
   the **right** edge.
3. **Ichiraku (pp. 10–18).** The counter runs across the frame in the middle ground. Naruto and
   Yugao sit on the near side in three-quarter rear view, Naruto frame right. Teuchi and Ayame work
   on the far side and are seen facing camera; **Ayame always enters from the frame-left kitchen
   doorway and crosses rightward** — that crossing is the scene's only staging movement and it is how
   the reader knows she is coming for Naruto. The nine arrivals stand in the street behind the pair,
   entering frame from the **left**.
4. **Direction of travel is leftward** in every walking panel, so movement reads forward in
   right-to-left order.
