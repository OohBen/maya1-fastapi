## Source effect

The volume's engine in miniature. Naruto loses an evening he cannot justify spending and finds it
pleasant, which is the only thing in the chapter that surprises him. Yugao wants to be seen, gets
refused a compliment and given a philosophy instead, and discovers she prefers the second thing. The
question she has carried since Volume 5 — who is the red-haired girl — is answered in four words and
her relief is then read aloud to her face, which is what it costs to ask this man anything. At the
door he says the warmest sentence he has ever said to a woman, immediately puts a hand on her chest
and pushes her away, and leaves without a goodnight. She smiles anyway. The reader should understand
that neither of them experienced a failure.

## Continuity in

- The same day as Chapter 1, after dark. Naruto has not taken anything on page and his Sharingan
  remains inactive; his eyes are plain blue throughout.
- Naruto wears his usual black **without gloves**, without a forehead protector, without armour, and
  carries no weapon.
- Yugao made the reservation and comes to his door. Neither of them treats that as strange.
- Sasuke has the Hokage's documents; the registered-shinobi files arrive in three days; the Police
  Force HQ plans are not started. He and Naruto have been talking easily since Naruto's return two
  days ago, which is new for both of them.
- Karin lives in Naruto's old house under his protection. Yugao has seen her once and has been
  privately assuming the worst since ch16.
- The council meeting Naruto humiliated the elders at happened earlier the same day; Yugao heard
  about it second-hand at ANBU HQ and was not present.
- Mei is the Godaime Mizukage and there is no engagement yet. Yugao raises her name unprompted.
