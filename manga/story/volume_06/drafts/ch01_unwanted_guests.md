## Source effect

Four volumes of refused approaches are closed in one sitting. Kakashi finally asks the question he
has been carrying since Team 7, and Naruto answers it completely and honestly — Minato is nothing,
the villagers are nothing, Jiraiya is nothing, and Kakashi is the single exception. Kakashi's relief
is then cut off before he can build anything on it, and the exception is converted into a rule:
do not come here again without business. The reader should feel that Naruto gave Kakashi more than
he has ever given anyone, and took it back in the same breath.

Underneath that, the volume's actual subject is established without a word of explanation: the most
powerful person in Konoha tries to open his Sharingan on an empty morning, fails, and asks a plant
for a stronger drug.

## Dialogue QA

- 79 balloons and one chapter marker across 14 pages; the marker is exactly
  "CHAPTER 1 — UNWANTED GUESTS". Balloon numbering restarts and stays contiguous on every page.
- Density is deliberately uneven: pages 1 and 2 carry one to five short thought balloons over five
  silent or near-silent panels; pages 3, 7, 8 and 10 run seven balloons of hard two-man exchange;
  page 12 has four balloons across six panels, three of which are wordless.
- Preserved verbatim from `SOURCE_TRUTH.md`: "THERE ARE MANY PEOPLE I SHOULD WATCH DIE AND NOT CARE,
  EVEN THE SLIGHTEST." (p9 B1, split from the second half at p9 B3, "BUT YOU ARE NOT ONE OF THEM.")
  and "I HAVE NO NEED FOR TRIVIAL THINGS AS FRIENDSHIP." (p10 B6).
- Kakashi's four tactics are distinct and escalate: scenery, grievance, self-justification, apology.
  He never repeats an argument and he loses each one.
- Naruto's lines question, refuse, dismiss, exempt, and then legislate. No line of his summarises
  plot for the reader's benefit.
- Nobody names Danzō, Root, the Akatsuki leader, Kurama, Minato's house, Yugao, Karin, Mei, or the
  Hiraishin. The only forward information in the chapter is the Edo Tensei preparation, which the
  source places here.
- Naruto never says what is wrong with him, because in the source he does not know: page 13 has him
  refuse the dependence without naming the cause, and the blood analysis and full body scan are not
  mentioned to Kakashi at all.
- No caption box, narration box, or time card is used in this chapter.

## Visual and continuity QA

- Naruto's irises are plain blue in all 81 panels. No tomoe, no Mangekyō, no Eternal Mangekyō, no
  red. Page 12 panels 3–5 are the proof, and no later panel contradicts them.
- Naruto wears plain black, **no forehead protector, no gloves, no armour, no Gunbai, no sword.**
  The lost V3 ninjato is not replaced.
- **Naruto never sits on the Yondaime's carved head.** The series bible records that he stopped doing
  so. He is on the bare clifftop ledge beside the heads on pages 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 and 14, and page 7
  panel 5 must read unambiguously as him seated *next to* the stone face and not on it.
- Kakashi's book is visible on page 2 panel 5 and put away on page 3 panel 1; it never returns.
- The chapter axis is fixed after page 3 panel 1: Naruto left, Kakashi right, the village below and
  to the left. The two men do not look at each other until page 7 panel 1, and Kakashi turns to
  Naruto once before that, on page 5 panel 5.
- Zetsu emerges from solid stone and never walks on. He is a half-figure on page 11 panel 2, waist-up
  on panel 5, and half-sunk on page 14 panel 1. He is never shown with legs.
- The medicine bottle on page 13 panel 1 is an insert of a room elsewhere; **nobody drinks it in this
  chapter.** The two on-page doses belong to Chapter 4 and Chapter 5 per the volume hard lock.
- No jutsu is used by anyone. No Shunshin is drawn for Kakashi's exit — only a small dispersing leaf
  swirl on page 10 panel 6, which does not read as a technique with a named origin.
- All in-panel writing is ILLEGIBLE SCRIBBLE: shop signage on page 1 panel 2 and page 8 panel 3, and
  the bottle label on page 13 panel 1. The only deliberate lettering is the chapter marker.
- No balloon covers a face, an eye, or the open palm on page 14 panel 5.
- Weather, light and time hold across the whole chapter: one continuous morning, no time card, no
  scene change. The chapter never leaves the Hokage Monument.
