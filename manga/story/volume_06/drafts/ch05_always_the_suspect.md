## Source effect

The volume's densest chapter of exposition, and the trap is that all five of its revelations could be
delivered as a receipt. They must not be, because none of the three people in the room wants the same
thing. Jiraiya wants a confession and keeps almost getting one. Tsunade wants a village she can
still govern and is frightened of the boy in the chair for reasons she will not say out loud. And
Naruto wants nothing from either of them except that Sasuke should arrive on schedule — every
"revelation" he hands over is a paving stone laid in front of a door that opens at exactly the right
minute.

So the reader should feel two things at once. First, that Naruto is telling the truth the entire
time: the Sandaime really did let Danzō try to kill him and did nothing, really did let Orochimaru
walk, really did watch children be handed over. Every word of it is real, and it is also all
tactical. Second, that he beat them without lying once. He says "maybe", he says "I have no need to
give you an answer", he agrees out loud that he had a motive — and then a door opens and a massacre
becomes the first successful operation of a legal institution. He walks out with the travel rights he
wanted, having refused the honour attached to them, and Tsunade's last line is not triumph or
suspicion. It is a woman who has just realised she has no idea what she signed.

  already in the hospital; Tsunade and Shizune saved his life overnight.
- Naruto has slept longer than he ever does, has bathed off-page, and the burns and blood of Chapter 4

  brought anything stronger.
- Naruto has not worn his forehead protector for several days — Chapters 1, 2, 3 and 4 all show him
  without it — and nobody has commented. He puts it back on in this chapter and keeps it for the rest
  of the volume until ch21.
- He has not spoken to Tsunade since she summoned him to give him the Sannin title; she has avoided
  him since. That summons is this chapter's flashback and its beats are locked by `SOURCE_TRUTH.md`.
- Sasuke was briefed and given the evidence **last night**, after Naruto punished Danzō. That
  arrangement is confirmed on page in Chapter 6, not here; here the reader only sees it work.
- No chakra signature and no scent of Naruto were found at the base. Inuzuka dogs found nothing.
- Root does not exist. Exactly one member survives, with the night erased.
- Orochimaru's poison is undiagnosed. Naruto tells nobody about it in this chapter.

## Dialogue QA

- **122 balloons, one chapter marker and three location/time cards across 18 pages.** The marker is
  exactly "CHAPTER 5 — ALWAYS THE SUSPECT". The three cards are exactly "THE NEXT MORNING" (p1),
  "HOKAGE'S OFFICE" (p4 CAP1) and "DAYS AGO" (p4 CAP2). Balloon numbering restarts and stays
  contiguous on every page.
- Per-page balloon counts, summing to 122: p1 4, p2 2, p3 3, p4 1, p5 6, p6 7, p7 7, p8 5, p9 7,
  p10 8, p11 9, p12 6, p13 10, p14 9, p15 7, p16 9, p17 9, p18 13.
- Per-page panel counts, summing to 123: p1 6, p2 6, p3 7, p4 7, p5 7, p6 8, p7 7, p8 6, p9 7, p10 6,
  p11 7, p12 6, p13 7, p14 7, p15 7, p16 7, p17 7, p18 8.
- **This is deliberately the volume's wordiest chapter and the density is still not uniform.** Pages
  1–4 average two and a half balloons — the waking sequence is nearly silent and page 4 carries a
  single thought across seven panels. The interrogation then runs at seven to ten a page, and page 18
  spikes to thirteen because four refusals, one bargain and an exit have to close in one page.
- Preserved from `SOURCE_TRUTH.md`'s verbatim list: "SENJU." (p6 B2), "MAYBE..." (p8 B5), "GENKAI
  KEKKEI." (p17 B8), "BECAUSE I CAN." (p18 B9), and Sasuke's "ON MY AUTHORITY, HOKAGE-SAMA." (p16
  B2). Naruto's answer to the Sannin summons is rendered as the single syllable "HN." (p6 B6) and
  must not be expanded into a sentence.
- **The three agendas never merge.** Jiraiya asks eleven questions and lands none of them; his lines
  are accusation, defence of his teacher, and one muttered complaint. Tsunade never once accuses
  Naruto directly — she governs, she reports, she bargains, and her three thought balloons carry
  everything she will not say aloud. Naruto answers only what advances the door opening at p14 panel
  7, and the only line in the chapter he volunteers without being asked is "I AM PLEASED WITH WHAT
  HAS HAPPENED TO HIM."
- **Naruto never lies and never confesses.** "MAYBE...", "I HAVE NO NEED TO GIVE YOU AN ANSWER", "I
  REFUSE TO ANSWER" and "YOU ARE CORRECT. THAT IS HOW ANY CLAN HEAD WOULD REACT" are the four
  positions he holds all chapter, and a reviewer must check that no added line converts any of them
  into an admission or a denial. **The open admission belongs to Chapter 8 (ch19:223) and must not be
  pre-empted here.**
- Sasuke has seven balloons and every one of them is institutional. He never refers to Naruto, never
  defends him, and never explains the Police Force to the reader — he argues jurisdiction and hands
  over a file.
- **Deliberate reordering, declared:** the source runs Tsunade's "I hope I won't regret giving him the
  same rights as you" first and Jiraiya's "let him think he can do as he pleases" afterward
  (ch18:378–388). Page 18 panel 8 reverses the two so the chapter closes on Tsunade's line, which is
  the ending beat `VOLUME_06.md` specifies. No content is added, removed or changed.
- Nothing here tells any character about the medicine, the poison, Zetsu, Oto, Guren, Kurama, the Edo
  Tensei, Minato's soul, the Hiraishin, or the Akatsuki leader's identity. Naruto's refusal to name
  the leader is on page and explicit.
- No caption box narrates an event. The three cards state place and elapsed time only.

## Visual and continuity QA

- **Eyes.** Plain blue from p1 panel 3 to p2 panel 4; **ordinary three-tomoe Sharingan** from p2 panel
  5 to p18. The Eternal Mangekyō appears **nowhere** in this chapter — it was switched off at the end
  of Chapter 4 and does not return until Chapter 7. Naruto in the flashback (pp. 5–6) also carries the
  ordinary three-tomoe.
- **The medicine.** One dose, on page, p2 panels 3–4. The bottle starts at the halfway line Chapter 4
  left it at and ends at about a third; the two numbers are stated in his own thoughts on p2 B1–B2.
  **This is the volume's second and last on-page dose** and no further dose may be shown or implied
  for the rest of Volume 6.
- **Dress state is the chapter's clock.** Plain black, bare-headed, unarmoured on pp. 1–3 panel 5;
  **full armour with the forehead protector** from p3 panel 6 to the end. In the flashback (p4 panel 7
  to p6 panel 7) he is plain black **without** the forehead protector, which is how the reader dates
  it. The **Gunbai stays on the wall rack** in p2 panel 6 and p3 panel 6 and is never carried — do not
  put it on his back.
- **Chapter 4's damage is gone.** He has bathed off-page overnight; no burns, no blood, no torn shirt
  anywhere in this chapter. That is source-correct and deliberate.
- **Borders.** Ordinary borders in the present. **Torn ragged** on every panel from p4 panel 7 through
  p6 panel 7 (the Sannin summons). **Soft-edged** on p7 panels 4 and 6 (Tsunade's and Jiraiya's
  reports). The three border treatments must be visibly different from each other, and p6 panel 8's
  return to an ordinary border is the scene change and must not be missed.
- **The two report insets are staged by implication.** Danzō is under a sheet with the wrong silhouette
  and no wound is drawn; the Root hall is a wood field with shapes under it, blood on walls, and no
  identifiable body. Chapter 4 already showed the reader everything; these are recollections by people
  who were not there.
- **Black-flame Shunshin appears three times** — p3 panel 7, p4 panel 2, p5 panel 2 — plus its
  aftermath on p18 panel 8. Every time, the flame originates at **his own feet and the ground or floor
  beneath them**, rises around him, and leaves a scorched disc. If it appears from a hand, from the
  eyes, or from empty air, the page fails. **Amaterasu is not used in this chapter and nothing is set
  on fire.**
- **No jutsu is performed by anyone else.** Tsunade's raised chakra (p6 panel 1) and Naruto's (p6 panel
  3) are pressure effects only — no seals, no named technique, no attack. The ANBU's departure on p1
  panel 6 is a body flicker with no flame.
- **The office's spatial layout is fixed** from p4 to p18: desk and Tsunade at frame left, window
  behind her, door at the far left, Naruto entering and then seated at frame right, Jiraiya moving
  between them. Off-panel balloons for Naruto exit the **right** edge except on p18 panel 6, where he
  has crossed to the left edge and the tail is specified accordingly.
- **Sasuke's Sharingan is not active at any point** and he has **no Mangekyō** — he does not get one in
  this volume. He carries the Police Force insignia and nothing of Naruto's.
- **Danzō appears only inside p7 panel 4** and never in the present. Kakashi, Karin, Yugao, Zetsu,
  Itachi, Tenzō, Orochimaru and the Sandaime are named and none of them is drawn, in the present or in
  any inset.
- **All in-panel writing is ILLEGIBLE SCRIBBLE:** the medicine label (p2 panels 3–4), the office
  paperwork (pp. 4–18), the hospital chart and lamps' markings (p7 panel 4), and every page of
  Sasuke's files (pp. 15–17). The only deliberate lettering is the chapter marker and the three cards.
- **Pages 6 and 18 are deliberately eight-panel pages**, both because a flashback has to close inside
  its own page and because the chapter's final page carries an exit and a two-hander coda. Every other
  page is six or seven panels.
- Naruto leaves the office armoured, with the travel permit granted, the Sannin title refused, a debt
  of village secrets owed, and Sasuke due at the council at one o'clock. Chapter 6 inherits all four,
  and the armour is the first thing Yugao remarks on.
