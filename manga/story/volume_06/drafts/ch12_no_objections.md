## Source effect

A room full of adults spends eight pages trading a sixteen-year-old's marriage for herbs, mission
percentages and bloodline arithmetic, and he sits through all of it without saying a word. That is
the chapter. The reader should feel the temperature of it: Homura wonders aloud what kind of monster
the two of them would produce, Koharu switches from shrieking to enthusiasm the moment the
arithmetic favours her, and nobody in the chamber asks Naruto anything until the terms are settled.
When he is finally asked, he gives four words, and they are not a concession — they are the sound of
a man who has already worked out what the alliance is worth to him and let the council do the
haggling for free.

Then the second half moves the same transaction to a room where he is in charge, and shows the reader
what he was actually buying. Konoha refused to help Kiri recover, so he supplies the help himself from
a village Konoha does not know he owns, using a woman who now owes him twice. The famous beat is the
order of two lines: he **neither admits nor denies** owning Oto, and only after Mei has answered her
own question does he confirm that Konoha does not know. He never says yes. She proceeds as though he
had, which is the entire trick, and he tells her precisely why she will keep it: gratitude and desire,
in that order, named to her face.

And the chapter ends with him refusing her too. Not coldly — he simply schedules her after the
paperwork. **She is engaged, unringed, and told to wait**, and the last image of the volume's most
powerful woman is her leaning across a dead man's desk getting the same treatment as a council.

## Final dialogue and executable `name`

Reading order is right-to-left within each page. Coordinates use page width `x=0–100` from left to
right and page height `y=0–100` from top to bottom. Panel 1 is always the top-right panel; within a
row, a lower-numbered panel is always further right. Balloon numbers restart on every page and are
final lettering order; within a panel the first-read balloon sits further right or higher. Every
balloon record gives placement and a visible tail target or explicitly states `OFF`/tail-less.

Integrated ImageGen lettering is intended. During production, each generated page must be compared at
full size with the exact page/panel/speaker order below; any missing, duplicated, misspelled, moved,
or misattributed line fails the page and requires editing or regeneration.

**Global geometry locks — these prevent tail inversion and must not be varied.**

1. **The council chamber (pp. 1–10) uses Chapter 8's lock unchanged.** Tsunade sits at the head of the
   horseshoe and is at **frame left** in every shot down the table. Koharu and Homura are on the
   near-left arc. Shikaku, Tsume, Hiashi, Inoichi and Shibi are on the far arc, in that order from
   frame left to frame right. **Naruto is seated alone on the near-right arc and is the rightmost
   figure in every chamber panel he appears in.** Nobody changes seat at any point.
2. **Mei stands at the open end of the horseshoe, right of centre, facing frame left toward Tsunade,
   in every panel of the chamber sequence.** She is therefore always frame-left of Naruto and
   frame-right of everyone else, which is exactly her position in the argument.
3. **The Uchiha house (p. 11).** Naruto enters through the door at **frame right**. Mei is seated at
   frame centre-left; Sasuke stands beyond her at **frame left**, nearest the corridor he leaves by.
4. **Minato's library (pp. 12–16).** The desk runs across the frame. **Naruto sits behind it at frame
   right; Mei sits in the chair in front of it at frame left**, in every panel of the scene. The
   camera never crosses the desk.
5. **Naruto is the rightmost principal figure in every panel of the chapter, without exception.** Any
   balloon of his that must exit a panel exits the **right** edge.

**Standing visual locks for this chapter.** Naruto's irises carry the **Eternal Mangekyō** on every
panel of every page; the ordinary three-tomoe appears **nowhere**. The heavy right bang covers his
right eye and only the left is visible, except in page 1 panel 4, which is a deliberate straight-on
close where the pattern must be unmistakable. He wears plain black with **no forehead protector, no
armour, no Gunbai and no weapon** throughout. Mei is in full Mizukage robes from page 1 to page 16.
**No medicine bottle appears. Nothing is set on fire, cut, or grown.** Every written surface — the
council's papers, the alliance draft, the library's books and shelf labels, the scrolls on Minato's
desk — is ILLEGIBLE SCRIBBLE, never readable words. The only deliberate lettering is the chapter
marker and the three cards.

**Border locks.** Present-tense panels have ordinary borders. **Page 16 panels 2 and 4 are imagined
plates** and carry a distinct hard-edged **inverted border — a heavy black frame with a thin white
inner rule** — used nowhere else in Volume 6 so far, so the reader cannot mistake them for memory.
Nothing else in this chapter is a flashback, memory, or inset.
