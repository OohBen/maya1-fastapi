## Source effect

Naruto takes his father's house apart with no ceremony whatsoever, and the reader watches him refuse
every emotional offer the scene makes — the dust, the untended hero's home, the abandoned library.
He wants four things out of it and gets them: a kunai, the Hiraishin, the Rasengan, and the private,
never-spoken admission that the man was a genius. Everything he does with the take is industrial —
two hundred clones, a week's estimate, a training regime dawn to dusk. Meanwhile, in two rooms
elsewhere, the consequences of Volume 5 start moving: Ōnoki begins a file on him, and Danzō makes
the one move nobody sane makes. The chapter's end effect is that Naruto has spent the whole chapter
seizing things — and then something is taken from him.

## Continuity in

- The day after the date. Naruto has not seen Yugao since.
- Naruto has taken his medicine off-page this morning, so his **ordinary three-tomoe Sharingan is
  active** from page 1. It is not the Mangekyō and not the EMS until page 16 panel 4. The two
  **on-page** doses of medicine belong to Chapters 4 and 5 and must not be pre-empted here.
- Naruto wears plain black, no forehead protector, no armour, no Gunbai, no sword.
- Karin lives in Naruto's old apartment, is under his protection, has agreed to train, has read most
  of a beginner's fuinjutsu text, and has never been inside the Uchiha compound.
- Minato's house has been sealed roughly seventeen years and is keyed to Naruto's blood.
- Orochimaru is dead. Kabuto survived Naruto's attack, is unconscious, and is in Iwa's custody — a
  fact Ōnoki has kept to a handful of people.
- Guren is rebuilding Oto under Naruto's undisclosed direction.
- Sasuke has the Hokage's documents and is choosing Police Force recruits.
- Tsunade has not spoken to Naruto since he was made clan head.
- Danzō, Koharu and Homura regard Naruto as an escalating threat and Danzō has an undisclosed plan.
- Naruto still cannot travel long distances quickly; Zetsu does that for him.

## Final dialogue and executable `name`

Reading order is right-to-left within each page. Coordinates use page width `x=0–100` from left to
right and page height `y=0–100` from top to bottom. Panel 1 is always the top-right panel. Balloon
numbers restart on every page and are final lettering order. Every balloon record gives placement and
a visible tail target or explicitly states `OFF`/tail-less.

Integrated ImageGen lettering is intended. During production, each generated page must be compared at
full size with the exact page/panel/speaker order below; any missing, duplicated, misspelled, moved,
or misattributed line fails the page and requires editing or regeneration.

**Standing visual locks for this chapter:** Naruto's eyes are the **ordinary three-tomoe Sharingan**,
red with three tomoe, in every panel from page 1 to page 16 panel 3. The Eternal Mangekyō appears for
the first time on page 16 panel 4 and holds to the end. Every written surface in the house — scrolls,
notes, book covers, seal plates, clipboard lists — is ILLEGIBLE SCRIBBLE. The Hiraishin is never
used by anyone at any point.
