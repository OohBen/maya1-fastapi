**Zetsu is not an Akatsuki member in this adaptation** and must not appear at the assembly. The ring
is exactly ten: the Leader, Konan, Itachi, Kisame, Sasori, Deidara, Hidan, Kakuzu, Tobi and Kabuto.

## Source effect

Three rooms, and in each of them somebody says his name out loud to somebody who did not have it
before.

Sasuke says it as a colleague: the cover held, he did not have to lie once, and in four days a
massacre has become the founding case of a legal institution. Karin says it as a person he has
damaged into ambition — she was taken, she could not stop it, and the thing she wants now is to be
made into a weapon, which he agrees to without a flicker and then argues her out of the philosophy
underneath it. And a masked man in a circle of holograms says it as a warning, twice: once to eight
of the most dangerous people alive, and once, privately, to the only one of them who could actually
do something about it.

The chapter's last beat is the one that has to land hardest and quietest. Yugao thanks him for a
rescue he did not perform. Zetsu wore his face into that cell; she will never learn otherwise in
this volume. She holds him, she thanks him, she pushes his bang aside and kisses his cheek, and he
gives her **nothing** — and then she says four words that rearrange his evening and walks off before
he can ask what they mean. The reader should feel the two halves of him in the same doorway: the man
Akatsuki has just flagged as a threat, standing perfectly still because a woman put her arms around
him.

## Continuity in

- Continuous with Chapter 5. It is late morning of the same day; the council sits at one o'clock and
  Sasuke is due there with the files.
- Naruto is in **full armour with his forehead protector on**, restored in Chapter 5. The Gunbai
  stays at home and is not carried.
- His eyes are the **ordinary three-tomoe Sharingan**, from the second and last on-page dose of the
  volume in Chapter 5 page 2. No further dose is shown or implied in Volume 6.
- Sasuke was briefed and given the evidence the previous night; he covered for Naruto knowingly and
  willingly, and this chapter is where that is confirmed on page.
- Karin was taken by Root and rescued by **Zetsu wearing Naruto's form.** She thanks Naruto directly.
  She has never seen Zetsu.
- Yugao was taken and rescued the same way and believes Naruto did it. **She must not learn
  otherwise in this volume.**
- Between Chapter 5 and this chapter, off-page, Naruto has used the Shinigami mask and taken Minato's
  soul out of the death god's stomach. He did not let the man speak and has stored him in a coffin.
  **The retrieval is never dramatised** — the coffin is all the reader gets.
- Guren has nearly finished the Crystal Palace. Konoha does not know Naruto controls Oto.
- Danzō is alive, crippled and condemned; his execution is never depicted anywhere in this volume.
- Orochimaru's poison is undiagnosed. Nobody but Naruto and Zetsu knows anything about it.

## Final dialogue and executable `name`

Reading order is right-to-left within each page. Coordinates use page width `x=0–100` from left to
right and page height `y=0–100` from top to bottom. Panel 1 is always the top-right panel; within a
row, a lower-numbered panel is always further right. Balloon numbers restart on every page and are
final lettering order; within a panel the first-read balloon sits further right or higher. Every
balloon record gives placement and a visible tail target or explicitly states `OFF`/tail-less.

Integrated ImageGen lettering is intended. During production, each generated page must be compared at
full size with the exact page/panel/speaker order below; any missing, duplicated, misspelled, moved,
or misattributed line fails the page and requires editing or regeneration.

**Standing visual locks for this chapter.** Naruto is in **full armour with the forehead protector
on** in every panel, with the **ordinary three-tomoe Sharingan** in both eyes; no Eternal Mangekyō
anywhere, and no Gunbai. The ten Akatsuki figures on pages 7–10 are **holographic images** —
semi-transparent, lit from inside, casting no shadow, with the floor visible through their feet — and
nothing may suggest they share a physical room. **Tobi's mask has one eyehole, on the right, with a
three-tomoe Sharingan behind it**; the mask has no mouth opening, so his balloon tails point to the
lower edge of the mask, the same convention this volume used for Root in Chapter 4. Every written
surface — the coffin's seal, Sasuke's files, the seal ring on the floor — is ILLEGIBLE SCRIBBLE,
never readable words. The only deliberate lettering is the chapter marker and the four cards.
