## Source effect

Two rooms, and in both of them a woman is being handled by a man who is better at it than she knows.

In the kitchen, Yugao gets further with Naruto than anyone has: he tells her he finds her presence
pleasing, he calls her a good cook, he tells her there is nobody more suitable to be his mate, and he
tells her she holds the keys. He also shows her, without warning and without apology, the single most
alienating memory he owns, watches her come apart over it, and then reads her fear of rejection back
to her face. The chapter must not resolve into romance. It resolves into a woman deciding that this
is what she wants and saying nothing, because saying anything would spend it. Her silence at the end
is a choice, and the reader has to be able to see that it is one.

In Oto, the same technique is being run by somebody else. Guren has built a village people are happy
in, and has done it by killing challengers in front of a crowd while a thing that eats people thins
the rest out at night. Zetsu tells her to smile at the Tsuchikage's granddaughter and reveal nothing,
and she does it perfectly. The chapter ends on that smile and on the word "everything," and the
reader knows exactly what it is worth even though Kurotsuchi does not.

## Continuity in

- The same evening as Chapter 8. Naruto has come in from the training field; his clones are still
  working outside.
- **The ordinary three-tomoe Sharingan is active in both eyes for the entire Konoha half** and does not
  switch off, including while he casts the genjutsu — the balloon on page 8 names it "SHARINGAN:
  GENJUTSU" and it is the ordinary eye that casts it. The Eternal Mangekyō was switched off at ch17:779

  ever visible except in the direct eye close-up on page 8.
- Naruto arrives in the armour and the forehead protector and removes both on page 4 panel 5.
- **Yugao believes Naruto rescued her from Danzō.** It was Zetsu wearing his form. She never learns
  otherwise and nothing in this chapter may hint at it.
- Yugao has been to the compound before and let herself in. Neither of them treats that as strange.
- The brothel visit is a real memory from Naruto's past and is already known to Mei, who reacted to
  it. Yugao does not know it exists until this chapter.
- Naruto cleaned Yugao while she was unconscious months ago; both of them remember it and neither is
  embarrassed by it.
- "You have not answered my question" refers to the question he put to her before Volume 6 opened.
  Neither of them restates it and the reader is not told what it was.
- Sasuke has gone out with Kakashi and is not in the house.
- Oto has been rebuilding for months under Guren, publicly, and under Naruto's undisclosed direction.
  Zetsu has never used Naruto's name there. Karin has not moved there yet.
- Ōnoki lost Kabuto out of a guarded cell and sent Kurotsuchi to Oto under a negotiating cover in
  Chapter 3. Kurotsuchi's arrival here is the delivery of that decision.

## Final dialogue and executable `name`

Reading order is right-to-left within each page. Coordinates use page width `x=0–100` from left to
right and page height `y=0–100` from top to bottom. Panel 1 is always the top-right panel and within
a row a lower-numbered panel is always further right. Within a panel the first-read balloon sits
further right or higher. Balloon numbers restart on every page and are final lettering order. Every
balloon record gives placement and a visible tail target or explicitly states `OFF`/tail-less.

Integrated ImageGen lettering is intended. During production, each generated page must be compared at
full size with the exact page/panel/speaker order below; any missing, duplicated, misspelled, moved,
or misattributed line fails the page and requires editing or regeneration.

**Standing visual locks for this chapter.** The **ordinary three-tomoe Sharingan is active in both of
Naruto's eyes in every panel of pages 1–12** and the Mangekyō appears nowhere, with the right eye
behind the bang. The kitchen's layout is fixed
from page 1 and never rearranges: **the doorway and corridor at frame right, the range and sink at
frame left, the table between them with Naruto's chair on the right side and Yugao's on the left.**
The Crystal Palace office's layout is fixed from page 13: **the window wall at frame left, the desk
at frame centre, the door at frame right.**

**Genjutsu-memory convention.** Pages 9 and 10 panels 1–6 are inside the illusion. Every illusion
panel carries a border whose outer edge dissolves into fine concentric ring distortion, and a palette
one step desaturated from the kitchen's. The present-day Naruto and Yugao stand **inside** those
panels as observers and are drawn at their current ages in their current clothes; the remembered
figures are separate. Page 10 panel 7 returns to the hard kitchen border and the change is the
reader's cue that it is over.

**Content lock for pages 9–10 — this is the series bible's content-staging rule and it is not
negotiable.** The sequence is: the building, the entrance, a slap, a stair, a door closing, a
corridor, the same door open on an unlit room, and Yugao's face. **Nothing is drawn beyond the closed
door. No figure is drawn on the bed. No nudity, no bodies, no contact of any kind is depicted at any
point.** The two young women are seen once, clothed, from behind, on a stair. If any panel shows more
than that, the page fails and no regeneration may reintroduce it.

  page 6 onward. The second place setting appears on page 4 panel 4 and is used from page 11 panel 6.
- **Three distinct panel borders are in use and must not be confused:** the ordinary hard border for
  present tense; the **ring-dissolved** border for the genjutsu on pages 9 and 10 panels 1–6; and the
  **torn ragged** border for the three Oto memory insets on page 13 panel 3 and page 14 panels 2–3.
- The genjutsu has one origin, one path and one contact and they are all on page 8 panel 6: the eye,
  the outward rings, and Yugao off-panel. **No hand seal is performed** — the source casts it on eye
  contact alone. The illusion is ended by a finger snap on page 10 panel 7 and by nothing else.
- **The content lock on pages 9–10 is absolute.** The furthest the sequence goes is a closing door,
  burned-down lamps, and a foot of a bed with two pairs of sandals beside it. No figure on the bed, no
  nudity, no contact, no partial figure, at any point, in any regeneration.
- Guren's crystal blade appears once, on page 14 panel 2, lowered and clean, with two covered shapes
  on the ground. No wound, no blood, no face on either shape.
- **Zetsu is never drawn with legs**, is never seen by Kurotsuchi or by the aide, and is out of the
  room before the knock on page 16 panel 5. The crystal floor closes without a seam behind him.
- **Naruto does not appear on pages 13–16 in any form** — not in a panel, not in an inset, not
  reflected, not as a silhouette. He is referred to only as "he" and "his."
- All in-panel writing is **ILLEGIBLE SCRIBBLE**: the brothel's street signage and lanterns (p9),
  Guren's reports and the struck-through name column (p14), and any Oto shopfront in the establishing
  panel. The only deliberate lettering is the chapter marker and the two cards.
- Light and time run in three blocks: warm low interior evening for pages 1–12 with no exterior shot
  at all; cool desaturated night inside the illusion; flat high daylight for Oto on pages 13–16. The
  Oto half is two days later and in another country, and nothing carries over from the kitchen but
  the reader's memory of it.
- No balloon covers a face, an eye, the Sharingan on page 8 panel 6, the closed door on page
  10 panel 4, or Guren's smile on page 16 panel 8.
