## Source effect

The prose narrates most of this in summary — "when the bloody affair was completed" — and gives the
reader Zetsu's front-row seat instead of the fight. That is a rendering choice, not a story choice,
and the story underneath it is enormous: a man who told Kakashi two chapters ago that he has no need
for friendship destroys an entire military organisation and dismantles its commander piece by piece
because two people he does not admit to caring about were taken. He does it with the armour
deliberately left at home so that he will not enjoy it too much. He does it while his own body bills
him for every technique — the left eye bleeding far past anything Amaterasu explains, half a bottle
of medicine drunk standing up in the middle of a hall with a hundred and fifty blades pointed at
him. And the punishment is not a killing. It is an inventory: the eye, the arm, the leg, taken in
that order and named out loud before each one, because Danzō loved power and Konoha and Naruto
intends him to live without either.

The reader should leave the chapter with two facts held together and no comment on either. First:
this is the most frightening he has ever been, and he was never once angry. Second: the very next
thing he asks for, standing in his own house covered in other men's blood, is his father.

## Final dialogue and executable `name`

Reading order is right-to-left within each page. Coordinates use page width `x=0–100` from left to
right and page height `y=0–100` from top to bottom. Panel 1 is always the top-right panel; within a
row, a lower-numbered panel is always further right. Balloon numbers restart on every page and are
final lettering order; within a panel the first-read balloon sits further right or higher. Every
balloon record gives placement and a visible tail target or explicitly states `OFF`/tail-less.

Integrated ImageGen lettering is intended. During production, each generated page must be compared at
full size with the exact page/panel/speaker order below; any missing, duplicated, misspelled, moved,
or misattributed line fails the page and requires editing or regeneration.

**Standing visual locks for this chapter.** The **Eternal Mangekyō** is open in both eyes from page 1
to page 24 panel 1; the irises are plain blue from page 24 panel 2 to the end. From page 2 panel 5
onward the **left eye bleeds** and the blood is never wiped until page 24 panel 2. Naruto wears plain
black with **no armour, no Gunbai, no forehead protector and no weapon of his own**; the only blade he
holds is the tantō picked up on page 15 panel 5 from a dead Root, and it stays in his right hand
until page 22 panel 1. Every written surface — seal tags, base signage, the medicine bottle's label —
is ILLEGIBLE SCRIBBLE, never readable words. The only deliberate lettering is the chapter marker and
the two cards. **Screams are rendered as jagged SFX, never as balloons**, and every killing is staged
by implication — silhouette, blood on a wall, a shape under wood — never as an open wound.

**Technique locks.** Every jutsu effect names origin, path and contact inside a single panel:
Amaterasu leaves **Naruto's left eye**, crosses visible ground, and ignites a named target; Mokuton
grows out of **Naruto's own body** at a stated point; Susano'o forms **around him from his own body**
and is used only to receive an attack. Nothing ignites from a hand, a palm, or empty air.
