# Volume 6, Chapter 14 — *I Do Like You*

**Source:** ch21:113–219 **and** ch21:261–352. The chapter begins at the "Uchiha compound" heading
(ch21:113), minutes after Chapter 13 page 12, and runs to ch21:351 with Yugao walking away from the
kitchen. The Iwagakure block that sits between those two ranges in the prose (ch21:221–259) was played
at the end of Chapter 13 — see that draft's "Deliberate reordering, declared". The Oto arrival at
ch21:353 opens Chapter 15 and no part of it is played here.

**Length:** **14 pages** — two above the 12-page planning estimate in `VOLUME_06.md` — seven spreads,
94 panels, 102 balloons plus one chapter marker, five location/time cards, and three SFX units.

**Why 14 and not 12.** The chapter carries four complete scenes, not two: Karin's relocation is
settled here, Mei is refused here, Yugao is answered here, and Yugao is left here. At twelve pages one
of them had to become a transition, and the only candidate was the garden — which is the chapter's
half of the volume engine (his last private room is entered, and he wins a stated mate by telling her
to wait). The extra two pages go to the garden and to the morning; the Karin scene is held to two and
the night to four. No scene was expanded to fill a total, and no scene was compressed to protect one.

## Source effect

Three women in one day, and the chapter is about the different price of each.

Karin costs him nothing and he settles her future in two pages of flat coaching, which is the closest
thing to care he is capable of showing on purpose. Mei costs him a conversation he does not want to
have, in the one part of his own house he uses for nothing, and he ends it by telling the Mizukage of
Kirigakure to wait — and then leaves without answering her last question, which is the only accurate
one anybody asks him all day. And Yugao costs him the last private room he has. She walks into a
bedroom she has never been shown, says the thing she would not say in Kiri, and he answers her with
the least romantic sentence available: he already told her she was acceptable and he does not throw
things away. She stays the night. Nothing happens. It is the most intimate thing in the volume and it
consists of two people lying on their backs in the dark, not touching, while the only light in the
room comes out of his eyes.

Then he is gone before she wakes, and he does not leave a word — only an object. The reader should
finish the chapter understanding that he did not run away from her and that it does not matter,
because from where she is standing it looks exactly the same.

## Final dialogue and executable `name`

Reading order is right-to-left within each page. Coordinates use page width `x=0–100` from left to
right and page height `y=0–100` from top to bottom. Panel 1 is always the top-right panel; within a
row, a lower-numbered panel is always further right. Balloon numbers restart on every page and are
final lettering order; within a panel the first-read balloon sits further right or higher. Every
balloon record gives placement and a visible tail target or explicitly states `OFF`/tail-less.

Integrated ImageGen lettering is intended. During production, each generated page must be compared at
full size with the exact page/panel/speaker order below; any missing, duplicated, misspelled, moved,
or misattributed line fails the page and requires editing or regeneration.

**Standing visual locks for this chapter.** The **Eternal Mangekyō is open in both of Naruto's eyes in
every panel he appears in**, including every panel in the dark room and including while he is lying
down. Dress state is the chapter's clock: **full armour and forehead protector on pages 1–8**;
**shirtless and bare-headed on pages 9–12**; **absent from page 13 onward.** The **Gunbai and the
armour hang on the bedroom wall rack in page 9 and are gone from it on page 13** — that swap is a
plot point and must survive. The **forehead protector is on the nightstand from page 9 panel 2 to the
last panel of the chapter and never moves.** Every written surface — Karin's explosive tag, the
clones' scrolls, anything on a wall — is ILLEGIBLE SCRIBBLE, never readable words. The only deliberate
lettering is the chapter marker and the four cards.

**The light lock, pages 9–12.** In the unlit bedroom **the only light source in the frame is Naruto's
two eyes.** No lamp, no candle, no moonlight through the shutter, no ambient fill. Faces, hands and
the edge of the bed are lit red from those two points and everything else falls to black. His right
eye's glow comes **through** the right-side bang rather than round it, per ch21:277. This is the image
Chapter 15's final page pays off, and if the room is lit by anything else the payoff dies.

**Scene geometry locks — four scenes, four fixed axes, and they never invert.**

1. **Training ground (pp. 1–3):** Naruto **frame right** facing left; Karin **frame left** facing
   right; the clone blocks work in the far background; the house and the approach path are at frame
   left.
2. **Garden (pp. 3–8):** Naruto **frame right** facing left or facing forward; Mei **frame left**
   facing right; the stream runs across the bottom of frame between them and the reader.
3. **Bedroom, night (pp. 9–12 panel 7):** Naruto **frame left** on the near edge of the bed, facing
   right; the **door is at frame right**; Yugao enters at frame right and crosses **leftward** toward
   him.
4. **Bedroom, lying down (p. 12 panel 8) and morning (p. 13):** the camera **crosses to the foot of
   the bed and stays there.** Naruto is **frame right** and Yugao **frame left** — which is what puts
   her on his right side as ch21:303 requires. **This is the only camera crossing in the chapter and
   it must be made in one clean cut on p12 panel 8, never mid-scene.**
5. **Kitchen (p. 14):** Sasuke **frame left** at the counter facing right; Yugao enters at **frame
   right** and stays right.

## Visual and continuity QA

- **Eyes.** The **Eternal Mangekyō is open in both irises in every panel Naruto appears in**, awake,
  asleep, indoors and in the dark. No three-tomoe, no plain blue, no deactivation. **He does not close
  his eyes anywhere in this chapter** — p12 panel 7 is explicit that he neither argues nor complies,
  and if he is drawn with his eyes shut the joke, the light source and the Chapter 15 payoff all fail
  at once.
- **The light lock, pages 9–12.** His eyes are the **only** light source in the frame. No lamp, no
  candle, no moon through the shutter, no ambient fill, no rim light from a hallway. If the room can
  be read without those two red points, the page fails. Morning light returns on page 13 and not
  before.
- **The medicine does not appear and must not.** The volume's two on-page doses were Chapters 4 and 5
  and `VOLUME_06.md`'s hard lock closes the subject. No bottle, no label, no reference.
- **Dress is the chapter's clock, and the swap is a plot point.** Armour and forehead protector on
  pages 1–8. Shirtless and bare-headed on pages 9–12. On **page 9 panel 2 the armour plates and the
  Gunbai are on the wall rack and the forehead protector is on the nightstand.** On **page 13 panel 6
  the rack is bare** and on **page 13 panel 5 and page 14 panel 9 the protector has not moved.** If
  the rack still holds the Gunbai on page 13, or the protector is missing, the chapter's ending is
  destroyed and Chapter 15's bare-headed Naruto becomes unexplained.
- **The single camera crossing is page 12 panel 8** and it must be one clean cut. Before it, Naruto is
  frame left and the door is frame right. From it onward — including all of page 13 — the camera is at
  the foot of the bed, Naruto is frame right and Yugao is frame left, which is his right side per
  ch21:303. No panel may sit between the two conventions.
- **Nothing physical happens beyond one brief kiss.** `VOLUME_06.md`'s hard lock states the night is
  **sleep only.** Page 12 panel 7 keeps Yugao's undressing entirely out of frame — one folded garment
  at the frame edge is the whole of it — and page 12 panel 8 shows two people on their backs with a
