## Source effect

Naruto gives away his deniability on purpose, in the most public room in Konoha, and it costs him
nothing because the deniability was never the asset. The asset is Sasuke standing up first. The
chapter's real event is that the Police Force is tested in open council — challenged on evidence,
on jurisdiction, on notification, on proportionality — and comes out of it with its authority
confirmed, one reasonable leash accepted, and the massacre entered into the record as its first
S-rank operation. Koharu screams for ANBU twice and nothing comes, and the reader should understand
that the silence is not Naruto's doing. It is Tsunade's.

Underneath that, three quieter things happen. Naruto says almost nothing all meeting, which Tsunade
notices and cannot read. Hiashi offers a daughter and gets a soldier taken instead of a wife. And
Naruto hands Sasuke a technique of his own invention along with a warning that it can kill him,
which is the closest thing to affection in the volume so far and is delivered as a safety notice.

## Continuity in

- The same day as Chapters 6 and 7, roughly an hour after Dragon Land. Naruto returned before noon
  exactly as he intended.
- Naruto is **wearing the armour and the forehead protector**; the **ordinary three-tomoe Sharingan is
  active in both eyes** and stays active. The Eternal Mangekyō does not appear — it was switched off at
  ch17:779 in Chapter 4 and is not switched on again until Chapter 11 (ch20:437). No medicine is drunk
  on page.
- The right bang covers his right eye. The visible eye is his left.
- Last night Naruto destroyed Root and crippled Danzō and did not erase anything linking him to it.
  Danzō is alive, unconscious, in hospital, with no right eye, no right arm and a ruined left leg.
- **Sasuke agreed off-page, last night, to claim the operation as the Police Force's,** and holds the
  evidence file Naruto supplied. This was settled in Chapter 6 and is not re-explained here.
- Naruto already met Tsunade privately about it (Chapter 5); the council does not know that, and
  Shikaku deduces it in this chapter.
- Zetsu, not Naruto, rescued Yugao and Karin. Yugao believes Naruto did it and thanked him this
  morning.
- Minato has been summoned, heard and released. Nobody in this room knows any part of that.
- Sasuke has been given the Police Force and its recruits; Naruto has 200 clones split between the
  Hiraishin formula and the Rasengan variants, and one clone is training Karin at Minato's house.
- Yugao told Naruto this morning that she would see him tonight. He does not know what she meant.

## Final dialogue and executable `name`

Reading order is right-to-left within each page. Coordinates use page width `x=0–100` from left to
right and page height `y=0–100` from top to bottom. Panel 1 is always the top-right panel and within
a row a lower-numbered panel is always further right. Within a panel the first-read balloon sits
further right or higher. Balloon numbers restart on every page and are final lettering order. Every
balloon record gives placement and a visible tail target or explicitly states `OFF`/tail-less.

Integrated ImageGen lettering is intended. During production, each generated page must be compared at
full size with the exact page/panel/speaker order below; any missing, duplicated, misspelled, moved,
or misattributed line fails the page and requires editing or regeneration.

**Standing visual locks for this chapter:** the **ordinary three-tomoe Sharingan is active in both of
Naruto's eyes** in every panel he appears in — no Mangekyō anywhere — and his right eye stays behind
the bang, so the visible eye is always his left. He wears the armour and the forehead protector on every page. The chamber's seating
is fixed from page 1 and never rearranges: **Tsunade at the head (frame left in every table shot),
the two elders on the near-left arc, the clan heads along the far arc, and Naruto and Sasuke together
on the near-right arc.** No ANBU is ever drawn inside the chamber.

## Dialogue QA

- 71 balloons, one chapter marker, two location cards and three SFX units across 12 pages. The marker
  is exactly "CHAPTER 8 — ARREST HIM"; the cards are exactly "KONOHA — COUNCIL CHAMBERS" (p1) and
  "UCHIHA COMPOUND — TRAINING GROUND" (p10). Balloon numbering restarts and stays contiguous on every
  page.
- Density varies by function: page 3 spends three of six panels wordless on reactions, and page 4 —
  the chapter's title beat — carries six balloons across eight panels of which four are silent
  architecture. Pages 5–9 are the dialogue-dense council block at six to seven balloons each. Page 12
  runs eight balloons but three of its panels are wordless technique.
- Preserved from `SOURCE_TRUTH.md`'s verbatim list: "ANBU! ARREST HIM!" (p4 B1), "I AM NOT DENYING
  THAT I SLAUGHTERED ROOT AND CRIPPLED DANZOU." / "BECAUSE I DID IT." (p3 B2–B3), "NEVER AGAIN
  ATTEMPT TO ORDER MY ANBU. ARE WE CLEAR?" (p4 B6) and "NARUTO-SAN." / "WHAT CAN I DO FOR YOU,
  HIASHI-SAN?" (p10 B1–B2).
- **Naruto speaks eight times in twelve pages and only three of those are in the chamber.** The
  chapter's silence is his and Tsunade names it in a thought balloon on page 9 rather than the script
  explaining it.
- The four council objections are given to four different people and none of them is a plot
  announcement: Koharu wants an arrest, Hiashi wants jurisdiction and disclosure, Homura wants the
  manpower, Shikaku wants a rule for next time. Only Shikaku gets what he asks for.
- Sasuke argues doctrine and never once says that Naruto planned the operation, which is the lie the
  whole scene rests on and which the reader knows from Chapter 6.
- Tsume's single line does the job of a narration box without being one; Chōza's single line
  establishes that the Mizukage has been expected for days.
- Nothing here mentions Kurama, Minato, the Edo Tensei, Dragon Land, the Hiraishin, Oto, Guren, the
  medicine or the illness. **Hiashi's private conversation is never dramatised** — the reader learns
  its content two pages later from Naruto, exactly as the source does it.
- No caption box narrates an event. The two cards state place only.

## Visual and continuity QA

- **The ordinary three-tomoe Sharingan is active in both of Naruto's eyes on every one of his panels**,
  with the right eye behind the bang so only the left is ever visible. **No Eternal Mangekyō anywhere in
  this chapter** — ch20:457 turns on the council being surprised to see the Mangekyō later, so it must
  not appear at this council. No plain blue.
- Naruto wears the **armour and the forehead protector** on every page, with no sword and no Gunbai.
  Chapter 9 page 4 panel 5 is the scene that removes them; nothing here may pre-empt it.
- **Sasuke's Sharingan is never activated in this chapter**, and page 12 panel 5 must show his irises
  black while he watches the seals — Naruto forbade it on page 11 and the panel is the proof.
- The chamber's geography is fixed from page 1 panel 1 and never rearranges: Tsunade at the head and
  at frame left in every shot down the table, the two elders on the near-left arc, the clan heads on
  the far arc, Naruto and Sasuke together on the near-right arc. Naruto does not change seat, and on
  page 9 panel 7 he is in the same chair in the same posture as on page 1.

  of the same empty doors and empty alcoves, and the repetition is the joke; if a figure appears in
  either, the page fails.
- Tsunade's killing intent on page 4 panel 8 is rendered as darkening grain in the air and lifting
  paper corners only — no aura shape, no chakra colour, no visible technique.
- Danzō does not appear. Root does not appear. The evidence is a folder of **ILLEGIBLE SCRIBBLE**
  with one legible line diagram of a forearm carrying ten dark circles — the only readable
  information anywhere in the file, and it is a drawing, not words.

  slowly (p11 p6) → white vapour leaving the skin itself, from the surface and nowhere else (p11 p7)

  in empty air (p12 p2) → rotation turns it into a dome (p12 p3) → carryover is a clean scorched ring

  from his mouth, or from nothing, the page fails.

  12 panel 5 — because the source has him demonstrate it twice. The second performance is not shown

  opened on page and its contents are never shown.
