## Source effect

Naruto keeps a promise, and the reader watches him pay for it in a currency he did not expect to
spend. Kurama gets his other half back and refuses to say thank you; the only warmth in the scene is
Naruto giving him his name, which is worth more than gratitude and which neither of them
acknowledges. Then the man who caused everything is stood up on a rock and allowed to speak, and the
chapter's real work happens in Naruto's face rather than in his mouth: the hatred that has organised
his life since he was seven does not survive contact with the actual account, and nothing replaces
it. He does not forgive Minato. He does not call him father. He also cannot hate him cleanly any
more, and both of those must be legible.

The two things he says that he has never said aloud — that he intends to be Rokudaime, and that
Minato will go to Kushina rather than back into the Shinigami — are gifts he gives without admitting
they are gifts. Minato's answer is the one thing in the chapter Naruto does not have a counter for,
and he says nothing at all. The reader must not be told what he felt.

The coda restates the cost as a joke Kurama does not find funny: the beast is denied his revenge
twice in three minutes, once on Minato and once on Obito, and the second refusal is not mercy. It is
ownership.

## Continuity in

- Continuous with Chapter 6. Naruto reverse-summoned out of his own bedroom seconds ago; Yugao has
  just kissed his cheek and told him she will see him tonight. It is still morning and he must be
  back in Konoha before noon for the council meeting, which is Chapter 8.
- Naruto is **wearing his armour and forehead protector**, no sword, no Gunbai. The lost V3 ninjato
  is still not replaced.
- **The Eternal Mangekyō is active in both eyes for the entire chapter** and does not switch off.
  The medicine was taken off-page; neither on-page dose belongs to this chapter.
- Minato's soul was retrieved from the Shinigami at the end of Chapter 6 and stored in a coffin
  **without being allowed to speak.** He has never heard a word from his son.
- Kurama is physically free, lives in Dragon Land, and is **half complete** — Naruto promised the
  other half in Volume 5 and has not delivered it until this chapter.
- Zetsu left ahead of Naruto and is already in Dragon Land. Naruto cannot sense him.
- Naruto has the Edo Tensei from Orochimaru's scrolls. He has used it once before, on Kushina, and
  intends never to use it again after today.
- Danzō is crippled and alive; Root is destroyed; Yugao and Karin were rescued by Zetsu wearing
  Naruto's form and Yugao believes Naruto did it.
- Naruto knows Obito is the masked man of the sealing night and intends to kill him personally. He
  learned this from Madara, not from Minato, and nothing in this chapter teaches it to him.

## Final dialogue and executable `name`

Reading order is right-to-left within each page. Coordinates use page width `x=0–100` from left to
right and page height `y=0–100` from top to bottom. Panel 1 is always the top-right panel and within
a row a lower-numbered panel is always further right. Within a panel the first-read balloon sits
further right or higher. Balloon numbers restart on every page and are final lettering order. Every
balloon record gives placement and a visible tail target or explicitly states `OFF`/tail-less.

Integrated ImageGen lettering is intended. During production, each generated page must be compared at
full size with the exact page/panel/speaker order below; any missing, duplicated, misspelled, moved,
or misattributed line fails the page and requires editing or regeneration.

**Standing visual locks for this chapter:** Naruto's eyes carry the **Eternal Mangekyō in both** on
every panel he appears in — no three-tomoe, no blue, no switching off. He wears the armour and the
forehead protector on every panel. Minato's Edo Tensei body keeps the cracked skin, the black sclera
and the kunai in the back of the skull in every present-tense panel, and has **none of those things**
inside a flashback plate. Zetsu is never drawn with legs.

**Flashback plate convention.** Pages 13–16 alternate PLATE panels (Minato's account) and PRESENT
panels (the bench). Every PLATE panel carries a soft grey-washed border with no hard black gutter
line; every PRESENT panel carries the ordinary hard border. Minato's narration inside a PLATE is a
**tail-less oval balloon whose speaker is Minato**; he is not drawn in those panels and the balloon
must never grow a tail toward anyone inside the plate. The last plate's border frays away to white on
page 16 panel 3, which is how the reader is told the account is over.

## Visual and continuity QA

- **The Eternal Mangekyō is active in both of Naruto's eyes on every one of his 63 panels.** No
  three-tomoe, no plain blue, no deactivation, and no bleeding — the eye damage of Chapter 4 is
  healed and the drug is not on page. Any panel that shows an ordinary Sharingan fails.
- Naruto wears the **armour and the forehead protector** in every panel, with no sword and no Gunbai.
  Chapter 9 is the scene that removes them; nothing here may pre-empt it.
- **Minato's Edo Tensei body is consistent in every PRESENT panel:** cracked ash-grey skin, black
  sclera with a pale iris, Hokage coat over the flak vest, and the kunai with its tag visibly seated
  in the base of the skull from page 6 panel 2 onward. It is **never** in a PLATE panel — inside the
  flashback he is alive, uncracked, ordinary-eyed and carries no kunai. If a plate shows cracked skin
  the page fails.
