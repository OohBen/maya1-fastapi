# Volume 6 — *Perfect Results*

**Verified source scope:** fic chapters 17–21.

**Status:** step 1 of preproduction only — source read and volume boundary. The source-truth ledger is
complete (`SOURCE_TRUTH.md`). **No dramatic scene scripts, no dialogue, no page/panel `name`, no
builders, no reference sheets, no manifests, no generated pages.** The chapter architecture below is a
plan to be scripted, not a storyboard.

**Title.** *Perfect Results* is Naruto's own recurring phrase in this range: he refuses interruption
because he cannot get "perfect results," he will not stop his clones until he can say "perfect," and
he is quietly furious that his own body no longer returns perfect results without a drug. The irony is
the volume. (Runner-up, kept on file: *Who Will You Be With?*, from Yugao's question in ch20:317.)

---

## Volume engine

> **Every chapter costs him control — and he answers each loss by seizing control of something bigger.**

Volume 5 was construction. Volume 6 is the invoice. Naruto opens it unable to activate his Sharingan
without medicine and closes it having been told, by the man he intends to murder, that the only person
who ever loved him never did. In between, every chapter takes something out of his hands and he
responds by taking hold of something larger:

| He loses | He seizes |
|---|---|
| His eyes without medicine | His father's house, library, Hiraishin formula and Rasengan notes |
| Kakashi's willingness to be refused politely | The right to be left alone, stated as a rule |
| Two people he claims not to care about | Root, Danzō's eye, arm, leg, future, and Shisui's eye |
| Deniability — he admits the massacre in open council | The Police Force as a legal instrument and Sasuke as its face |
| The hatred that organised his life since he was seven | Kurama whole, Minato closed out, and the word "Rokudaime" said aloud |
| His privacy — a woman is in his kitchen, then his bed | A stated mate, an alliance, and a Mizukage who will keep his largest secret |
| His body — the virus will take every bloodline he has | A scripted death and a Rinnegan resurrection |
| The one certainty he had about Madara | Nothing. Obito takes it and leaves. |

The emotional counterpoint is Yugao's question in ch20:317 — *who will you be with if you push
everyone away* — which he cannot answer, and which recurs as the two questions he starts putting to
Zetsu in ch21: am I worse than Madara, and would my mother be proud. **The source is explicit that
none of this changes his goals.** He gets fractionally warmer. He does not become someone else.

---

## Why the volume begins at fic chapter 17

Volume 5's last page is Yugao at lunch, telling Anko and Kurenai she is going out with Naruto tonight
and privately deciding to ask him about the red-haired girl (ch16:617–683). Fic ch17 opens the same
day and delivers that dinner and that question. Volume 6 starts on the morning of that day. **Nothing
between the volumes is skipped and nothing from ch16 is re-adapted.**

## Why the volume ends at fic chapter 21

Fic ch21 closes on the Valley of the End: Obito arrives and asks the five questions that dismantle
Naruto's entire account of his own childhood — why Madara abandoned his dream, why Obito left him
alive, why he never forced the dream on Naruto, why he waited until Obito had gone, why he let a boy
he knew of from birth suffer for six years — and concludes that a heartless man could not have loved
him. **The source refuses to show Naruto's reaction.** He walks to the waterfall and jumps. That is a
genuine turn: the volume's engine is loss of control, and the last thing taken from him is the story
he told himself about the one person who was kind to him.

It is also a clean structural seam. Fic ch22 opens a discrete three-chapter unit — the author states
outright that ch22–24 are one arc: Hidan and Kakuzu, then Itachi and Sasuke. Ch22 also carries the
first Katon: Rasengan in combat, the Kusanagi carried for the first time, and Sasuke's road to his
Mangekyō. **All of that belongs to Volume 7, which begins at ch22.**

The ROADMAP sketch of Volume 6 was verified against the prose and is **correct on its start and on
five of its seven items**, but incomplete and wrong in two places. See "Corrections to the ROADMAP
sketch" below.

## Expand / compress decisions

The rule from Volumes 2 and 3: dramatise what the prose dwells on **and** what has the strongest
visual material; montage what the prose summarises **and** what the character finds unremarkable. Where
those conflict, preserve the source's end effect and follow the character.

### Expand

| Sequence | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **The Root massacre and Danzō's punishment** | ch17:637–813 | The prose narrates most of the fight in summary — "the bloody affair was completed" — with Zetsu watching. That is a rendering choice, not a story choice: the outcome carries Danzō's total ruin, Naruto's escalating self-damage, and the first sustained look at Mokuton erupting from his own body. It needs the full causal loop from the fight guide: geography, the suicide squad, the Susano'o cost, the medicine drunk mid-fight, the eye bleeding past explanation, and the punishment delivered in named order — eye, arm, leg. 26 pages. This is the volume's set piece. |
| **The Kakashi conversation** | ch17:27–105 | Four volumes of refused approaches resolve here into one line. The prose gives it a page; the manga needs the pauses, the two men not looking at each other, and the beat where Kakashi's relief is cut off. |
| **The date** | ch17:149–331 | The whole engine in miniature: he loses an evening and finds it pleasant. Expand the walk and the doorstep; the restaurant meal itself compresses. |
| **The bench conversation with Minato** | ch19:77–171 | The prose is one uninterrupted monologue. Expand the *silence* around it — Naruto's face, Minato's failure to reach him, the refused embrace. |
| **Yugao's question at Ichiraku** | ch20:313–323 | Four lines of prose. It is the emotional spine of the volume and earns a full spread with the cold bowl. |
| **The two problems** | ch20:335–367 | A medical briefing in prose. Give the reader the geometry of the threat — which bloodline fails how, what it costs to use each one — because every later panel of Amaterasu, Mokuton and Susano'o in this series now carries a price. |
| **Obito at the Valley of the End** | ch21:471–499 | The last four pages of the volume. Five questions, one conclusion, one Kamui, one jump. Expand the wordless aftermath and give the reader nothing. |

| Sequence | Source | Why |

## Hard locks

- **The seam is exact.** Volume 6 opens on the day of the dinner Volume 5 anticipated. Do not restage
  or re-summarise any part of ch16.
- **Zetsu, not Naruto, rescued Yugao and Karin** — wearing Naruto's form. Yugao thanks Naruto for it.
  She never learns otherwise in this volume.
- **Exactly one Root survives**, with his memory of the night erased minutes later. He is the reason
  Danzō lives to be executed. He has seen Zetsu.
- **Naruto leaves his armour behind on purpose** before the Root attack, to keep himself from becoming
  excited and releasing too much power. He puts it back on the next morning and it restores his focus.
- **Armour and forehead protector travel together.** Two source lines constrain this: ch18:571 has
  Yugao remark that he is back in his armour, and ch19:373 has him return to the kitchen "without
  his armour and forehead protector." The only reading consistent with both is that the armour is
  worn *with* the protector and the plain black attire is worn without it. Chapters 6 and 7 were
  drafted independently and both arrived at this state, so it is settled: armoured means protector
  on; plain black means protector off. Chapter 9 page 4 panel 5 is the on-page changeover.
  This also resolves the council: ch20:455 has him "wearing black, as always" and states outright
  that he is not wearing the protector, so Chapter 12 is plain black with no protector. Chapter 5's
  closing QA note claiming he keeps the protector on for the rest of the volume is WRONG and has now
  been amended — the source overrules it. **The one deliberate exception in the volume is Chapter 14
  page 13**, where he takes the armour and the Gunbai off the wall rack and leaves the protector on the
  nightstand, which is why Chapter 15 is armoured and bare-headed. That break is the source's own
  staging (ch21:319, ch21:529) and is a plot point, not a continuity error.
- **His habitual eye is the ORDINARY three-tomoe Sharingan, not the Mangekyō.** ch20:457 is
  decisive: at the council "it was a surprise to see him with it activated, he always had the
  Sharingan activated not the Mangekyou," and Tsunade reflects that she has never seen his real
  eyes. So obtaining Shisui's eye in Chapter 4 gives him the Eternal Mangekyō but does NOT put it
  permanently on his face. Any draft asserting "EMS permanently active" is wrong — **Chapters 7, 8
  and 9 asserted it and have been corrected to the ordinary three-tomoe.** The correct
  state through the volume is: ordinary Sharingan active by default (Chapters 1 and 2 are the
  source-locked exception, where it will not activate at all),
  Mangekyō only where a page explicitly switches it on, switched on deliberately in Chapter 11
  and worn into the Chapter 12 council, where its appearance is the surprise the scene turns on.
  Chapter 11 therefore carries two eye states and needs both bound.
- **Danzō's injuries occur in this order:** right eye removed by hand, right arm severed and burned
  with Amaterasu (destroying Hashirama cells and ten Sharingan), left leg crushed bone by bone. He does
  not scream for the eye. He screams for the arm and the leg.
- **The medicine is drunk on page, twice** — before the Root fight and on waking after Oto. The
  dependency is the volume's opening image and must not quietly disappear.
- **Edo Tensei is used once.** Minato is released to Kushina, not returned to the Shinigami.
- **Naruto does not forgive Minato and never calls him father.** He also does not hate him cleanly any
  more. Both must survive.
- **Naruto refuses the Sannin title and accepts the rights that come with it.** Both.
- **Naruto does not save Gaara, and Suna survives anyway** — using the counter-intelligence he sent
  and deliberately withheld from Jiraiya.
- **Naruto neither confirms nor denies Oto to Mei** before confirming that Konoha does not know. The
  order of those two beats is the whole trick.
- **No wedding, no consummation, no Mei relationship progress.** She is engaged, unringed, and told to
  wait.
- **The night with Yugao is sleep only.**
- **Obito's questions get no answer, no thought balloon, and no caption.** The withholding is the beat.
- **None of this changes his goals.** The source says so directly. He becomes fractionally warmer and
  slightly more social; he does not become good, and he does not become kind.
- **The ch20:213–219 counter exchange is CUT, and the cut is confirmed.** Chapter 10's draft made the
  cut conditional on whether Chapter 9 staged ch19:429 aloud. It does: Chapter 9 page 12 balloons B8
  ("there is no one more suitable to be my mate…") and B9 ("you hold the keys. you have not answered
  my question."), both spoken with tails to a visible mouth. The standing game is therefore named on
  page once, in Chapter 9, and "how long are you going to play this game / you are proving to be quite
  a woman, Uzuki Yugao" is not restored in Chapter 10.

### Reconciliation decisions recorded here

1. **`env_hideout_throne` is a separate sheet.** `env_hideout_tablets.png` does not cover it. The
   room's load-bearing feature is the **empty Gedō Mazō socket with the chains hanging into nothing**,
   on page in seven panels of Chapter 11 and again in Chapter 13. Build it with the socket empty.
2. **Two Uchiha-house interiors, not one.** `env_uchiha_house_kitchen` (Chapters 2, 6, 9, 14) and
   `env_uchiha_house_sitting_room` (Chapter 12) are separate sheets. Chapter 9 locks the kitchen's
   range and sink as fixed frame-left furniture across twelve pages; Chapter 12 receives a foreign
   Kage at a low table with cups already poured and no kitchen fixtures in frame. One sheet would
   either put a stove behind the Mizukage or strip the kitchen of the fixtures three chapters depend
   on. They share the house's vocabulary — same wood tone, shoji and lamp — so the second is a cheap
   build, and it is the one of the two that can be staged around if the audit declines it.
3. **Chapters 3 and 15 share one `kamui_vortex` sheet** and now describe the vortex in identical
   terms. See the Tier 1 row.
4. **Chapter 10's conditional restore is resolved as a confirmed cut.** See "Hard locks".
