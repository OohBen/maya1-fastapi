**Locations:** academy classroom, village streets, Madara's hideout
**Job:** the volume's only ensemble chapter — and the world getting larger and worse at once.

9. **Final:** *"For the next year you will not go back to Konoha."* Blood clone takes his place (1p)

## Notes
This is the reader's only real look at the classmates before Volume 2, so make them legible and
distinct. It's also the chapter where Naruto's moral drift becomes visible: he hears that a man
murdered his own family and his reaction is *approval*. Play that flat, not triumphant.
Madara's satisfaction at the massacre should be genuinely unpleasant — first crack in his warmth.
