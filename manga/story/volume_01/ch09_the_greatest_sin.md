# Vol 1, Ch 9 — "The Greatest Sin"
**26 pages** (extra-length finale) · fic ch3 · **Cast:** Naruto (13), Madara, Zetsu, Kakashi, Sasuke, class
**Locations:** Naruto's apartment (night), hideout, burial ground, academy classroom
**Job:** the volume's payoff. Patricide, the Mangekyō, and the reveal.

## Beats
1. Night. The apartment. He is asleep (1p)
2. A figure in the room. One hand crushing his throat, the other **over his eyes** so he cannot see (3pp)
3. He can't breathe, can't scream, can't see. Hand under the pillow (2pp)
4. The kunai. Contact. The grip loosens (2pp)
5. He opens his eyes (1p)
6. *"G-grandfather—"* (1p, full page) (1p)
7. Madara on the floor, **smiling**. Last breath (2pp)
8. **The Mangekyō awakens** — full page. Ring at centre, six blades to the rim (1p)
9. Blackout. Zetsu carrying two bodies (1p)
10. Wakes at the hideout. *"Madara wanted to die by your hands."* (3pp)
11. The reason: the eyes only turn through loss. It was the last thing he had to give (2pp)
12. The burial. No tears — he is done with those (2pp)
13. Transition: three days later, the academy (1p)
14. **The reveal.** Black shirt, red swirl, hair to the shoulders, dead eyes. The room goes silent (2pp)
15. Sasuke: *"Dobe. What is with the change?"* (1p)
16. *"I didn't change. I was always like this — you were just too blind to see it."* (1p)
17. **Final page:** Kakashi's eye narrowing. Volume 2 hook (1p)

## Notes
Beat 2 is deliberately staged so the reader **cannot identify the attacker either** — the hand over
the eyes is doing double duty. Keep the figure in silhouette until beat 6.
The Mangekyō page (beat 8) is the single most important image in the volume. **High tier, budget
several re-rolls, and bind `mangekyo_design` as a hard reference** — the six-blade pattern is custom
to this fic and the model will otherwise invent a canon one.
The reveal (beat 14) should rhyme visually with ch3's first day at the academy — same room, same
doorway, same stares, inverted meaning. Consider matching the camera exactly.
Do not let him look at anyone on the final pages. He has stopped needing them.
