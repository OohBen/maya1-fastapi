# Vol 1, Ch 2 — "The Old Man in Black"
**20 pages** · fic ch1 (age 7 section) · **Cast:** Naruto (7), Madara, Zetsu, mob
**Locations:** Jōnin lounge, village streets, the alley, Naruto's apartment, unknown location
**Job:** the hook. Deliver the vanishing mob — the volume's first genuinely uncanny image.

## Beats
1. One year on. Naruto, 7, has learned to get "lost" in the Jōnin lounge and overhear things (3pp)
2. He learns the Kyūbi is sealed *inside* him — so he is its prisoner, not the thing itself (3pp)
3. He works out why the village stares. It changes nothing (2pp)
4. Dragged out of his apartment, thrown into the alley. Small mob tonight (3pp)
5. He closes his eyes and waits. **The pain doesn't come** (2pp)
6. He opens them. **The alley is empty.** (1p — full page)
7. Madara: black robes, red eyes, cane. *"Your savior."* (2pp)
8. Walks him home in silence. *"Don't tell your Hokage anything about me."* (2pp)
9. *"My name is… Madara."* Gone. (1p)
10. **Final:** elsewhere — Zetsu rises from the ground. *"Did you tell him?" "Not yet."* (1p)
