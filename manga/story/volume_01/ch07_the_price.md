# Vol 1, Ch 7 — "The Price"
**20 pages** · fic ch2 · **Cast:** Naruto (8–9), Madara, Zetsu, bandits
**Locations:** hideout (tablet room, training clearing), bandit camp in forest
**Job:** the thesis chapter. Hardest to stage in the volume — see notes.

## Beats
1. The Sage of Six Paths. Senju body, Uchiha eyes, the Rinnegan. **Mythic flashback plates** (4pp)
2. Madara's own history: Hashirama's cells, the self-experiments, the face in his chest (3pp)
3. The infusion. Body horror. Naruto goes under (2pp)
4. He wakes in a bandit camp. Zetsu is gone. **Twenty-five men** (2pp)
5. The fight. He is nine and losing (3pp)
6. Near death — **the Sharingan awakens** (1p, full page) (1p)
7. The aftermath. Zetsu finishes the rest. Naruto on his knees in it (2pp)
8. Guilt. He has killed. It was arranged (1p)
9. **Final:** Madara's cane across his back to stop the crying. *"Is this the price of power?"* (2pp)

## Notes — the hard chapter
Beats 1–2 are, in the source, a lecture at a table. That cannot be drawn as-is. Solution: render the
Sage myth as **full-bleed mythic plates** in a deliberately different visual register — flatter,
older, closer to woodblock or scroll-painting — intercut with small panels of Naruto's face
listening. The register shift is what makes it readable.

Violence staging: the bandit fight is a nine-year-old killing twenty-five adults. Shoot it in
fragments — a hand, a dropped weapon, blood on grass, his face — never a clean wide of the act.
The Sharingan awakening on beat 6 is the page everyone remembers. Give it the full page and light
it from the eye.
