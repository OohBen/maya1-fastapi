## Notes
The name-change on beat 5 is the quiet climax of the volume's first half — he chooses to become the
thing that was done to him. Give it two clean pages and no action.
Beat 9 must play as farewell **without either of them saying so**. Naruto half-registers it; the
reader fully registers it. That gap is the page-turn into ch9.
Two time skips in one chapter — use title cards and montage strips, don't fight it.
