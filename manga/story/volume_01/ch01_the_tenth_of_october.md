# Volume 1, Chapter 1 — "The Tenth of October"

**24 pages** (extra-length opener) · Source: fic ch1, opening through the hospital scene
**Cast:** Naruto (age 6), Hiruzen, Kakashi, mob (unnamed)
**Locations:** festival street (night), back alley (night), hospital room (day)
**Volume-1 job:** establish the world's cruelty as *ordinary*, and end on the boy deciding to stop
asking adults for the truth.

## Direction notes

The chapter has one trick and it must land: **the festival is genuinely beautiful.** Pages 1–2 should
be warm, crowded, lovely — the kind of page you'd want as a poster. The horror works only because the
reader liked the place first. Do not foreshadow. Let page 3 turn it.

Naruto **does not cry and does not speak** through the entire beating. That is the characterisation.
Every instinct will be to give him dialogue — don't. His silence is what unnerves the mob, and it's
what makes the "Kyūbi" line land on page 14.

**Violence staging:** implication only. The knife on p12 is the one hard image and it should be shot
as *his face plus the blood on the ground*, not the wound. p16 is silhouette against lantern light.
This is both a moderation necessity and better craft.
