# Volume 1 — *The Price of Power*

**Covers:** fic chapters 1–3, through the academy reveal.
**9 chapters · ~190 pages.**

**Arc:** a boy is given a family and made to destroy it.

It ends on the Mangekyō and the reveal, **not** the bell test — patricide followed by a training
exercise is an anticlimax. The bell test opens Volume 2.

**Escalation check** — each chapter must cost him something:
2 secrecy · 3 nothing (deliberate breather) · 4 the myth of his parents · 5 his childhood ·
6 a year of his life · 7 his innocence · 8 his name · 9 his grandfather.

Ch3 is the only chapter that takes nothing. That's why it's the warm one, and why it goes
immediately before the reveal that breaks him.
