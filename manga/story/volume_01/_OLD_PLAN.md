## The premise

Konoha's peace is a mask. Underneath, the village breeds hatred and passes it to its children like a disease. Every October 10th the villagers celebrate the Kyūbi's defeat by beating the six-year-old it was sealed into.

Naruto stops running from the mobs — not from courage, from arithmetic. They always catch him. Then one night the mob simply *vanishes*, and an old man in black with red eyes and a cane is standing in the alley.

He says his name is Madara. He is telling the truth. He is also Naruto's grandfather — Kushina was his daughter, born of an Uzumaki seal-master who hid the Uchiha blood in her. Over six years Madara feeds him, teaches him, infuses him with his own cells and Hashirama's, and rebuilds him into something that can carry both Uchiha eyes and Senju life force.

The cost structure is the point. The Sharingan awakens when Madara has Zetsu abandon him in a bandit camp and he kills twenty-five men to survive. The Mangekyō awakens the night Madara breaks into his bedroom, hands on his throat and over his eyes, and lets his grandson stab him in the neck — because a body that was dying anyway is worth more spent than kept.

Naruto's inheritance is an instruction: take Konoha back from the fools who run it, kill the man wearing Madara's name, and take the Rinnegan off his corpse.

**The line that is the whole book:** *"Is this the price of power?"*

## Volume 1: *The Price of Power*

**Coverage:** fic chapters 1–3, through the academy reveal.
**Shape:** 9 chapters, ~190 pages.
**Why it ends where it ends:** the bell test is an *anticlimax* after patricide. Volume 1 ends on the Mangekyō and the reveal; the bell test opens Volume 2.

The volume is one clean arc — *a boy is given a family and made to destroy it* — and it resolves. That's what makes it a volume rather than a chunk.

---

### Ch 1 — "The Tenth of October" · 24pp (extra-length opener)

Cold open on the festival: lanterns, warm light, laughing crowds. Then the alley behind it.

- Naruto, 6, stops running. Deliberately.
- The beating. The kitchen knife through his hand — he doesn't scream, and it's the silence that unsettles them.
- *"You're stubborn today, eh Kyūbi?"* — the word lands. He doesn't know what it means yet.
- Hospital, two days later. Eyes shut, only his ears working. Overhears Hiruzen and Kakashi: Jiraiya refused him. He has family. They didn't want him.
- Wakes. Asks Jiji why they hate him. Gets *"people hate what they don't understand."* Again.
- **Final beat:** he says nothing, and decides to find the truth himself.

*Splash candidates: the festival-to-alley reveal; the knife.*

---

### Ch 2 — "The Old Man in Black" · 20pp

- One year on. Age 7. Getting "lost" in the Jōnin lounge; learns the Kyūbi is sealed *inside* him — so he's its prisoner, not the thing itself.
- Dragged from his apartment into the alley. Small mob. He closes his eyes and waits.
- The pain doesn't come. **The mob is simply gone.**
- Madara: black robes, red eyes, cane. *"Your savior."*
- Walks him home in silence. *"Don't tell your Hokage anything about me."*
- *"My name is… Madara."* Then he isn't there.
- **Final beat:** Zetsu rises out of the ground elsewhere. *"Did you tell him?" "No, not yet. I need to gain his trust first."*

*This chapter is the hook. The vanishing mob is the volume's first real image.*

---

### Ch 3 — "Academy" · 20pp

- Hiruzen enrolls him. *"What is a shinobi?"* — the lesson Naruto will spend the volume inverting.
- First day: the parents' faces curdling as he walks past. Iruka's contempt. Thrown out of class in the first ten minutes.
- Playground. Two older boys. His lunch on the dirt.
- Home. Knock at the door — Madara, with grocery bags. Admits he broke in to check the cupboards.
- Offers books.
- **Final beat:** the first genuine smile in the volume.

*Tonal breather. Needed — chs 1–2 are relentless and ch 4 is a gut-punch.*

---

### Ch 4 — "Kushina" · 20pp

- Three months of dinners on Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays. Hokage Monument — he sits on Hashirama's head now, never the Yondaime's.
- Ichiraku. Ayame's hug, six free bowls, "make it twenty-six." The volume's warmest page.
- Apartment. *"I have questions that need to be answered. Who are you?"*
- *"I am that man."* — Naruto flatly does not believe him.
- Madara pivots: *"How about I tell you about your parents."*
- **Final beat:** *"Your mother was the jinchūriki of the Kyūbi before you. Her name was Kushina Uzumaki."*

---

### Ch 5 — "Grandfather" · 20pp

- *"Your father was Minato Namikaze, the Yondaime Hokage."* Rage — hot enough to pull Kyūbi chakra out unconsciously.
- The horror of the resemblance. *"You look a lot like him, have you noticed?"*
- *"I know all of this because I was Kushina's father. Your grandfather."*
- He cries. He'd promised never to again. He makes one exception.
- The hideout. *"Your childhood is over."*
- Weights. Fifty laps. Collapse; Zetsu carries him to bed.
- **Final beat:** Madara's grin — the one that promises pain.

---

### Ch 6 — "Traitors" · 20pp

- Academy interlude, and the volume's only ensemble pages: Shikamaru, Chōji, Hinata's stare, Sasuke's entrance with the fangirls behind him, Sakura and Ino.
- **The Uchiha massacre breaks.** Konoha in chaos.
- Madara: *"Those traitors got what they deserved."*
- The truth: it was a coup, and Itachi stopped a civil war. Naruto catches on fast.
- Zetsu formally introduced — *"my spy, my creation."*
- **Final beat:** *"For the next year you will not go back to Konoha."* Blood clone takes his place.

---

### Ch 7 — "The Price" · 20pp

- The Sage of Six Paths. Senju body, Uchiha eyes, the Rinnegan. Needs real visual invention — the source is a lecture.
- The infusion. Madara's cells and Hashirama's. Body horror; Naruto goes under.
- Wakes in a bandit camp. Zetsu is gone. Twenty-five men.
- The fight. Nearly dies. **Sharingan awakens.**
- Zetsu finishes the rest. Naruto is on his knees in the aftermath.
- **Final beat:** *"Is this the price of power?"*

*This is the volume's thesis chapter and the hardest to stage — see production risks.*

---

### Ch 8 — "Inheritance" · 20pp

- Two years on. Age 10, Sharingan matured, back on the monument sorting a clone's memories.
- The tablet room. Madara's full confession: Obito, the Akatsuki, Nagato and the transplanted Rinnegan, the man wearing his name.
- *"Take back everything that belongs to me."* Rule Konoha. Kill Tobi. Take the eyes.
- **He chooses the name.** *Uchiha Naruto.*
- Three years later — graduation, forehead protector, Team 7 assignment.
- The Gunbai, handed over. *"I am proud of you."*
- **Final beat:** *"Never forget what I taught you."* Naruto notices it sounds like goodbye.

*Two time skips in one chapter. Handle with montage; don't fight it.*

---

### Ch 9 — "The Greatest Sin" · 26pp (extra-length finale)

- Night. A figure in the bedroom. One hand crushing his throat, the other over his eyes so he can't see.
- Kunai from under the pillow. Contact. The grip loosens.
- He opens his eyes.
- *"G-grandfather!"*
- Madara hits the floor smiling, and stops breathing.
- **Mangekyō awakens** — full-page. Locked design: small black ring at center, six black blades radiating to the rim.
- Zetsu: *"Madara wanted to die by your hands. It was the greatest power he could leave you."*
- The burial. No tears — he's done with those.
- **The academy reveal.** Black long-sleeve, red Uzumaki swirl, hair to the shoulders, dead eyes. The room goes silent.
- Sasuke: *"Dobe, what is with the change?"*
- **Final page:** *"I did not change. I was always like this — you were just too blind to see it."* Then Kakashi's eye, narrowing.

## What this volume needs from the pipeline

### Reference pack (~30 images, not 9)

Nine was an invented number. The actual requirement, derived from the script:

**Naruto — four distinct ages, and this is the #1 consistency risk in the volume.** Every one needs full-body + face:
1. **Age 6** — short spiky blond, white short-sleeve with Uzumaki swirl, orange shorts, blue sandals
2. **Age 7** — orange jumpsuit, goggles on forehead, black sandals
3. **Age 10** — mid-growth, matured Sharingan, hair lengthening
4. **Age 13 "reveal"** — black long-sleeve, *large* red Uzumaki swirl on chest and small one on back, black shinobi pants/boots, black gloves with swirl on each hand, shoulder-length blond hair, two heavy bangs with the right one covering the eye, whisker marks faded, no expression

Page generation must select by age tag. Getting this wrong is what makes it look like 190 unrelated images.

**Other characters** (full-body + face each): Madara (old, black robes, cane, three-tomoe Sharingan), Hiruzen, Zetsu (white/black split, yellow eyes, black cloak), Kakashi, Sasuke, Sakura, Shikamaru, Chōji, Hinata, Iruka, Ayame + Teuchi, and Kushina/Minato for the reveal imagery.

**Environments** (generated empty, no characters): festival street at night with lanterns; the alley; Naruto's rundown apartment (kitchen + bedroom); academy classroom; academy grounds; Hokage's office; Hokage Monument overlook; Ichiraku; Madara's hideout (corridor, kitchen, training clearing); hospital room; bandit camp; the tablet room.

**Locked props** — these drift badly if unbound:
- **Mangekyō design** — ring + six blades. Custom to this fic. Must be a reference, not a prompt.
- **The Gunbai** — wide, tomoe, long handle, bandages at the base, chain, purple with black border.
- Madara's cane; the forehead protector.

### Budget — this is the real constraint

190 pages at medium with ~5 refs is **$0.10/page = $19 for one clean pass**, before any revision, plus ~$5 for a high-quality reference pack. Realistic finished volume with normal iteration: **$35–45**.

The key has **$10**. That does not cover Volume 1. It comfortably covers a **Chapter 1 pilot** — 24 pages plus the reference pack, ~$6–7 with revisions — which is the right first move anyway: it validates references, lettering, and drift control on real pages before committing to 190.

### Production risks specific to this material

1. **Four Naruto designs in one volume.** Age-tagged references, enforced by the selector. Highest drift risk here.
2. **Interiority.** Chs 1–2 and 7–8 are mostly internal monologue and exposition. The Sage of Six Paths lecture and the Akatsuki history dump are talking heads on the page. These need invented visual language — mythic flashback plates, the tablets themselves, symbolic panels — or they die.
3. **Time skips.** One year, three months, six months, two years, three years. Ch 8 carries two. Montage pages and title cards.
4. **Content.** The fic is rated M and Volume 1 contains a child beaten by a mob and stabbed through the hand, a child killing twenty-five men, and a child killing his grandfather. gpt-image-2 will likely refuse or soften direct depictions. Design these panels for implication now — silhouette, reaction shots, blood on the floor rather than the wound, the moment after instead of the moment. This is a staging discipline, not a blocker, and honestly it's how good manga shoots these beats anyway.
