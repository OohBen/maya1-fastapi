# Roadmap — the whole adaptation

The fic is **50 chapters**. Four volumes cover the first eleven, plus Volume 4 restores the
omitted ending of chapter 7. The earlier rate suggests an adaptation on the order of 13–15 volumes,
but that estimate is not a page target. Source events and manga-native scene construction determine
future volume length. This file exists so nobody has to re-derive the shape of it.

## How to plan the next volume

1. Read the fic chapter being adapted plus at least two complete chapters before and two after it.
   Do not plan from headings or summaries.
2. Build the source-truth sheets required by `MANGA_WRITING_GUIDE.md`. The fanfiction supplies plot,
   character state, and consequences; it does not supply manga scene length, dialogue rhythm,
   paneling, camera, or page turns.
3. Find the volume's **engine** — the one sentence describing what each chapter costs him or
   wins him. Every volume so far has one and it is what keeps the pacing decisions honest.
4. Decide expand/compress per sequence. The rule from V2/V3: dramatise what the prose dwells on
   *and* what has the strongest visual material; montage what the prose summarises *and* what
   the character finds unremarkable. When those conflict, preserve the source's end effect and
   follow the character rather than a historical page count.
5. Write the dramatic scene scripts with final dialogue. Every scene needs an objective, obstacle,
   tactic, turn, decision, consequence, and exact handoff unless the source intentionally omits one.
6. Pick each chapter-ending beat and build backwards into a rough manga `name` for every spread:
   panel geometry, balloon order, focal panel, and page-turn pressure.
7. Run a source comparison and a context-clean cold read on the scripts and `name`. The reader must
   understand the story without prose notes.
8. Produce the 8–12-page writing/storyboard pilot required by `MANGA_WRITING_GUIDE.md`; it must test
   conversation, tactical action, reaction, and a page turn before any builder or final art.
9. List new characters and environments, build reference sheets, and then run the separate visual
   probe before full production.

## Continuity ledger

Things established that later volumes must not contradict:

- Naruto's ninjato is **lost** (V3 ch2) and not replaced as of V3.
- The **Mangekyō is public knowledge** (V3 ch8) — a stadium and two kage saw it.
- **Susano'o is orange**, per the author's own note in fic ch9.
- **Zetsu's spore is on Danzō** (V2 ch4) and has never been removed.
- The **Sandaime is dead**; Naruto did not attend the funeral (fic ch8).
- Naruto **refused Jiraiya** (V3 ch6) and vanished from the village for three weeks.
- Naruto and **Gaara** have met twice and Gaara is fixated on him.
- Naruto defeated and released Gaara; **Gaara apologized to Temari and Kankuro**.
- **Kabuto** is known to Naruto as Orochimaru's spy and a former ROOT agent (V3 ch6).
- **Karin** is under Naruto's protection, knows his mother was an Uzumaki, and was promised eventual
  safety in Konoha.
- Volume 4 ends with Yagura down, Naruto depleted, and an **unexplained blue chakra column**.

Established by Volume 6 (fic ch17–21):

- **Naruto is sick.** Orochimaru's engineered poison now targets his Kekkei Genkai. He cannot activate
  his Sharingan without medicine; Amaterasu tears at his eyes, Susano'o crushes him and cannot be held,
  Mokuton costs more chakra than it should. The medicine is adapting against him and the stated
  outcome, unsolved, is the loss of every bloodline. **Not cured in V6.**
- **Naruto has scripted his own death.** Zetsu is under standing orders to kill him if necessary and to
  force Nagato to use Rinne Tensei to revive him. **Nagato must therefore be kept alive.**
- **Root does not exist.** Naruto killed the entire force. Exactly one member survived, with his memory
  of the night erased, to carry Danzō out.
- **Zetsu, wearing Naruto's form, rescued Yugao and Karin.** Yugao thanks Naruto for it and does not
  know Zetsu exists.
- **Danzō is finished** — right eye taken, right arm severed and burned with its Hashirama cells and
  ten Sharingan, left leg crushed — then condemned by Tsunade to a private execution the source never
  depicts. **Shisui's eye is at Naruto's hideout, unused.**
- **The massacre is legal.** Sasuke claimed it as the Police Force's first S-rank operation and won the
  council on Naruto's evidence. The Police Force must now notify the Hokage before such operations.
- **Kurama is whole**, lives free in Dragon Land, and works with Naruto without obeying him. Naruto
  knows and uses his name.
- **Minato has been summoned by Edo Tensei, heard in full, and released to Kushina** — not returned to
  the Shinigami. Naruto never calls him father and never forgives him, but can no longer hate him
  cleanly. Naruto stated aloud that he intends to be Rokudaime.
- **Naruto holds the Hiraishin formula but cannot use it.** He also holds Minato's house, its library,
  a three-pronged kunai, and the Rasengan notes, and has completed elemental Rasengan variants he has
  not tested on a person.
- **New techniques on the record:** the black-flame Shunshin, Mokuton erupting from his own body,
  the Amaterasu dome prison, *Uchiha Hono Shiurudo* (his own invention, taught to Sasuke), and
  *Uchiha Return* — the Gunbai reflecting an attack, demonstrated on Jiraiya.
- **Naruto refused the Sannin title** and took the Jiraiya-equivalent travel rights anyway. He owes
  Tsunade village secrets "in due time."
- **Konoha and Kiri are allied; Kiri and Oto are allied.** Naruto is engaged to Mei Terumi with no ring
  and no date; he told her to wait. **Mei knows he controls Oto. Konoha does not.**
- **Yugao is Naruto's girlfriend.** She has slept in his bed; nothing beyond that.
- **Otogakure is finished** and is Naruto's base of operations. Guren leads it publicly; Karin lives
  and works there; the Crystal Palace is Naruto's own design and effectively cannot be infiltrated.
- **Ōnoki is hunting him.** Kabuto was taken from Iwa's custody by Kamui in front of witnesses who saw
  a Sharingan; Kurotsuchi was sent to Oto and recalled; two Iwa ANBU were killed and dropped into the
  Tsuchikage's office. Ōnoki has suspicion and no proof, and will send nobody else to Oto.
- **Tsunade has publicly stated she trusts Naruto**; Jiraiya has decided to stop investigating him.
- **Akatsuki:** Tobi and Kabuto are members, paired on intelligence. Sasori and Deidara are dead —
  Sasori killed by Suna using intelligence Naruto withheld from Jiraiya, Deidara beheaded by Zetsu for
  the bounty. Shukaku is uncaptured; Gaara survived with a crushed right leg and a severed right hand.
  Tobi has named Naruto a threat and told Nagato to collect the Kyuubi personally.
- **Naruto chose not to save Gaara** and does not intend to save jinchūriki by default.
- **Obito has told Naruto that Madara never loved him.** Naruto gave no answer and the source shows
  none. Do not resolve this in a later volume without the source resolving it first.
- **Sasuke has no Mangekyō** as of ch22 and envies Naruto's. He commands 45 Police Force recruits and
  will be given Hanabi Hyūga as a future recruit — Naruto refused the marriage Hiashi offered.
