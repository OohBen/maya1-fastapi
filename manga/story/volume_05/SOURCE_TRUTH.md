## Chapter 12 — victory becomes leverage

### Immutable sequence

1. Danzō receives a verified report that Naruto has become much stronger than expected and calls for
   Koharu and Homura (ch12:7–19).
2. Two weeks after the battle, Kiri is celebrating, rebuilding, and has installed Mei as Godaime
   Mizukage (ch12:41–49).
3. Kiri calls Naruto its hero or savior; he dislikes the attention and denies acting for fame
   (ch12:49–61).
4. Naruto confirms Yagura's death means the Three-Tails will take at least several years to reform,
   delaying Akatsuki's ordered sealing plan (ch12:67–83).
5. Naruto plans to have Zetsu track Akatsuki and to intercept the first and second jinchūriki hunts so
   Obito must treat him as a threat, likely by sending Nagato (ch12:77–89).
6. Jiraiya's network has found Naruto. He is coming with Kakashi and Sasuke (ch12:93–103).
7. Danzō and the elders intend to use the Clan Restoration Act on Naruto and Sasuke; Naruto changes
   the subject when Zetsu asks about candidates (ch12:105–121).
8. Zetsu has located Orochimaru in Stone Country; Naruto will move after Jiraiya arrives (ch12:117–125).
9. Yugao cannot answer when Naruto directly asks whether she is interested in him. Naruto tells her
   she will return with Jiraiya, Kakashi, and Sasuke while he continues to Orochimaru (ch12:127–167).
10. Mei's work as Mizukage exhausts her. Naruto offers rare encouragement, then they walk through the
    rebuilding village and debate whether power creates a duty to pursue world peace (ch12:169–281).
11. Jiraiya, Kakashi, and Sasuke arrive. Naruto and Sasuke use Tsukuyomi for a private agreement to
    recover Uchiha property and rebuild the clan/compound (ch12:283–337).
12. Naruto refuses to return with Jiraiya, directs Kakashi to Yugao, and refuses to explain Kurama
    (ch12:339–395).
13. Flashback: about one year into training, Naruto offers Kurama physical freedom without Sharingan
    control. Kurama initially distrusts him (ch12:395–433).
14. Naruto and Zetsu weaken Minato's seal. Minato's chakra manifestation interrupts; Naruto dismisses
    it and completes the release (ch12:435–481).
15. Extraction nearly kills Naruto. Black Zetsu sustains his body; Kurama voluntarily leaves chakra
    behind. Days later Kurama still does not trust Naruto but agrees to work with him on equal terms
    (ch12:481–513).
16. Naruto leaves Jiraiya unanswered. Yugao returns to Konoha with the group. Mei stops Naruto's
    departure, kisses him, and he kisses her back briefly before leaving (ch12:515–555).

### Start and end effect

- **Start:** Naruto won a war but is physically depleted and politically unaccounted for.
- **End:** Kiri is stable, Sasuke is aligned with Naruto, the Kurama relationship is explained, and
  Naruto leaves for Orochimaru while two women have made their interest visible.

## Chapter 13 — Kushina's instruction

### Immutable sequence

1. Naruto dreams a recurring memory of a real chakra encounter with Kushina (ch13:5–15, 67–85).
2. Her embrace makes Naruto cry from happiness; he calls her Mother and behaves with unguarded warmth
   unlike his public self (ch13:7–29).
3. Kushina has watched his life and tells him about herself and the Uzumaki clan. Naruto learns her
   speech tic is the source of his childhood tic (ch13:31–39).
4. Kushina confirms Madara was her father, making her as much Uchiha by blood as Naruto while she
   remains proudly Uzumaki (ch13:41–43).
5. Kushina asks Naruto to enjoy life, smile, remain himself, and know that her love and pride do not
   depend on the path he chooses (ch13:45–63).
6. She asks him to understand Minato's kindness without demanding forgiveness (ch13:57–63).
7. Naruto wakes at midnight sweating and unable to return to sleep; he and Zetsu resume the journey
   (ch13:67–77).

### End effect

Kushina does not cure Naruto's detachment. She gives him a standard against which his later choices
about friendship, love, and happiness can be judged.

## Chapter 14 — the snake ends; Sound begins

### Immutable sequence

1. Ōnoki begins treating Naruto as an S-rank threat after Kiri. Naruto's resemblance to Madara and
   relation to Minato both concern him (ch14:9–35).
2. Naruto and Zetsu discuss removing the spore watching Danzō because total information makes Danzō
   predictable. Naruto knowingly chooses risk and says Danzō has not yet earned death (ch14:37–75).
3. They enter Orochimaru's Earth Country hideout. Zetsu searches for the Shinigami mask while Naruto
   goes to the throne room (ch14:77–103).
4. Orochimaru uses the Sound Four to occupy Naruto. Naruto identifies coordinated teamwork and tries
   to prevent their curse seals, but two reach transformed states (ch14:117–249).
5. Naruto kills the Sound Four. Kabuto is present but immobilized by genjutsu, not dead (ch14:167–265).
6. Orochimaru is severely weakened and never intended a fair fight. He poisons Naruto and tries to
   transfer into Naruto's body because Naruto is no longer a jinchūriki (ch14:251–327).
7. Naruto recognized Orochimaru's true form and placed him under a genjutsu that made him believe the
   transfer had succeeded. Naruto reveals the reversal and kills Orochimaru (ch14:329–371).
8. Zetsu finds the Shinigami mask. Naruto also takes Kusanagi and preserves the hideout's valuables
   (ch14:363–377).
9. Naruto decides Oto can become a real village and sends Zetsu to prepare it and survey Uzushio
   (ch14:377–405).
10. Jiraiya, Kakashi, Sasuke, and Yugao return to Konoha. Sasuke requests the confiscated Uchiha
    property; Yugao tells Kakashi that Naruto is lonely and still cares about him (ch14:407–501).
11. In Kusa, Naruto avoids unnecessary killings, notices imprisoned children, and recruits Guren
    through confrontation rather than mind control (ch14:503–651).
12. Guren accepts leadership of a rebuilt Oto as a new start. Naruto orders innocents freed, the
    hideout destroyed, and his own role kept secret (ch14:623–659).

### End effect

Orochimaru is dead, but Naruto inherits useful parts of his network and turns one of his victims and
subordinates toward construction. This is a power expansion, not an altruistic redemption arc.

## Chapter 15 — return and negotiation

### Immutable sequence

1. Iwa learns Naruto entered Earth Country and begins investigating (ch15:5–27).
2. Naruto returns to Konoha with Karin. He protects her abilities and identity, gives her his old
   house, and moves himself into the Uchiha compound (ch15:29–101).
3. Naruto sees the results of Sasuke and Kakashi's training. Kakashi asks to speak later (ch15:101–125).
4. Naruto tells Sasuke Orochimaru is dead and that the confiscated clan scrolls were probably copied
   before their return (ch15:129–193).
5. Tsunade and Jiraiya question Naruto about his disappearance, Orochimaru, Kiri, and Akatsuki. Naruto
   protects his private sources and Karin's healing ability (ch15:203–343).
6. Naruto says the still-unnamed Akatsuki leader defeated Hanzō and warns that the leader will kill
   Jiraiya in Ame. Jiraiya still intends to go (ch15:329–367).
7. Naruto requests travel rights equivalent to Jiraiya's and argues that he supplies intelligence
   Jiraiya cannot (ch15:369–399).
8. Naruto introduces Karin to Sasuke as someone she can trust. Their conversation reveals Naruto and
   Sasuke's new ease, but both admit it is recent (ch15:401–469).
9. Sasuke proposes restoring and commanding the Police Force. Naruto agrees to supply political help,
   intelligence, and training (ch15:473–501).
10. Naruto tells Karin she must learn fūinjutsu and taijutsu because he cannot always protect her.
    Karin agrees reluctantly (ch15:503–553).
11. Yugao visits Naruto and asks him to dinner. He accepts and smiles; their date remains in the next
    source chapter, outside this volume (ch15:555–607).
12. Danzō, Koharu, and Homura discuss Karin, the Uchiha scrolls, and Naruto as an unmanageable threat.
    Danzō says he already has a plan (ch15:609–639).

## Chapter 16 — institutional power

### Immutable sequence

1. Naruto is already treating an unexplained physical problem with medicine at the hideout. Zetsu
   reports that Guren has begun hiring contractors and expects Oto construction to start in about a
   week (ch16:7–57). Do not identify the poison's full effect before the next source chapter.
2. Naruto and Sasuke prepare for the Police Force before being summoned to council (ch16:59–111).
3. Naruto sits in the Uchiha seat and establishes himself as clan head. The advisers and Hokage do
   not have authority to select a clan head (ch16:113–171).
4. Naruto refuses to reveal where or how he trained and notes the matter was already settled with
   Tsunade (ch16:173–229).
5. The council proposes the Clan Restoration Act to preserve Sharingan. Naruto exposes the village's
   weapon logic and temporarily redirects the same logic toward the last Senju, Tsunade (ch16:233–289).
6. Naruto is under eighteen and cannot be forced into the act. Sasuke may marry before eighteen but
   rejects the council choosing his spouse or treating his children as weapons (ch16:291–307).
7. Danzō proposes a team containing Naruto, Sasuke, and Root. Naruto rejects the surveillance. Tsunade
   instead establishes an elite Naruto/Sasuke/Kakashi team for exceptional missions (ch16:309–369).
8. Sasuke proposes restoring the Police Force. Naruto and Sasuke overcome the advisers' objections by
   making it open to non-Uchiha recruits and subject to Hokage oversight (ch16:371–485).
9. Naruto claims part of the Uchiha and Minato inheritances for Police and Oto construction
   (ch16:487–499).
10. Naruto exposes the advisers' illegal copies of Uchiha scrolls and forces them to burn the copies;
    Tsunade retains the right to punish them (ch16:501–525).
11. Afterward, Naruto admits he deliberately displayed superiority and provoked the elders into more
    drastic decisions. He gives Sasuke Root intelligence for Police work (ch16:527–567).
12. Iwa finds Kabuto alive and the stripped hideout. Ōnoki orders further investigation
    (ch16:569–615).
13. Yugao tells Anko and Kurenai about her dinner with Naruto. This is anticipation, not the date
    itself (ch16:617–683).

### End effect

Naruto has translated personal strength into legal, clan, intelligence, and institutional power.
Danzō's already-prepared response remains unresolved, while the source closes on Yugao anticipating
her dinner and deciding to ask Naruto about Karin.
