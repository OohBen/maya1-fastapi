**Verified source scope:** fic chapters 12–16.

**Status:** writing preproduction complete. All 13 chapters have final dialogue and page/panel
`name`s; 232 pages and 1,327 planned panels passed independent chapter review, a context-clean
full-volume cold read, mechanical audit, and the ten-page storyboard pilot. No builders, reference

**Production contract:** integrated ImageGen lettering and effects remain the default. Every page is

## Volume engine

Winning in Kiri gave Naruto a reputation. This volume asks what he builds with it.

Each chapter turns destructive power into something that can last: freedom for Kurama, a working
bond with Sasuke, protection and a future for Karin, a successor state in Oto, a restored Uchiha
seat, and a new Police Force. The reader-facing tension is that every constructive act also expands
Naruto's private power beyond anybody's oversight.

The emotional counterpoint is Kushina's request that he live rather than merely complete objectives.
Naruto does not suddenly become warm. He begins allowing specific people to matter, then immediately
tries to organize those relationships as promises, duties, property, training, or institutions.

## Why the volume ends at fic chapter 16

Fic chapter 16 resolves the volume's construction arc: Naruto returns to Konoha, claims the Uchiha
seat, prevents the council from controlling the clan's future, and helps Sasuke restore the Police
Force. Danzō's undisclosed response was prepared earlier and remains open pressure. The source's
final beat is personal rather than political: Yugao anticipates dinner with Naruto and decides to ask
him directly about Karin.

Fic chapters 17–20 were read before fixing this boundary. They begin a different causal engine:
Yugao and Naruto test a relationship, Danzō retaliates against Yugao and Karin, Naruto destroys Root,
Sasuke legalizes the operation, Kurama becomes whole, Naruto confronts Minato, Akatsuki begins its
bijū hunt, and Mei proposes a political marriage. None of those outcomes may be pulled into Volume
5. They belong to the next volume.

## Volume handoff in

- Yagura is down and the full Three-Tails is gone from the battlefield.
- Naruto is on his knees, chakra-depleted, with his EMS inactive.
- An unexplained blue chakra column has just erupted from Yagura and vanished.
- Mei, Yugao, and the rebel force are running toward Naruto.
- Kiri's civil war has reached its decisive end, but the immediate human and political aftermath has
  not been shown.

## Volume handoff out

- Kiri is rebuilding under Godaime Mizukage Mei and regards Naruto as its savior.
- Yagura is dead; the Three-Tails will take years to reform, delaying Akatsuki's ordered sealing plan.
- Kurama is physically free but only half complete; Naruto has promised to restore the other half.
- Naruto and Sasuke recognize each other as partners in restoring the Uchiha clan.
- Orochimaru is dead, the Shinigami mask is secured, and Kabuto is alive but incapacitated.
- Guren is rebuilding Otogakure as a real village under Naruto's undisclosed direction.
- Karin lives under Naruto's protection in Konoha and has reluctantly agreed to train.
- Naruto is the acknowledged Uchiha clan head; Sasuke will head the restored Police Force.
- Naruto has travel rights under discussion but refuses the Sannin title as a measure of his worth.
- Danzō, Koharu, and Homura regard Naruto as an escalating threat and have begun planning against him.
- Yugao has asked Naruto to dinner; their first date has not happened yet.

## Hard locks

- The blue column is not explained in Volume 5. The source confirms only Yagura's death and the
  Three-Tails' delayed reformation.
- Naruto freed Kurama about one year into the time skip. The extraction nearly killed him; Black
  Zetsu temporarily sustained him; Kurama left chakra behind voluntarily.
- Minato's chakra manifestation appears during the seal break and Naruto dismisses it. The later
  Edo Tensei conversation with Minato does not occur until the next volume.
- Kushina's meeting is a recurring dream of a real chakra encounter. It changes Naruto's future
  choices without instantly changing his personality.
- Orochimaru's final counter is body theft, not a stronger conventional fight. Naruto defeats it by
  recognizing and replacing Orochimaru's perceived inner world with genjutsu.
- Orochimaru's poison has already entered Naruto even though the symptoms are subtle in this volume.
  Do not diagnose it before fic chapter 17.
- Kabuto is immobilized by genjutsu and survives the hideout. Do not show his death.
- Oto's reconstruction is an expansion of Naruto's power, not charity.
- Naruto returns with Karin, not Guren. Guren remains in Oto.
- Naruto and Sasuke are newly cooperative, not lifelong friends. Their ease with each other should
  surprise them without erasing old hostility.
- The council may pressure Naruto and Sasuke about heirs, but Naruto is under eighteen and refuses
  the Clan Restoration Act. No marriage is assigned in this volume.
- The restored Police Force is Sasuke's proposal and Sasuke's command. Naruto supplies political
  leverage, training, intelligence, and money.
- Volume 5 does not depict the first date, Danzō's kidnapping, Root's destruction, Kurama becoming
  whole, Minato's final conversation, Akatsuki's capture assignments, or Mei's marriage proposal.
