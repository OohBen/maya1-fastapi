# Volume 5, Chapter 11 — *Family*

**Source:** ch15:401–639 and ch16:7–111.

**Length:** 18 pages, nine spreads.

**Status:** final dialogue and first textual `name`; ready for a context-clean writing review, not for
builder or final art.

## Source effect

Naruto turns three recent attachments into structures he can control: Sasuke becomes the one person
Karin may trust, the two Uchiha divide clan and Police responsibilities, and Karin's protection
becomes an obligation to train. Their easy three-way argument briefly resembles a family before the
characters have language for it. Yugao then asks Naruto to dinner, making another attachment visible.

Danzō, Koharu, and Homura read the same developments as a consolidation of power and prepare a
response without revealing its method. The following morning, an unexplained physical problem shows
the private cost beneath Naruto's control while Oto and the Police plans keep moving. The chapter ends
with Naruto and Sasuke arriving together at the closed council doors, where those private plans will
become public power.

## Continuity in

- Naruto has returned to Konoha with Karin, given her his former secured house, and moved into the
  Uchiha compound with Sasuke. He is still wearing his red armour on the first evening.
- Naruto has just left Tsunade and Jiraiya after requesting independent travel rights, protecting
  Karin's healing ability, and warning Jiraiya away from Ame.
- Sasuke knows Orochimaru is dead and that the returned Uchiha scrolls were probably copied. He and
  Naruto agreed in Kiri to restore the clan, but their cooperation is only weeks old.
- Karin knows Naruto's mother was an Uzumaki. She is a sensor and healer but has never received
  proper shinobi training. Her identity and healing ability remain private.
- Yugao returned from Kiri with Jiraiya's group. She and Naruto have acknowledged an attraction only
  indirectly; they have not gone on a date.
- Orochimaru's last poison is already affecting Naruto. Neither he nor the reader may receive a full
  diagnosis in this chapter.
- Guren remains in Oto. Naruto returned with Karin, not Guren, and Oto is being rebuilt under
  Naruto's undisclosed direction.

## Dialogue QA

- Conversation density deliberately peaks in the three-person family argument and the Police plan,
  then drops for the medicine, movement, and final arrival. The chapter contains no expository
  narrator captions; the single time card only marks the evening transition.
- Naruto's lines organize trust, ownership, roles, protection, schedules, and space. His admission
  that he cannot always protect Karin is a limit, not warmth pasted onto his voice.
- Sasuke initiates the Police Force, claims its command, identifies the recruitment route, and takes
  the still-blank headquarters sheet. Naruto never absorbs Sasuke's institution into his own role or
  supplies the later funding answer early.
- Karin challenges the men's reproductive framing, tests their claimed friendship, objects to being
  moved without asking, and reluctantly accepts basic training. She is not a passive clan resource.
- “WE AREN'T,” “WE WERE CIVIL IN KIRI,” and “A FEW WEEKS AGO” prevent their ease from implying a
  lifelong friendship or retroactive Team 7 closeness.
- Yugao asks for dinner directly. Naruto accepts; no dinner, first date, or aftermath appears.
- Danzō's final line establishes preparation only. No target, method, abduction, Root attack, date,
  or outcome is named.
- Naruto's physical problem is shown as weakness relieved provisionally by medicine. No character
  calls it poison, identifies its mechanism, or predicts the next chapter's diagnosis.
