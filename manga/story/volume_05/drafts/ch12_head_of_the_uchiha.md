## Source effect

The source turns a council interrogation into Naruto's first exercise of formal Uchiha power. He does
not defeat the elders by intimidation alone: he identifies which authority belongs to the clan,
which belongs to the Hokage, and which the advisers have merely become accustomed to exercising.
When the council reframes future Uchiha children as strategic inventory, Naruto makes its rule touch
Tsunade, then uses the Act's present age and consent limits to refuse immediate enrollment. He does
not abolish the statute or prevent compulsion at eighteen. Sasuke distinguishes his own voluntary
plan to marry before eighteen from compulsory enrollment and the village's desire to own the result.
