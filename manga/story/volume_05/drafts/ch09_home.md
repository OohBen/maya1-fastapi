# Volume 5, Chapter 9 — *Home*

**Source:** ch15:5–201.

**Length:** 18 pages, nine spreads.

**Status:** final dialogue and first textual `name`; ready for a context-clean writing review, not for
builder or final art.

## Source effect

Naruto returns from expanding his power outside Konoha and immediately starts deciding what “home”
will mean inside it. He brings Karin through the gate without exposing what Orochimaru valued in her,
gives her his old house because he recognizes her unease, and places himself in the Uchiha compound.
The reunion with Sasuke is unexpectedly easy, but their restored clan property already carries proof
that the village treated Uchiha knowledge as its own. The chapter ends before Naruto can act on that
discovery: Tsunade summons him to account for his return.

## Continuity in

- Orochimaru and the Sound Four are dead. Kabuto is alive but immobilized and left behind.
- Naruto has the Shinigami mask and Kusanagi secured through Zetsu, but does not disclose either to
  Sasuke here.
- Guren remains outside Konoha to rebuild Oto. Zetsu is handling Oto and surveying Uzushio.
- Karin leaves Orochimaru's network with Naruto. She is under his protection, but she is uneasy around
  him and has not surrendered the right to decide what her new life becomes.
- Naruto wears his red armour, carries his gunbai and the distinct plain post-skip sash sword that
  left Kusa with him, and has long blond hair. The sword is not the lost Volume 3 ninjatō and is not
  Kusanagi. Orochimaru's poison is already in his body; only slight pallor is visible, and nobody
  diagnoses it.
- Sasuke and Kakashi have returned from training. Sasuke recovered the confiscated Uchiha property
  and scrolls after meeting Naruto in Kiri.

## Dialogue QA

- 123 spoken balloons, nine SFX, and three location cards across 18 pages. The count is high because
  this chapter carries four negotiations; the silent compound and spar panels keep the rhythm from
  becoming uniform.
- Dialogue density varies by function: the silent compound page and spar observation make room
  around denser boundary, reunion, and clan-information exchanges.
- Karin questions Naruto, names the first condition she needs, chooses whether to leave the ANBU's
  sight, and explicitly accepts the house only after Naruto says he will leave her to settle in.
  Protection never becomes gratitude-only passivity or an absolute future access rule.
- Naruto reassures badly, then changes tactic when Karin objects. At the gate he withholds her
  identity and ability; at the house he answers her boundary directly; with Sasuke he reveals only
  what partnership requires.
- Sasuke asks, reads Naruto's refusal, chooses not to press, notices his condition, and redirects to
  the clan. His ease with Naruto is new and slightly surprising, not rewritten as lifelong intimacy.
- Kakashi's request is distinct from Sasuke's reunion and remains unresolved. Ōnoki receives only the
  border report. No speaker gains future knowledge.
- Individual balloons remain compact. The densest pages distribute turns across moving bodies,
  hands, keys, shelves, and reaction panels instead of stacking speeches over static faces.
- Page 17's 11 balloons are allocated before art: three two-word object labels in the full-width P5,
  two balloons each in P3 and P7, and one in every other panel. None competes with a face or crosses
  a panel border.
