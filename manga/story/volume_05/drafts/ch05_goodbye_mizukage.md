# Volume 5, Chapter 5 — *Goodbye, Mizukage*

**Source:** ch12:517–555, with the travel-state handoff supported by ch13:67–75 and ch14:37–39.

**Length:** 10 pages, five spreads.

**Status:** final word-for-word dialogue and first textual `name`; ready for a context-clean writing
review, not for builder or final art.

## Source-truth sheet

### Spread 1 — pages 1–2: the place beside him

**Spread composition:** The empty space Naruto left behind dominates Page 1; the group's competing
interpretations tighten around Yugao on Page 2. The spread's focal panel is Page 2 panel 3, where
Yugao's battlefield explanation cannot fully conceal the personal concern Kakashi named.

#### Page 1 — 6 panels

**Purpose:** Preserve the immediate consequence of Naruto's disappearance and let Yugao make an
offer only after it can no longer be accepted.

1. Shallow top strip. Preserve Chapter 4 Page 18's blocking exactly: Jiraiya stands foreground at
   screen-right facing the empty screen-left where Naruto exited; Kakashi, Sasuke, and Yugao remain
   clustered behind Jiraiya at centre-right. The entire left half stays open. No one has crossed
   into Naruto's former position, and Sasuke has not moved left. Reserve a bounded rectangle in the
   upper-left sky/negative space; no figure, effect, balloon, or eye-line may enter it. **TITLE1**
   (tail-less, centred in the protected rectangle): **“CHAPTER 5 — GOODBYE, MIZUKAGE”**.
2. Upper-right vertical. Yugao turns from the empty space toward the others, shoulders squared but
   one hand still half-raised after Naruto. **B1** (upper right; tail down-left to Yugao):
   **Yugao:** “WHEN I SAID TO STOP TRYING TO FORCE HIM BACK TO KONOHA...”
3. Upper-left vertical. Close on Yugao's hand lowering. **B2** (upper left; tail down-right to
   Yugao, whose lower face remains in frame): **Yugao:** “I LEFT OUT THE PART WHERE SOMEONE SHOULD
   GO WITH HIM.”
4. Middle wide. Keep the same centre-right cluster behind Jiraiya. Sasuke shifts his eye-line toward
   Yugao without changing sides or moving into the open left half; Kakashi watches from behind her
   shoulder. **B3** (upper-centre/right above the right-side cluster; short downward/down-right tail

5. Lower-right square. Yugao meets Sasuke's eye rather than looking at Kakashi. **B4** (upper right;

   Jiraiya remain behind and to her right. **B5** (directly above Yugao; straight-down or down-left
   tail directly to her mouth, kept clear of her leftward eye-line and the open left half):

## Dialogue QA

- 19 spoken balloons, one time card, and one exact chapter marker across 10 pages. The marker reads
  “CHAPTER 5 — GOODBYE, MIZUKAGE”. Density falls deliberately from five and six balloons on the
  group exchange to two-balloon approach pages and then wordless kiss, travel, and dream-threshold
  pages.
- Every spoken balloon has a named speaker, final text, position, and tail target. Mei's first line
  is the only off-panel balloon and points to the right edge, never to Naruto.
- Yugao's dialogue expresses concern without supplying a confession she does not make in the
  source. Kakashi tests it, Sasuke challenges its practical logic, and Jiraiya ends the exchange.
- Mei's two questions change the interaction: the goodbye-kiss line closes physical distance, and
  the return question establishes that the kiss carries no promise.
- Naruto speaks only when an answer changes the scene. His final line preserves the source's formal
  address and returns Mei to the public role he is leaving behind.
- No thought balloon interprets Naruto's response to the kiss. The reader receives only the visible
  reciprocation, separation, smile, and resumed mission.
