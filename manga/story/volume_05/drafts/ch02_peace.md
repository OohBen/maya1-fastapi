# Volume 5, Chapter 2 — *Peace*

**Source:** ch12:127–281. The chapter begins exactly at the nighttime Yugao exchange and ends
before the ch12:283 arrival scene.

**Length:** 16 pages, eight spreads, 101 panels.

**Status:** revised final dialogue and executable textual `name`; ready for independent source and
context-clean writing review, not for builder or final art.

## Source effect

Yugao cannot name what she wants from Naruto, while Mei directly challenges what he believes power
obligates him to do. Naruto permits both conversations but refuses both women's premises: affection
does not automatically own him, and strength does not automatically make the world his duty. His
objection to imposed peace is made explicit: peace bought by taking everyone's choices is another
cage. The reader should nevertheless see that he stays in both conversations longer than his
philosophy requires.

## Continuity in

- Two weeks have passed since the Kiri battle.
- Naruto walks normally and wears repaired armour; he is not described as still recovering.
- Jiraiya, Kakashi, and Sasuke are several days away.
- Zetsu has left for the hideout on an uncertain timetable and remains part of Naruto's onward
  route; Naruto never claims that he will travel alone.
- Mei is the newly installed Godaime Mizukage and is overwhelmed by reconstruction work.
