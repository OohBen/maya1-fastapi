# Volume 5, Chapter 10 — *Permission*

**Source:** ch15:203–399.

**Length:** 18 pages, nine spreads.

**Status:** final dialogue and first textual `name`; ready for a context-clean writing review, not for
builder, image generation, or final art. Text and effects are planned for later integrated rendering.

## Source effect

Tsunade summons Naruto to reassert that a Konoha shinobi answers to the Hokage; Jiraiya wants the
truth his spy network could not obtain; Naruto wants his sources, Karin, and his movements outside
their control. He gives enough ground to make himself useful—Orochimaru is dead, Kiri may become an
ally, Akatsuki profiles will be supplied, and Ame is fatal—but refuses to make usefulness equal
obedience. Jiraiya rejects the Ame warning, and Naruto converts the same argument into a demand for
Jiraiya's travel rights while refusing to use the Sannin title as a measure of his worth. Tsunade
leaves the request under discussion, not granted.
