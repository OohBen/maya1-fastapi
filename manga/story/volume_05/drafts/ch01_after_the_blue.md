# Volume 5, Chapter 1 — *After the Blue*

**Source:** ch12:7–125. Pages 1–2 are a silent continuity bridge from the exact ch11:577–581
handoff; no post-ch12:125 event enters this chapter.

**Length:** 16 pages, eight spreads, 84 panels.

**Status:** revised final dialogue and executable textual `name`; ready for independent source and
context-clean writing review, not for builder or final art.

## Source effect

Naruto's victory closes Kiri's war but makes him visible to Danzō, Akatsuki, Jiraiya, and the world.
Two weeks later, Kiri calls him a hero while he privately converts Yagura's death into a delay
against Akatsuki. Jiraiya's approach brings Sasuke back into Naruto's plans; the Clan Restoration
Act makes clear that Konoha sees the two surviving Uchiha as a resource. Zetsu locates Orochimaru,
and Naruto orders his next objectives: meet Sasuke, kill Orochimaru, and recover Kurama's missing
half.

## Continuity in

- Naruto is kneeling and depleted; no EMS, Susano'o, or usable Kurama summon.
- Yagura lies down at the crater centre; the blue column has vanished.
- Mei and Yugao reach Naruto first; rebels remain behind them.
- Naruto is approximately sixteen, long-haired, and still wearing damaged red armour.
