# Volume 5, Chapter 4 — *The Open Cage*

**Source:** ch12:395–515.

**Length:** 18 pages, nine spreads.

**Status:** final dialogue and exact textual `name`; ready for a context-clean writing review, not for
builder, lettering cleanup, or final art.

## Source effect

Naruto offers the Nine-Tails physical freedom before he has the beast's trust and follows through without using
the Sharingan to control him. Breaking Minato's seal nearly kills Naruto: Minato's stored chakra
appears only when Naruto starts removing the paper seal, Naruto dismisses it, Zetsu completes the
extraction, and Black Zetsu temporarily sustains Naruto. The Nine-Tails is free to kill the helpless
Naruto but does not; he voluntarily leaves chakra behind and later agrees to answer Naruto's summon
by choice, without becoming a pet or compelled subordinate. The flashback is reader-only. Naruto's
present-day answer to Jiraiya gives the other characters none of this information.

## Continuity in

- Present day is two weeks after the Kiri battle. Naruto wears repaired red armour, carries his
  gunbai, and keeps the plain post-skip sash sword equipped at his left hip. This is not the lost
  Volume 3 ninjato. His ordinary three-tomoe Sharingan is active; his EMS is inactive.
- Jiraiya has just asked what an unspecified report about the Nine-Tails means; neither he nor the
  chapter states what the report contains. Chapter 3 ends with Naruto on reader-right facing Jiraiya
  on reader-left. Kakashi, Sasuke, and Yugao are present and have not been told how the Nine-Tails
  was freed.
- The flashback is about one year after Naruto left Konoha, before the Kiri campaign. Flashback Naruto
  is younger and not in the repaired Kiri armour.
- The Nine-Tails is Minato's sealed half only. The other half is not recovered in this volume.
- Inner-seal axis: Naruto remains reader-right, looking and moving left toward the gate; the Nine-Tails
  remains reader-left behind the bars, looking right. Do not flip this axis before the gate opens.
- Physical Dragon Land axis: Naruto begins centre-right; joined Zetsu kneels to his left and pulls
  the Nine-Tails' chakra farther left. The Nine-Tails materializes on the left. Movement toward freedom therefore
  travels right-to-left with the reading direction.
- Dragon Land's verified visual baseline is a Fire-Forest-like wilderness with active volcanoes
  (ch12:441). Basalt terraces, a sheltering fissure, and an ash-red sky are continuity inventions,
  not additional source facts.

## Lettering and effect rules

- Reading order is right-to-left and top-to-bottom. Positions below are reader-relative.
- Balloon numbers restart on each page and are final lettering order. All quoted dialogue, captions,
  title text, pain cries, and SFX are exact.
- Every standalone ordinary balloon has exactly one visible tail to the named speaker. Every
  connected balloon stack has exactly one shared visible tail for the whole stack; continuation
  balloons inherit that tail and never grow additional tails. Thought balloons and memory echoes
  have no tail. Off-panel tails enter from the specified border and never point to a visible
  non-speaker.
- Lettering and effects are generated as part of the art. Reserve the specified negative space; do
  not add a separate post-render letterer or substitute effect text.
- The Nine-Tails' speech uses the same lettering font as the cast, with a slightly rough balloon outline;
  it remains fully legible and is never rendered as decorative monster text.
- No eye-control effect appears at any point: no reflected Sharingan in the Nine-Tails' eyes, no genjutsu
  rings, and no visual tether from Naruto's gaze. The only tether is chakra physically pulled through
  the seal by Zetsu.

## Dialogue QA

- Final text totals 67 numbered spoken/pain-cry balloons, one numbered thought balloon, five
  captions, one title unit, 18 SFX, and one unnumbered memory-echo balloon across 18 pages. Density
  moves from negotiation, to sparse procedure and physical crisis, back to a short relationship
  exchange.
- Naruto speaks to change terms, assign causal roles, dismiss authority, request rather than compel,
  and accept the Nine-Tails' limits. He never narrates neutral lore to an informed listener.
- The Nine-Tails refuses twice in different ways: first he refuses a presumed control bargain; later he
  refuses trust while offering bounded cooperation. The second exchange therefore changes the
  relationship without erasing the first. “Come when I call” remains voluntary: later summons are
  the Nine-Tails choosing to honor these terms, not Naruto compelling a summon or owning a pet.
- Minato receives only the lines needed for his safeguard role. No forgiveness, Kushina discussion,
  parentage debate, Edo Tensei knowledge, or next-volume father-son material is introduced.
- “LEAVE SOME CHAKRA BEHIND” is a request made after the gate opens. The Nine-Tails gives no answer, Naruto
  turns his back, and the chapter reveals compliance only through Black Zetsu's observation.
- Jiraiya, Kakashi, Sasuke, and Yugao learn nothing from the flashback. It is reader-only framing,
  not a memory Naruto experiences in the present scene; only the original flashback participants
  heard its dialogue.

  and it must not be replaced by the lost Volume 3 ninjato.

  consistently leftward in physical space and with the page's reading direction.

  the Nine-Tails pushes -> Naruto starts removing paper -> unidentified hand stops him -> page 9

## Reported uncertainties

- **Verified baseline with invented continuity details:** ch12:441 describes Dragon Land as like the
  Fire Forest with active volcanoes. The recurring basalt terraces, sheltering fissure, and ash-red
  sky are medium-specific inventions used to keep the extraction geography readable.
- **Unverified design:** the source does not specify flashback Naruto's clothing at this point in the
  time skip. The dark training-layer choice distinguishes chronology but still needs an approved
  character reference before a builder exists.
- **Source wording ambiguity:** ch12:489 says the Nine-Tails' chakra is already in Naruto's pathways
  after Black Zetsu possesses him, while ch12:477 shows Naruto requested it. The scene resolves this
  by withholding the Nine-Tails' answer and showing the chakra as already present, preserving a voluntary act
