# Volume 5, Chapter 6 — *Mother*

**Source:** ch13:5–77. The source’s author note at ch13:85 is used only to verify that the dream
repeats a real chakra encounter; it is not adapted as dialogue or narration.

**Length:** 16 pages, eight spreads.

**Status:** final dialogue and first textual `name`; ready for a source-effect and context-clean
writing review, not for a builder, lettering pass, or final art.

## Source effect

Kushina reaches the part of Naruto that training, pride, and control cannot protect. Her embrace
makes him openly happy; her request gives him a measure of life beyond objectives without curing
his detachment. She does not ask him to excuse Minato. She asks him to understand that Minato was
kind, to remain himself, and to know that her love and pride are unconditional. Naruto wakes from
the recurring dream-memory at midnight, cannot return to sleep, and resumes the journey with the
same controlled public manner.

## Continuity in

- Chapter 5 ends after Mei kisses Naruto goodbye and he leaves Kiri. No additional romantic beat is
  inserted here.
- Current Naruto is approximately sixteen, with long blond hair. At the camp he sleeps in the black
  under-layer; repaired red armour, the plain sash sword, and gunbai remain within reach.
- Dream-memory Naruto uses the younger training design established for the Kurama-release flashback.
  He does not wear the current Kiri armour in the encounter.
- Kushina uses the established Volume 1 reveal design: very long red hair and the green dress with
  the pale high collar. She is a real chakra presence in the remembered event, not a metaphor,
  ghost invented by the dream, or ordinary flashback reconstruction.
- The opened inner seal space must visually match the aftermath of Chapter 4’s release scene. Faint
  broken seal-script supplies continuity; there is no intact cage and Kurama does not appear.

## Continuity out

- Naruto wakes at midnight sweating and confirms to Zetsu that this is the same recurring dream.
- He attempts to lie down again, remains awake, and accepts Zetsu’s suggestion that they continue
  the journey.
- His mission remains Orochimaru. He leaves with repaired red armour, the plain sash sword, and the
  gunbai; no equipment or injury state changes in the dream.
- Naruto has not forgiven Minato, adopted a cheerful manner, or resolved how to enjoy life. Kushina’s
  request remains private pressure that later choices may answer.
- Zetsu knows the dream recurs but receives no account of its content on-page.

## Lettering and visual grammar

- Reading order is right-to-left within every page. Panel numbers below are the required reading
  order; balloon numbers reset on each page and are the final lettering order.
- Dream balloons are ordinary white speech balloons with clean black outlines. Kushina’s balloons
  do not become ghostly or wavy when she fades; the encounter was real.
- The dream has shallow reflective water, warm red-gold seal light, and no hard exterior-world
  shadows. The waking camp uses cold moonlight, hard blacks, and dry ground. Page 15 must make the
  change of reality immediate without a caption.
- Balloons stay in the upper third of their panel unless a placement below is specified. No balloon
  may cover Kushina’s hands, Naruto’s tears, the chest-touch motif, or the fading edges of her body.
- Naruto smiles only inside the remembered encounter. Current Naruto does not smile on pages 15–16.

## Dialogue QA

- 80 speech balloons across 16 pages. Density ranges from zero balloons on the opening page to seven
  on the two identity-and-history pages; the disappearance and wake receive more visual space than
  the explanatory turns.
- Naruto’s dream voice is warmer but still concise. He asks, tests a literal definition, admits what
  he does not know, and refuses forgiveness. He never gives a sweeping promise that would require
  an immediate personality change.
- Kushina’s lines perform distinct actions: spare him a retelling, identify herself, tease, correct,
  request, limit that request, acknowledge harm, and give unconditional love. She does not exist
  only to praise him or excuse Minato.
- “LIVE, NARUTO,” “SMILE WHEN THE SMILE IS YOURS,” “REMAIN YOURSELF,” and “I CANNOT ASK YOU TO
  FORGIVE HIM” preserve the four separate parts of her instruction rather than collapsing them into
  generic encouragement.
- Page 11 prevents the request from becoming an order to act cheerful. Pages 15–16 confirm the
  distinction visually: current Naruto is impassive, unable to sleep, and still moving toward the
  mission.
- All balloons have named speakers, explicit tail targets, and fixed placement. No off-panel speaker
  is lettered with an on-panel tail.
