# Volume 5, Chapter 3 — *The Other Uchiha*

**Source:** ch12:283–394.

**Length:** 16 pages, eight spreads.

**Status:** final dialogue and first textual `name`; ready for a context-clean writing review, not for
builder, lettering test, or final art.

## Source effect

Naruto treats Sasuke's arrival as the beginning of an Uchiha project rather than a reunion. In a
private instant, the two Uchiha positioned to restore the clan accept that rebuilding requires
cooperation;
Naruto gives Sasuke the first concrete task, and their unexpected ease lets both of them imagine a
shared future without erasing their old distance. Back in public, Naruto refuses Konoha's order to
return. Yugao's testimony prevents Jiraiya from pretending rank or force will solve that conflict,
so Jiraiya changes the question from where Naruto is going to what he has done with Kurama.

## Dramatic scene script

| Scene | Want, resistance, tactic, turn, and inherited state |
|---|---|
| 1. The arrival | Naruto wants to assess Sasuke without submitting to Jiraiya's return agenda. The meeting is public and Jiraiya and Kakashi are already measuring what Naruto has become. Naruto ignores their opening banter, acknowledges Sasuke's growth, and moves the real conversation into Tsukuyomi. The public scene inherits only the visible change in Naruto's eyes. |
| 2. The private agreement | Naruto wants a working Uchiha partnership; Sasuke needs proof that Naruto means family as something practical rather than rhetorical. Naruto tests him with the direct cooperation question, then assigns recovery of the confiscated estate. Sasuke's concern about the council becomes shared amusement instead of rivalry. They leave the illusion committed to recovering the clan's property and rebuilding the compound together. |
| 3. The failed retrieval | Jiraiya wants Naruto to obey an order; Naruto rejects the premise that rank gives Jiraiya control over him. Yugao's arrival supplies a witness who has seen Naruto's power and spent a month learning his behavior. Jiraiya's force tactic collapses when Kakashi confirms her judgment. Jiraiya pivots to Kurama, and Naruto's silence creates the Chapter 4 handoff. |

  protector, the ordinary three-tomoe Sharingan active, the gunbai strapped to his back, and the
  plain straight post-skip sash sword at his left hip.

  old friends; their willingness to cooperate is new and should surprise them.

  carries no sword here.

  not established in this source range.

## Dialogue QA

- 74 dialogue balloons plus one time card across 16 pages; balloon numbering resets and remains
  contiguous on every page.
- Dialogue density varies by purpose: the arrival and final interrogation are sparse; the
  Tsukuyomi agreement, return dispute, and testimony carry more turns.

  history or explain Kurama to people who do not need the information.

  unanswered romantic feelings.
- No line gives Jiraiya, Kakashi, Sasuke, or Yugao the release method, Minato manifestation, Black

- “HIM” in Jiraiya's opening comparison remains unnamed, matching the source range rather than
