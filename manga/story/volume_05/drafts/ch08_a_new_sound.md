# Volume 5, Chapter 8 — *A New Sound*

**Source:** ch14:373–659.

**Length:** 20 pages, ten spreads. The chapter grows one spread beyond the working allocation so
Guren's hesitation, acceptance, operational orders, and first act do not compete for lettering
space.

**Status:** final dialogue and revised textual `name` after writing review; ready for follow-up
confirmation, not for a builder, model-integrated lettering/effects, or image generation.

## Source effect

Naruto does not merely kill Orochimaru; he keeps the useful infrastructure, people, and reach of
Sound and puts them behind a leader Konoha will not know he directs. The Konoha intercut shows the
other half of his expansion taking shape as Sasuke begins recovering Uchiha property and Yugao
pushes Kakashi to approach Naruto again. In Kusa, coercion gets Guren to listen but does not win her.
Naruto hands her a finished construction plan; she hesitates, then accepts the chance to lead Oto
and begin a new life. Only after her acceptance does Naruto give the operational instructions for
trusted personnel, innocent captives, valuables, dangerous experiments, Zetsu's construction
contact, and secrecy. Oto's strength expands his power; this is not a charitable mission or a new
negotiated division of authority.

## Dramatic scene script

| Scene | Pressure, tactic, turn, and inherited state |
|---|---|
| 1. Outside the Earth Country hideout | Zetsu expects another destroyed base; Naruto instead identifies the labs, records, personnel, and network as assets. Naruto identifies Guren and her Kusa role because Zetsu does not already know her. Zetsu tests whether Naruto means to rule openly. Naruto chooses a proxy structure: Guren will be offered visible leadership while his direction remains hidden. Zetsu leaves with exact stripping, Oto, and Uzushio tasks. |
| 2. Konoha return | The returning party must turn extraordinary events into official consequences. Yugao reports survival and Kiri; Sasuke immediately asks for the Uchiha estate and records. Tsunade agrees to confront the advisers, and Sasuke reserves the next matter for Naruto. |
| 3. Yugao and Kakashi | Kakashi seeks reassurance that Naruto is fine. Yugao refuses the low standard of mere survival, identifies Naruto's loneliness, and counters Kakashi's failed history with a direct instruction to try again. Kakashi leaves with permission to hope, not proof of reconciliation. |
| 4. Kusa approach | Naruto wants Guren without wasting the personnel he may inherit. Genjutsu replaces killing. Unaltered civilian captives occupy an evacuation wing; dangerous altered experiments remain behind a separate sealed barrier. The imprisoned children briefly interrupt his purely logistical reading of the base, but he continues toward the person who can convert it into Oto. |
| 5. Forced audience | Guren tries threat, physical control, and loyalty. Naruto uses Tsukuyomi to make her witness Orochimaru's death. She chooses revenge; his killing intent stops the immediate threat. Coercion produces attention, not agreement. |
| 6. The cost of loyalty | Naruto attacks Orochimaru's hold on Guren by naming Yukimaru's mother, Yukimaru, and the discarded vessel promise. Guren identifies his access to her guilt as another possible chain. Naruto refuses blackmail and states the lethal boundary only if she obstructs him. |
| 7. A new Sound | Naruto offers Guren leadership of a real village and admits the motive is hidden power. He hands her the construction scroll before she answers. Guren inspects it, hesitates, and accepts the opportunity to lead Oto and begin a new life. Her acceptance grants no new veto, exclusive chain of command, or personal relationship with Naruto. |
| 8. First instructions | After Guren accepts, Naruto orders her to take trusted personnel and innocent captives to Oto, let civilians with families leave, seal valuables, destroy the dangerous experiments with the hideout, work through his trusted friend Zetsu for instructions and construction connections, and keep Naruto's role secret. She learns Naruto's name, opens only the civilian cells, and keeps the dangerous experiment wing sealed for destruction. Her action closes Orochimaru's Sound and begins Naruto's. |

## Final dialogue and `name`

Reading order is right-to-left within each page. Unless a panel says otherwise, balloons are oval,
white, and placed in numbered order from upper right toward lower left. Every balloon below gives
speaker, tail target, and placement. No additional captions, internal monologue, or model-invented
lettering is permitted. Architectural plans and office papers contain diagrams or illegible marks,
never readable unscripted text.

**Attribution rule:** A speaking character's visible mouth is explicitly stated in the panel
description, and every direct tail targets that visible mouth. If the mouth is not visible, the
speaker is marked **(OFF)**; the balloon is tail-less unless its border-cut tail explicitly exits
toward the speaker's already-established off-panel position. No tail points to an eye, hand, memory
figure, or other proxy.
