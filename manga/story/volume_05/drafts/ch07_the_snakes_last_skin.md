# Volume 5, Chapter 7 — *The Snake's Last Skin*

**Source:** ch14:9–371, with continuity carried from ch13:67–77 and forward to ch14:373–405,
ch15:143–157, ch16:7–57, and ch16:569–615.

**Length:** 24 pages, twelve spreads. The working allocation was 22 pages. Two pages were added so
Orochimaru's apparent body-transfer victory, Naruto's genjutsu reversal, the physical kill, Kabuto's
survival, and the mask recovery do not collapse into one unreadable resolution.

**Status:** final dialogue and first textual `name`; ready for source comparison and a context-clean
writing review, not for a builder or final art.

## Source effect

Naruto voluntarily gives up perfect surveillance of Danzō because a predictable enemy no longer
tests him, then walks into Orochimaru's hideout believing superior force will make the next problem
simple. The Sound Four make him spend time, chakra, a clone, and the comfort of his left forearm;
Orochimaru then reveals that the conventional fight was only preparation for poison and body theft.
Naruto survives because he reads the true objective before Orochimaru reads Naruto's eyes: he lets
the snake perceive a successful transfer inside a genjutsu, takes that perceived inner world away,
and kills him in the physical room. The victory secures the Shinigami mask and Kusanagi, leaves
Kabuto alive, and leaves Naruto carrying an unrecognized poison exposure into later chapters.

  `N-center` on the dais. Kabuto begins at the dais's `E` stair.
