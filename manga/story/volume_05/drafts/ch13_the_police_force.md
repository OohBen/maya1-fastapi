# Volume 5, Chapter 13 — *The Police Force*

**Source:** ch16:309–683.

**Length:** 22 pages, eleven spreads.

**Status:** final dialogue and first textual `name`; ready for source-effect and context-clean
writing review, not for a builder, lettering pass, effects pass, or final art.

## Source effect

The council tries to turn Naruto and Sasuke's strength into a monitored village asset. Naruto blocks
Root surveillance, but Sasuke uses the same meeting to restore an institution the Uchiha once lost.
The proposal survives because Sasuke makes the Police Force open to non-Uchiha recruits and accepts
Hokage oversight; Naruto supplies leverage, training, intelligence, and money without taking command.
Naruto then forces the advisers to surrender illegal copies of Uchiha scrolls and admits privately
that his public superiority was deliberate: he wanted the elders angry enough to expose themselves
through a drastic move. Iwa's discovery of Kabuto keeps the wider political consequence alive. The
volume ends exactly on Yugao wondering what Karin is to Naruto and deciding to ask him that night;
the dinner and every act of retaliation remain outside the volume.
