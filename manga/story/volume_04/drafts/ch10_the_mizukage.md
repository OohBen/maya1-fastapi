## Chapter engine

Naruto has just broken an army and refuses Mei's offered protection because he wants to measure the Mizukage himself. Yagura initially makes that choice credible: the two fight as shinobi, and the water terrain gives the Mizukage a real tactical edge. Naruto's stored chakra is a finite refill, not proof of endless power; it lets him continue after the army battle and makes the later fireball a deliberate decision rather than an effortless finish.

The reader-facing line is **control → pressure → unresolved escalation**. Naruto remains quiet and analytical. Mei's concern is the emotional counterweight, not a romance beat. The final fireball must feel like it has forced an answer that the chapter withholds.
