# Prologue — After the Barrier

## Purpose and source range

Eight-page bridge from the exact final image of Volume 3 into Volume 4. It adapts fic chapter 7,
lines 291–565, and ends before chapter 8 reveals Hiruzen's death.

This bridge must not repeat the stadium fight, Naruto's public Mangekyo/Susano'o reveal, the start
of the invasion, or the purple barrier forming. Volume 3 already shows those events.

## Continuity in

- Naruto is thirteen, long-haired, black-clad, and has no sword.
- The village is under attack and the purple barrier has just sealed Hiruzen and Orochimaru apart
  from the battlefield.
- Naruto's Mangekyo and Susano'o are already public knowledge.

## Pacing rule

Compress repeated attacks, not choices or consequences. The Gaara rematch is two pages because its
important content is the suppression, exchange about strength, Sasuke's imitation, and Gaara's
first apology—not a blow-by-blow display.

## Manual review gate

- The page order reads continuously from Volume 3's barrier ending into the existing funeral
  aftermath without revealing Hiruzen's death early.
- Gaara's apology, Sasuke's matured Sharingan/imitation, Orochimaru's attempt to take Naruto, and
  Naruto's Shikamaru connection are readable to a first-time reader.
- Naruto never wears orange, never has short spiky hair, and never carries a sword.
- Any visible Mangekyo is the canonical six-bladed pattern.
- No page turns the compressed action into unexplained teleportation or changes who caused an
  outcome.
