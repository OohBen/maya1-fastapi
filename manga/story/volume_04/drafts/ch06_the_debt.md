## Chapter engine

Naruto's help is neither Konoha loyalty nor softness. He pays a precise personal debt to Yugao because she protected him when no institution did. Yugao begins by reading the armour and headband as proof that he is still Konoha's missing teenage shinobi; by the end, she understands that he follows his own ledger and is sailing deliberately toward a civil war to test himself.

The chapter must feel like a decompression after the Oto fight that tightens into unease, not a romance chapter. Yugao is an experienced ANBU and a witness with agency: her fear, questions, and decision to keep travelling are the point. Naruto remains controlled, guarded, and difficult to read.
