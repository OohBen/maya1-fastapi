# Volume 4 continuity audit — source-grounded 11-chapter layout

**Verdict:** the proposed 11-chapter structure is viable only with three boundary corrections: retain Naruto's solo departure, keep the Kiri transition intact, and move the final break to Yagura's Sanbi transformation. The current repository overview remains inaccurate where it says Naruto leaves with Jiraiya and calls Mei the Mizukage; the source has Naruto leave alone and Mei lead the rebellion against the still-reigning Yondaime Mizukage, Yagura. ## Page-level review gates

Before accepting a page, verify the following against its chapter's planned state.

1. **Chronology:** the caption and visual state match the timeline above; no character uses knowledge they only gain later.
2. **Motivation:** Naruto's Oto raid has the Karin/mask objective, Kiri help is a strength test, and Yugao's presence is the repayment debt—not generic Konoha loyalty.
3. **Silhouette:** use N13 only through ch08; use the long-hair/red-armour/sword/gunbai design after the 2y8m card; remove armour only for the noted camp rest/hill scenes.
4. **Cost:** carry Amaterasu eye strain, Oto exhaustion, and final Sanbi depletion forward visibly. Do not portray every major technique as free.
5. **Knowledge:** Kurama's cooperation is shown but unexplained; Mei is rebel leader; the council's false Jiraiya-training story remains false but intact.
6. **End beat:** check the source endpoint before packaging each chapter. In particular, ch10 ends on Yagura being engulfed, while ch11 begins with Sanbi emergence—not after it.
