## Chapter engine

Naruto begins with a controlled answer to Yagura's escalation, then uses every available layer of his arsenal until the final layer fails to leave him standing. The chapter progresses through three visibly distinct opponent states—crimson chakra cloak, restrained human Yagura, full Three-Tails—so the reader sees why Naruto keeps escalating. His win is immediate, not clean: the first bijuudama makes his defensive limit visible, the second forces his last counter, and he ends depleted before the unexplained column appears.

The reader must be able to follow one causal chain: **crimson cloak → complete Susano'o exchange → clone stakes and Wood Serpent remove the cloak → full Three-Tails → first bijuudama / Wood Locking Wall → drained Naruto → second bijuudama charge / final Susano'o bomb → Yagura down → blue column without explanation.** Do not stage the final column as Naruto's technique, an attack, a victory effect, or a resolved transformation.
