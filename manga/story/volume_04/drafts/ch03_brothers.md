# Vol 4, Ch 3 — "Brothers"

**16 pages** · fic ch8 · source boundary: the Akatsuki scouting party outside Konoha through the `Two weeks later` card, immediately before Tsunade's return.
**Source:** `manga/.source/uchiha-naruto-the-sage/ch08.txt:203-267`
**Cast:** Naruto (13), Itachi, Kisame (briefly, no face-to-face scene).
**Locations:** Konoha outskirts; deserted Uchiha compound; Naka Shrine.

## Chapter engine

Naruto enters a private Uchiha inheritance and is immediately tested by its most dangerous surviving witness. The confrontation is not a fight: each man proves that the other knows too much, then defers the collision. Naruto leaves with a concrete conclusion — the Akatsuki are already surveying jinchūriki, so he must train outside Konoha. This is the Volume 4 turn from village aftermath to a wider threat.

## Source facts that cannot move

- Itachi and Kisame have already infiltrated Konoha to gather information about Naruto and the Nine-Tails; Itachi separates from Kisame to confirm the Uchiha/Mangekyō reports. `ch08.txt:203-213`
- Naruto visits the still-intact but deserted Uchiha compound, then enters Naka Shrine using Sharingan and the shrine pass technique. `ch08.txt:215-225`
- Naruto can read only what his Mangekyō permits from the tablet; the remaining material requires Rinnegan, although Madara had already taught him what he can use. `ch08.txt:227-229`
- Itachi's entry and Naruto's access to the shrine confirm Naruto's Uchiha status. Both activate Mangekyō; neither attacks. `ch08.txt:231-239`
- Itachi says Akatsuki are collecting information on jinchūriki, not yet capturing them. Naruto demonstrates knowledge of the organisation, Kisame, and four living Uchiha. `ch08.txt:241-253`
- Naruto says revealing the massacre truth to Sasuke would require killing Sasuke; Itachi then says their next meeting will be a battle and leaves. `ch08.txt:255-261`
- Alone, Naruto resolves to leave Konoha for years of unhindered training at Madara's hideout. The source cuts to `Two weeks later`; do not show Tsunade's return in this chapter. `ch08.txt:263-267`
