## Chapter engine

Naruto enters the field to test techniques, not to save Kiri. Each successful test gives the opposing force a little more information and forces it to organize; when it finally does, Naruto treats that adaptation as permission to reveal a larger answer. The reader's path is deliberately simple: **one fighter walks toward an army → the army breaks → the army learns → the fighter is still beyond its scale**.

The chapter must keep the moral temperature cold. Naruto is satisfied with experimental results, not gleeful. Mei, Yugao, Ao, rebels, and Yagura's soldiers provide the human scale: worry, calculation, revulsion, then fear. Stage casualties through silhouettes thrown by shockwaves, dropped weapons, smoke, empty gaps in ranks, and stunned survivors. Do not show torn bodies, burning flesh, or close-up wounds.

## Title, hook, and chorus

The page-1 title plate may read **WHAT ARE YOU?**, but it is only a chapter label. The answer to whom it belongs arrives on page 24: it is the shared question of terrified rebels and surviving opposing soldiers, sourced to `ch11.txt:375`. Naruto does not think, say, narrate, or receive the line. Do not put it in a Naruto balloon and do not add a balloon tail.

The final image is not the next antagonist. It is Naruto powering down in the crater while witnesses reinterpret him. Yagura's first action and first dialogue are outside this chapter and must remain withheld for the next handoff.
