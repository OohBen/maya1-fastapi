## Verified

The eleven briefs form a chronological story with a readable emotional progression: bereavement and political pressure → independent training → public re-emergence → a personal debt → Kiri’s civil war → the cost of treating people as a test. The strongest reader promise is that Naruto’s power becomes less reassuring as more people are forced to witness it. Volume 3 ends with the invasion still underway, while Chapter 1 correctly opens after its consequences rather than replaying the battle (`manga/story/volume_03/VOLUME_03.md:42-46`; `ch01_the_professor.md:9-15`).

The corrected briefs total **207 pages**, not the prior eight-chapter/~110-page plan (`ch01_the_professor.md:3`; `ch02_not_cut_out_for_it.md:3`; `ch03_brothers.md:3`; `ch04_two_weeks.md:14`; `ch05_orange.md:5`; `ch06_the_debt.md:3`; `ch07_kiri.md:5`; `ch08_mei.md:3`; `ch09_what_are_you.md:3`; `ch10_the_mizukage.md:3`; `ch11_the_three_tails.md:3`). The production decision is one 11-chapter Volume 4; the revised manifest and page budget are recorded in `../VOLUME_04.md`.

## Required volume-level gates

1. **Keep the resolved 11-chapter/207-page scope during generation.** The old eight-chapter/~110-page sketch is superseded by the manifest in `../VOLUME_04.md`; do not silently treat three chapters as overflow.
2. **Lock one age/state vocabulary.** Naruto is thirteen through Chapter 4, then an older teen after the two-years-eight-months card; do not call him an adult in Chapter 10 while surrounding briefs call him sixteen (`ch05_orange.md:7-10`; `ch08_mei.md:5`; `ch10_the_mizukage.md:5`).
3. **Retitle Chapter 8 before any title art.** A repeated *What Are You?* weakens the later chorus and makes the first title look like an answer promised too early (`ch08_mei.md:1`; `ch09_what_are_you.md:1,85-89`).
4. **Use a chapter-end reader test after lettering.** For each exported PDF, a reviewer should answer without notes: where/when are we; what does Naruto want; what changed; who now has a different understanding of him; and what unresolved image pulls to the next chapter. This extends the existing chapter-level checks rather than substituting for them (`ch02_not_cut_out_for_it.md:173-188`; `ch05_orange.md:217-229`; `ch11_the_three_tails.md:71-82`).
5. **Keep the Kiri middle as moral escalation, not romance.** Yugao’s concern and Mei’s authority must continue to counter Naruto’s self-testing; the relevant Chapter 8 material stays non-sexual and non-romantic on page (`ch06_the_debt.md:21-25`; `ch07_kiri.md:79-101`; `ch08_mei.md:15,50-57`).
