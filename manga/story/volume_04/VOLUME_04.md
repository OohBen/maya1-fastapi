# Volume 4 — *What Are You?*

**Covers:** fic ch8 → ch11 (the invasion's aftermath through the Kiri arc).
**Planned 8 chapters · ~110 pages.** Not yet generated — see "Budget" below.

**Arc:** the last person with a soft spot for him is dead, and nothing is holding him any more.

Volume 3 broke his certainty that he was the strongest thing in the room. Volume 4 removes the
other constraint: the Sandaime, who was manipulative but fond of him and therefore exploitable.
Naruto does not attend the funeral. What follows is him testing, deliberately and coldly, how
far he can now go — first out of the village with Jiraiya, then into Kiri to pick a fight with
strangers to see what he is.

## The spine

Volume 1: *power bought with loss.* Volume 2: *every chapter costs him privacy.*
Volume 3: *every chapter costs him a story he told about himself.*
Volume 4: **every chapter he takes something that was previously being withheld from him.**

## Continuity carried in

- **No ninjato.** Lost to Orochimaru in V3 ch2 and never recovered. Bind `N13`, not `N13S`,
  unless and until the fic replaces it.
- **Both eyes.** He revealed the Mangekyo publicly in V3 ch8 in front of a full stadium and two
  kage. He cannot un-reveal it; the village's posture toward him should show that from page one.
- **Susano'o is orange** — the author changed its colour explicitly in ch9 because his chakra is
  orange. Volume 3 already drew it orange, so this stays consistent.
- **Zetsu has a spore on Danzo** since V2 ch4. That is a loaded gun and ch8 puts Danzo in play
  for Hokage.
