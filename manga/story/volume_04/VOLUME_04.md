# Volume 4 - *What Are You?*

**Covers:** fic chapters 8-11, from the invasion aftermath through the unresolved blue-chakra column in Kiri.

**Production scope:** 11 manga chapters, approximately 207 pages. This replaces the earlier eight-chapter / ~110-page sketch after a complete source read showed that the Oto raid, Kiri setup, army battle, and Yagura/Sanbi climax could not remain chronological and readable at that compression.

## Volume engine

Hiruzen's death removes Naruto's last useful political buffer. Every chapter then lets him take or expose something that had been withheld: freedom of movement, clan knowledge, a private promise with Kurama, legal cover, the result of his training, an Uzumaki survivor, access to Kiri's civil war, Kurama's public cooperation, an army as a test, a Kage-level opponent, and finally the cost of reaching his limit.

The reader-facing progression is: Naruto's power becomes less reassuring as more people are forced to understand what he is willing to do with it.

## Chronology locks

- Naruto never leaves with Jiraiya. He refuses the trip, departs alone before the permission meeting, and uses a clone to reveal the truth.
- Mei is the Kiri rebel leader. Yagura is still the Fourth Mizukage throughout this volume.
- Naruto is thirteen through Chapter 4. After the explicit two-years-eight-months card he is an older teen, approximately sixteen.
- The post-skip sword is a new, plain sash sword of unspecified origin. It is not the ninjato lost in Volume 3.
- Kurama's future restoration/freedom promise is shown in Chapter 2. His later cooperation is shown but not explained; that explanation belongs to later source material.
- Karin is recovered; the Shinigami mask is not. Orochimaru and Kabuto escape.
- The final blue chakra column is not named or explained in this volume.

## Production order

Each chapter is completed as a separate reader-tested unit:

1. Source and continuity preflight.
2. Final dialogue and page specs.
3. First five pages generated and visually reviewed.
4. Corrections applied before the remaining pages are generated.
5. Every page reviewed in sequence for identity, chronology, dialogue, action causality, and visual continuity.
6. Chapter PDF rendered and read without notes using the questions in `REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md`.
7. Only then proceed to the next chapter.

## Volume-level visual locks

- Use the same printed-colour manga style reference across the volume unless a page is a deliberate memory/register shift.
- Early Naruto uses the existing black outfit and carries no sword. Post-skip Naruto uses the same face/hair across armour, sword, and black-under-suit references.
- Orange Susano'o has a partial ribcage state and a complete horned, two-bladed state. Neither is a glow; the scene remains legible through and around it.
- Transformations remain distinct: human Yagura -> crimson three-tail cloak -> full Three-Tails turtle.
- Major techniques show a visible cost. Carry Amaterasu eye strain, Oto shoulder damage/exhaustion, stored-chakra release, and final Sanbi depletion forward.
