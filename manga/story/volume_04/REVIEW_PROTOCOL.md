# Volume 4 chapter review protocol
This gate applies to every chapter before the next chapter begins. A chapter is not complete because all PNGs exist; it is complete when the sequential read works without the source notes.

## 1. Source preflight

- Confirm the chapter's exact source range and its first and last adapted beats.
- Read the previous chapter's last two pages and the next source paragraph before finalizing the opener.
- List the knowledge, outfit, weapon, eye, injury, chakra, and relationship state carried in.
- Check that no later explanation, alliance, technique, or political title has moved earlier.
- Finalize all dialogue before generating art.

## 2. Page-spec preflight

- Every named visible character or creature has a numbered binding and a matching ordered reference path.
- Every location needed for orientation has a location reference; close-up panels may use flat/blank backgrounds deliberately.
- The complete visible cast is constrained. Off-panel speakers are marked as off-panel.
- Each page has one dominant panel, uneven geometry, and a deliberate reading order.
- Action pages state the failed tactic that causes the next escalation.
- Time jumps use explicit cards. Cards and signs contain only supplied wording; incidental writing is illegible scribble.
- Dialogue balloons name the speaker and position. Model lettering is the default; deterministic lettering is the fallback for a page whose text alone fails.

## 3. Five-page pilot gate

Generate the first five pages, then inspect them at full size and as thumbnails.

Reject and regenerate a page for:

- wrong face, hair, age, named-character identity, creature form, or Naruto state;
- missing/extra panels that break the scripted beat;
- dialogue misspelling, wrong speaker, invented text, or obscured reading order;
- equal-strip layouts, lineup staging, painterly/3D style drift, soft-glow effects, or unreadable gutters;
- chronology or motivation that cannot be understood from the page itself;
- a technique result shown before its cause.

Minor costume seams, footwear drift, or background ornament may pass only when identity, action, and continuity remain unambiguous at reading size.

## 4. Full chapter page review

For each page, record pass/revise and check:

| Area | Required question |
|---|---|
| Story | What changed on this page, and does it follow from the previous page? |
| Cast | Are the right people present, correctly bound, and acting from what they currently know? |
| Dialogue | Is every line exact, legible, attributed correctly, and necessary? |
| Staging | Can the eye follow the intended panel order and locate the dominant beat? |
| Continuity | Do outfit, weapon, eyes, injuries, chakra, weather, location, and time match adjacent pages? |
| Effects | Is the environment still legible; does the effect use hard graphic shapes rather than glow? |
| Emotion | Does framing/posture carry the emotional beat without relying on a source caption? |

Review all pages once individually, then again in uninterrupted order. A page that looks good alone but breaks the read must be revised.

## 5. Chapter PDF reader test

Render the chapter PDF and contact sheet, then answer without consulting the brief:

1. Where and when are we?
2. What does Naruto want in this chapter?
3. What causes each major decision or escalation?
4. Who understands Naruto differently by the end?
5. What changed in the physical and political state?
6. What unresolved image or question pulls the reader to the next chapter?

If any answer is unclear, identify the first page where comprehension failed, fix that page or the transition before it, rebuild the PDF, and repeat the test.

## 6. Volume continuity gate

Before packaging the full volume:

- Read all chapter-ending and chapter-opening pairs in sequence.
- Check the exact time cards: two weeks; three days; two years eight months; one week; another week; two nights; two days; three nights; one week; three days.
- Confirm Naruto leaves alone, Mei is never called Mizukage, the new sword is never treated as recovered, and the mask remains missing.
- Confirm the title `What Are You?` appears only on Chapter 9 and belongs to the observers' shared question.
- Confirm Chapter 10 ends before the Sanbi form and Chapter 11 starts with that escalation.
- Confirm the blue column remains unresolved on the final page.

## 7. Independent cold-reader / same-end-effect gate

After the volume PDF is assembled, assign a context-clean reviewer who did not produce the pages.
The producing agent's summaries, ledgers, and pass labels are not evidence for this gate.

The reviewer must:

- read the full adapted source range plus at least two complete source chapters before and after it;
- read every builder, caption, balloon, and chapter boundary in the volume;
- inspect every generated page image, using contact sheets only to navigate and opening the
  original full-size page whenever text, identity, chronology, action, or causality is ambiguous;
- read every chapter-ending/chapter-opening pair and the assembled PDF in order;
- compare source and manga by end effect rather than sentence count or identical pacing.

Record a chapter-by-chapter pass/revise result against this table:

| Evidence | Required same-end-effect check |
|---|---|
| Setup | The reader receives the source facts and motivation needed for the event. |
| Result | The event leaves the same physical, political, and emotional outcome. |
| Decision | The same character makes the consequential choice for the same understood reason. |
| Relationships and knowledge | Trust, hostility, promises, discoveries, and withheld information move to the same state. |
| Physical continuity | Injuries, eyes, chakra, outfits, weapons, techniques, locations, and elapsed time carry forward. |
| Future consequence | Any payoff needed by a later source chapter remains established, even if the intermediate action was compressed. |

A volume fails this gate when it is visually readable but changes or omits a material outcome. Fix
the first page or transition where the effect diverges, rebuild the affected chapter and both
volume PDFs, then repeat the independent read.
